## ProtoCosmo2 omega-iter recovery

- **Observed:** ProtoCosmo2's Telegram runner remained alive while both its owning supervisor and `iter.py` worker were absent. Two requests had accumulated in the Iter inbox after the last completed work on Sep 6 morning. The runner was orphaned under PID 1.
- **Inferred defect:** the supervisor waited only on the Telegram runner, so an Iter-worker exit could leave transport apparently healthy while reasoning stopped; it also lacked an independent Iter restart/readiness check.
- **Recovery result:** preserved both queued requests, replaced the orphaned topology with one owner supervising exactly one `iter.py` and one Telegram runner in the same process group, and observed the backlog fully drain: inbox/processing returned to zero and completed increased from 66 to 68. A minimal lifecycle patch was applied during the repair; its exact committed/tested provenance still needs confirmation in the project record.
