\section*{Executive summary}

This was a small, local, three-seed calibration of typed CMCP evidence
accounting inside a predictive-coding (ePC) distillation objective. It was
designed to answer a narrow question: can CMCP prevent a burst of
duplicate/derived teacher messages from being counted as four independent
pieces of evidence, while retaining a deliberately independent teacher view?

\textbf{Observed.} CMCP did the duplicate-protection part cleanly: its mean
effective precision was $1.354$ instead of naive accumulation's $4.0$. It
also improved final Task-B loss relative to naive accumulation in all three
paired seeds (mean difference $-0.0190$ nats). However, CMCP gave the
constructed independent view only about $0.354$ additional credit, whereas
the constructed oracle credited it fully and therefore had total precision
$2.0$. CMCP was worse than ordinary one-view KD/ePC on Task-B loss in all
three seeds (mean $+0.00341$ nats), while its Task-A retention was only
slightly better (mean $-0.00144$ nats).

\textbf{Conclusion.} The run supports CMCP as a guard against duplicate
precision inflation. It does not support a claim that the present response
score or raw weighting improves transformer-to-ePC distillation. The most
important issue is a confound: CMCP changes both \emph{which} packet is
trusted and the \emph{total KD objective mass}. The next test should hold the
total non-duplicate KD mass fixed while comparing novelty estimators.

\section*{Question and scope}

Under equal optimizer-update and teacher-call budgets, does typed CMCP
accounting prevent duplicate/derived teacher packets from inflating credit,
retain an independent view, and improve calibration or sequential-curriculum
retention for an ePC student?

This is a synthetic, reduced calibration---not a transformer-scale result and
not a confirmation experiment. It uses a two-layer causal character
transformer with vocabulary 12, width 16, two attention heads, feed-forward
width 32, sequence length 16, and dropout 0. The teacher and student are
different models with a shared fixed architecture. The teacher is trained for
32 AdamW updates on the forward synthetic sequence task and then frozen. Each
student starts from the same copied initialization within a seed.

\section*{Exact protocol}

\subsection*{Curriculum and budgets}

Each seed runs 160 AdamW student updates: 80 Task-A updates followed by 80
Task-B updates. A batch contains 6 sequences; evaluations use 6 batches of 8
sequences. Task-A is a stochastic forward modular-increment sequence task.
Task-B uses another generated stream but its targets are shifted by half the
vocabulary: $y_B=(y+6)\bmod 12$. Thus Task B contradicts the Task-A target
mapping rather than merely continuing it.

The seeds are 101, 211, and 307. Every arm receives exactly 160 optimizer
updates, 160 teacher forward calls, and 640 packet presentations (four per
update). The ePC distillation objective uses two settlement steps,
temperature 2.0, relaxation learning rate 0.1, and the overall objective

\[
  L = 0.8 L_{CE}(y, f_\theta(x)) + 0.02 \sum_p w_p L_{ePC}(f_\theta(x),z_p).
\]

Gradients are clipped at norm 1.0. All recorded ePC energy traces were
monotone in every arm and seed.

\subsection*{Teacher packet burst}

For each student batch, the frozen teacher emits base logits $z$. Four typed
packets, all for the same cohort/batch, are presented:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lYYY}
\toprule
Packet & Payload & Typed innovation/provenance relation & Constructed oracle credit \\
\midrule
Base & $z$ & canonical innovation; teacher/base & 1 \\
Exact duplicate & $z$ copied & same innovation as base; teacher/cache-copy & 0 \\
Descendant & $z/2$ & same innovation as base; teacher/temp-transform & 0 \\
Seeded view & $z+0.35\epsilon$ & distinct innovation; teacher/seeded-view & 1 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

Here $\epsilon$ is a deterministic seed-and-update-specific Gaussian tensor.
The oracle designation is a fixture assumption: base and seeded view are
counted as two independent pieces of evidence even though the latter is an
additive perturbation of the former. That assumption is useful for diagnosing
under-credit, but it is not a claim that noisy logits are generally
independent teacher knowledge.

\subsection*{Arms and exact weights}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{lYYY}
\toprule
Arm & Packet weights in burst order & Total effective precision & Purpose \\
\midrule
Ordinary & $(1,0,0,0)$ & 1 & one canonical teacher target \\
Naive & $(1,1,1,1)$ & 4 & unprotected multi-view accumulation \\
Oracle & $(1,0,0,1)$ & 2 & fixture-known deduplication target \\
CMCP & $(1,0,0,r)$ & $1+r$ & typed guards plus estimated novelty \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

CMCP first rejects later packets with an already seen innovation identity.
For a new innovation, it converts logits to a flattened standardized response
score: softmax, flatten batch and position, subtract each vocabulary-column
mean, divide by its population standard deviation (floor $10^{-5}$). It then
uses the existing ridge-regularized conditional-information-ratio routine
against stored canonical scores, clamps the ratio to $[0,1]$, and assigns that
ratio as the packet weight. Hence base has weight 1 and the seeded view has
weight $r$; duplicate and descendant have weight 0 by typed identity.

\section*{Measurements}

Task-A retention is the final Task-A loss minus the Task-A loss immediately
after update 80; lower is better. Task-B metrics are evaluated after update
160. Expected calibration error (ECE) uses ten fixed bins $[0,0.1),\ldots,
[0.9,1.0)$. Effective precision is the update-average sum of packet weights.

\section*{Interpretation and alternative explanations}

\textbf{What is directly supported.} Typed innovation identities prevent both
the byte-identical packet and the declared descendant from being counted
again. This reduces the pathological four-fold KD accumulation. CMCP's lower
Task-B loss versus naive is consistent with avoiding that overcount.

\textbf{What is not supported.} Neither the response-score novelty estimator
nor raw CMCP weighting demonstrated a benefit over ordinary one-view KD/ePC.
The oracle did not dominate ordinary either, so the fixture is not yet a
validated setting in which ``more genuinely independent teacher evidence''
should improve this student outcome.

\textbf{Primary confound.} Raw CMCP has total KD weight about 1.354, while
the oracle has 2 and naive has 4. It therefore jointly changes novelty
selection and global distillation strength. Its result can arise from less KD,
from better selection, or their interaction. The CE term is unchanged, but the
relative CE/KD balance is not.

\textbf{Why the independent view was underweighted.} The view is defined as
$z+0.35\epsilon$, so its score remains strongly conditionally predictable
from $z$. The current estimator measures residual response variation, not the
fixture's declared causal independence. This may be exactly the desired answer
for a noisy re-expression of one teacher, but disagrees with this fixture's
oracle label. It should be resolved by a testable definition of independent
teacher information, not by simply increasing the weight.

\textbf{Other limitations.} The descendant $z/2$ is declared redundant by
identity without testing whether temperature-scaled logits have a distinct
optimization effect. The same frozen teacher generates base and seeded view;
there is no independent teacher checkpoint, data shard, augmentation pipeline,
or causal intervention. Task B's supervised labels contradict the forward
teacher's task, so it is deliberately a stress test of plasticity, not a
natural multi-teacher distillation problem. Accuracy is close to the 1/12
chance regime; loss and ECE are more informative here, but also small and
variable.

\section*{Weighting improvements worth testing}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Factor identity, novelty, and total mass.} Keep hard typed
deduplication as a zero/one guard. Separately estimate novelty only for
distinct innovations, then normalize the accepted weights to a predeclared
total KD mass (for example 2 in this fixture). Compare raw CMCP, normalized
CMCP, and an equal-mass ordinary control. This makes the causal question
``which evidence'' rather than ``how much KD.''

\item \textbf{Estimate incremental \emph{training signal}, not raw-logit
residual.} For each candidate packet, residualize its distillation gradient or
ePC parameter-credit vector against the accepted packets and weight its
positive residual norm. A temperature transform or noisy logit view can be
redundant in probability space but non-redundant in parameter-update space,
which is the quantity relevant to assimilation.

\item \textbf{Calibrate novelty on a fixture family with known fractions.}
Vary the independent-view mixture continuously, e.g. $z'=\sqrt{1-\rho^2}z+
\rho u$ for a separately trained teacher $u$, and test whether estimated
weight tracks the known incremental source fraction. Include exact copies,
temperature transforms, paraphrase/augmentation views, independent
checkpoints, and adversarially correlated views. Select the estimator before
running a student outcome test.

\item \textbf{Treat descendant semantics explicitly.} Keep a provenance
rule for deterministic cache copies, but make temperature, truncation, and
augmentation descendants eligible for a conditional incremental test. Compare
``always zero descendants'' with ``gradient-novel descendants'' under equal
total KD mass.

\item \textbf{Use a gated assimilation/maintenance split.} A packet with
low novelty need not be discarded: send it to replay/maintenance credit while
using only a novelty-normalized fraction for current parameter assimilation.
Evaluate both separately; otherwise repeated packets can look useless even if
they stabilize a learned representation.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Recommended next experiment}

Before any large transformer run, use a 5--10 seed synthetic calibration with
frozen primary metrics: (i) calibration error of estimated incremental credit
against a known mixture fraction; (ii) paired final Task-B loss; (iii) Task-A
retention; and (iv) energy monotonicity. Hold optimizer updates, teacher calls,
and total KD mass constant. Compare ordinary-one-view, naive, oracle,
raw-CMCP, mass-normalized CMCP-response, and mass-normalized
CMCP-gradient-novelty. Promote to a real teacher/student only if the estimator
first tracks known incremental credit and then improves a preregistered paired
student metric over equal-mass ordinary KD/ePC.
