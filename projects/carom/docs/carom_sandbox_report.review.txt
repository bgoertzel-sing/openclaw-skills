        Sandbox Report: A CPU-Scale Prototyping Ladder for
           Scheduled, Fixed-Point, and Itinerant CAROM
    Working note — companion to “From Scheduled Execution to Itinerant Dynamics”

                                                July 2026

                                                  Abstract
          We report a CPU-only sandbox implementation and dry run of the CAROM prototyping
      ladder: one shared command-routed operator library executed under three semantics (ex-
      ternally scheduled, deep-equilibrium fixed point, and itinerant generalized Lotka–Volterra
      control). The purpose was engineering validation — flush out bugs, mistuned dynamics,
      and silent failure modes before committing GPU budget — not scientific measurement. The
      run succeeded on those terms: all three variants train stably; the itinerant control system
      produces and, critically, preserves under gradient training perfectly sequential heteroclinic
      itineraries (256/256); and three substantive findings emerged that reshape the experimen-
      tal design: (i) a routing-bottleneck expressivity constraint tying the operator count K to
      the primitive instruction set and thereby to command-vocabulary design; (ii) a silent deep-
      equilibrium pathology in which the “fixed-point” model trains into a non-convergent unrolled
      network unless a contraction penalty is included — with a one-line fix verified to reverse the
      residual profile; and (iii) a package of six tuning rules without which the itinerant dynamics
      either seize, die, or start mid-channel. We record the exact configuration, quantitative results
      per rung, what the sandbox does and does not establish, and the questions deliberately left
      to GPU-scale runs.


1     Purpose and scope
The sandbox run had three goals, in priority order:
 (1) De-risk the code path. Verify that every component — task generator, shared operator
     core, the three execution wrappers, training harness — runs, learns, and checkpoints, so
     that a subsequent Colab run spends GPU time on science rather than debugging.
 (2) De-risk the dynamics. The itinerant variant couples a learned neural field to a gen-
     eralized Lotka–Volterra (GLV) competition; whether this coupled system is trainable by
     backpropagation at all — gradients flowing through ∼70 integration steps without insta-
     bility, and the heteroclinic channel surviving parameter updates — was the largest a priori
     risk in the entire proposal.
 (3) Discover unknown unknowns. Toy-scale contact with the architecture tends to surface
     constraints invisible on paper. It did.
    Explicitly out of scope: converged accuracies, variant comparisons at matched compute,
statistical claims (all runs are single-seed), and the free-routing order-discovery question, which
is a research problem rather than an engineering one. Where the sandbox touches these, results
are reported as trends only.

2     Setup
2.1   Compute environment
CPU-only Linux container, PyTorch 2.13, no GPU. Two consequences shaped the methodology:
(a) model width, batch size, and step counts were cut well below the intended Colab scale (d=48
vs. 64; hundreds-to-thousands of steps vs. 4–8k); (b) the container reaps background processes
between tool invocations, so all training ran in foreground time-budgeted chunks with check-
point/resume (model, optimizer, LR schedule, and both RNG states serialized). The resume
machinery, built as a workaround, is retained in the deliverable because it makes the Colab
notebook robust to session timeouts at zero cost.

2.2   Task: composition of sequence transforms
Workspace of n = 6 slots over Z8 . A program is a sequence of up to Lmax = 5 command tokens
drawn from a 10-token vocabulary: seven primitive whole-sequence transforms

inc (x+1), dbl (2x), neg (−x), rev, shr (rotate right), swp (swap slots 0, 1), cms (prefix sums),

all mod 8; two synonym tokens semantically identical to inc and rev (to exercise routing reuse);
and noop. Programs of effective length L ∈ {2, . . . , 5} are padded with trailing noops, so tensors
are fixed-size while critical-path depth varies per sample; depth = effective length. Supervision:
per-slot cross-entropy on the final state; chance accuracy = 1/8 = 0.125. The generator self-
verifies by replaying every sampled program symbolically.
    This task is the second design; the first, and its failure, is a principal finding (Section 3.2).

2.3   Shared operator core
K = 8 operators, d = 48, single attention head, all K batched along a leading axis (one einsum
per stage, no Python loop). Each operator: content-addressed attention with queries/keys/values
over [LN(w); e; p] (mutable content, immutable evidence, fixed address); a two-layer GELU
transform; a sigmoid gate (bias-initialized near 0, so gates open at ∼0.5); update proposal
u = g ⊙ tanh(z). Routing is an embedding A ∈ R10×8 softmaxed per command. Readout:
LayerNorm–MLP per slot. Identical core instantiated once and shared by all three variants —
the controlled quantity of the ladder.

2.5   Protocol
AdamW, one-cycle LR (2 · 10−3 peak), gradient clipping at 1.0, batch sizes 128 (scheduled), 96
(fixed-point), 48 (itinerant), sized to per-step wall-clock. Evaluation on fresh 256–512-sample
batches (the task distribution is procedurally infinite; there is no train/test split issue at these
exposure levels, though held-out-composition splits are a Colab matter). Single seed throughout.


3     Results
3.1   Rung 1: scheduled CAROM
Steady, monotone learning to ≈ 70% per-slot accuracy at ∼3.9k steps (chance 12.5%), no
instabilities, still improving at budget exhaustion. Establishes that the shared core, task, and
harness are sound, and provides the reference trajectory for the other rungs’ early dynamics.

3.2   Finding 1: the routing-bottleneck constraint
The first task design was an SSA-style register program: commands of the form slot5 =
add(slot2, slot3), with source/destination addresses baked into the token — 210 distinct
commands. Scheduled CAROM stalled near chance (0.24 at 600 steps, trajectory flattening).
The cause is structural, not a bug. Command-only routing means each command’s semantics
is a point in the convex hull of K command-blind operator functions: sharp routing realizes at
most K distinct behaviors (the simplex vertices); soft routing realizes blends, and a blend of
“write to slot 3” and “write to slot 5” is not a program step but a smear. Because the operators
cannot see the command, they cannot disambiguate; the realizable instruction set has cardinality
≈ K. Slot-addressed instruction formats multiply the primitive count by the number of address
combinations and thus demand infeasibly large K.
    Design rule extracted: keep command vocabularies small and behavior-typed; let addressing be
content-based (the workspace’s job), not command-encoded. The replacement task (Section 2.2)
follows this rule — and, notably, matches the original CAROM’s reported sweet spot (“compos-
able functions over sequences”), suggesting its designer’s task choice was implicitly load-bearing.
This constraint belongs in the theory note as an expressivity bound on command-only routing.

3.3   Rung 3: fixed-point CAROM, and Finding 2 (the silent DEQ pathology)
Trained to ∼960 of 1400 steps. Two results.

Depth-graded accuracy. At the ∼830-step checkpoint, per-depth accuracy was

                               depth   2     3     4     5
                               acc   0.438 0.325 0.294 0.194

— shallow compositions learned first, the expected curriculum-like structure and the raw mate-
rial for the sweeps-vs-depth analysis at convergence.

The pathology. At the same checkpoint the per-sweep relative residual rose monotonically,
0.116 → 0.181 over 8 sweeps: the model was not converging to any equilibrium. It had learned
to exploit the sweep loop as a weight-tied unrolled network — a known deep-equilibrium failure
under naive BPTT with no contraction pressure, but dangerous here because nothing visible
breaks: the loss falls, accuracy climbs, and the model silently ceases to be the thing the exper-
iment is about (equilibrium semantics, adaptive compute, residual-as-confidence all presuppose
convergence).

The fix, verified. Adding the final sweep’s mean residual to the loss (weight 1.0) reversed the
profile within ≈ 130 steps: residuals 0.080 → 0.0845 → 0.080 (rise-then-decay, final ≤ initial)
with accuracy unharmed (0.31, consistent with the pre-fix trajectory). Protocol rule extracted:
the residual-per-sweep plot is the only observable that identifies which model was actually trained;
it is mandatory, not diagnostic garnish.

3.4   Finding 3: making the GLV channel run (six tuning rules)
The itinerant control dynamics required an iterated tune before training was attempted; each
failure below was observed, diagnosed, and fixed, with the untrained hand-built channel as the
test article (success = dominant- mode itinerary 0→1→2→3→4).
                                                      P
  (i) Inhibition index convention. With compm = j ρmj aj , ρmj is inhibition felt by m
      from j. The chain asymmetry wired backwards produces a single mode seizing control
      permanently. (Observed: mode 2 dominant for the entire trajectory.)

 (ii) Positive fitness baseline. Without it, competition drives total activity to the floor and
      the control state dies. (Observed: all am → floor by mid-trajectory.) Winner equilibrium
      a∗ ≈ b/ρmm ; b = 3.0, self-inhibition 1.0.

(iii) Channel entry by initialization. At uniform initialization the chain structure itself
      disfavors mode 0: every successor enjoys a weak- inhibition discount from its predecessor,
      while mode 0 receives no discount and absorbs the full successor-kills-predecessor term
      from mode 1. Middle modes win. (Observed: best sweep configuration achieved only 2/16
      correct itineraries from uniform starts.) Fix: a0 = 1.0, others at floor.

 (iv) Activation floor sets the clock. Saddle passage time scales as ln(1/floor)/λ; a floor of
      10−4 makes invasions consume the whole trajectory. Floor 0.05, dt = 0.2, S = 70 yields
      ≈ 12 steps per phase — five phases fit.

3.5    Rung 4: itinerant CAROM, hand-built chain, trained
With the channel frozen and everything else (operators, routing, fitness MLP, biases, scales)
trained by full BPTT through all 70 integration steps, ∼440 steps at batch 48 gave: accuracy
above chance and depth-graded (0.286/0.221/0.217/0.202 for depths 2–5; 0.189 on all-depth-5
evaluation), loss decreasing throughout, and — the central result of the sandbox —

    256/256 evaluation trajectories retained perfectly sequential itineraries after
                                      training.

Gradient flow through the coupled GLV–workspace system is stable at these settings (no clipping
saturation, no divergence), and parameter updates do not corrode the heteroclinic channel. This
was the largest a priori risk in the itinerant proposal, and it is retired at toy scale.

3.6    Rung 5 (abbreviated): free ρ
With ρ learnable from a near-symmetric initialization and only 124 training steps (the honest
limit of the CPU budget), the system showed: accuracy 0.171; asymmetry ∥ρ − ρ⊤ ∥F = 0.72
(vs. ≈ 15 for the hand-built chain); mean phases visited 1.0 (winner-take-all, no handoffs);
itinerary–order Kendall τ = 0.0. Order discovery did not emerge in this budget — as expected,
and reported as a null trend, not a negative result. Its value is confirmatory: the rung structure
correctly isolates order-execution (rung 4, now established) from order-discovery (rung 5, the
open research question).


4     What was established, what was suggested, what remains
4.1    Established (at toy scale, single seed)
     The full pipeline — generator (self-verifying), shared core, three wrappers, chunked train-
      ing with exact resume — runs and learns.

     Scheduled CAROM learns the composition task strongly (70%, climbing).

     The fixed-point wrapper trains, exhibits depth-graded competence, and — with the con-
      traction penalty — can be held to genuinely convergent behavior.

     The coupled itinerant system is trainable by backpropagation, and the heteroclinic channel
      is robust to training. Sequential program execution as an emergent property of a flow,
      rather than a clocked loop, works mechanically.

     The routing-bottleneck constraint: realizable instruction-set size ≈ K; command vocabu-
      laries must be behavior-typed.



                                                5
4.2    Suggested (trends; require GPU-scale confirmation)
     Depth-graded accuracy in both non-scheduled variants hints that the sweeps/phases align
      with compositional depth as theorized; the quantitative version (residual half-life vs. crit-
      ical path; dwell statistics vs. ln(1/η)/λ) awaits converged models.

     Early-training trajectories of all three variants are roughly comparable, weakly suggesting
      no variant pays a large optimization tax at matched (small) compute.

     The one-line contraction penalty appears sufficient (not merely necessary) at this scale;
      whether it holds at larger d, more sweeps, and full convergence is untested.

4.3    Open questions, deliberately deferred
(Q1) Convergence endpoints and gaps. Do all three variants reach comparable asymptotic
     accuracy at matched compute? Does itinerant CAROM match scheduled once trained
     out?

(Q2) Order discovery. Can free ρ learn chain asymmetry, and which of the candidate pres-
     sures (itinerary/entropy regularization requiring ≥ L phase transitions; fatigue annealing;
     program-length curriculum; weakly asymmetric ρ prior) is sufficient? This is the central
     scientific question of the itinerant proposal.

(Q3) Routing interpretability. Do the two synonym tokens converge to the same operator
     mixture as their primitives? (Instrumented in the deliverable; never run to convergence.)

(Q4) Sweeps-vs-depth and dwell-time laws at convergence, as quantitative signatures of
     the claimed semantics.

(Q5) Length/depth generalization (train L ≤ 4, test L = 5 and beyond), untouched in the
     sandbox since it requires dedicated retraining.

(Q6) Variance. Everything above at ≥ 3 seeds.
