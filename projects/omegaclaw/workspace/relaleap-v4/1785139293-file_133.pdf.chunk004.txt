A.6    Stage C5: CAROM track (CPU or ≤ 4 GPU-h)
Purpose. The three CAROM semantics, in increasing order of novelty.
   Numerics. Reuse the CAROM sandbox configuration: operator library K = 8, 16 slots,
program length 6, 4 context families; BPTT-trained scheduled variant as substrate (i); fixed-
point variant as substrate (ii); itinerant/LV arbitration as substrate (iii).
 1. Scheduled: operator-level gating actions (M = K + 1 counting the control system as a
    module); tangent propagator through the unrolled program; gates as in C2 with the same
    margin discipline; 60 decision states × 3 seeds, horizons {1, 5, 10}.
 2. Fixed-point: IFT sensitivities via conjugate gradients (tolerance 10−6 , max 200 iterations,
    warm-started); validation against nudged finite differences (nudge 10−3 , central): relative
    error ≤ 10% at 20 probe equilibria before any effect estimates are reported.
 3. Discrete commutators: 500 paired CRN measurements of d(Ui Uj , Uj Ui ) across the 6 context
    pairs from snapshots along training, d = max over per-context eval-loss differences. Gate:
    Spearman ≥ 0.5 between measured commutator magnitude and realized forgetting when the
    contexts are subsequently trained sequentially (both orders, paired). This is the CAROM-
    native critic claim; if it fails while (1) passes, the discrepancy localizes the non-gradient
    components as the obstruction — report which.

A.7    Stage C6: policy pilot (only if C2 and C4 pass)
The conservative V-series policy, re-expressed on vector outcomes and the validity radius (§5),
evaluated on the C2 test bed first: retention vs ordinary and support-score controls, incoming
loss within 0.02 nat, positive paired bootstrap (104 resamples), superiority to frequency-matched
random, ≥ 50% of oracle improvement (oracle from exhaustive tables), credit-wavefront and
effective-rank diagnostics preserved. New protocol version, fresh seeds, thresholds frozen before
the first confirmation seed is opened — V-series discipline unchanged.

A.8    Frozen choices and allowed knobs
Never change: the estimand (1) and CRN pairing; the gate definitions and thresholds once C1
completes; the validity-radius definition (ρ ≥ 0.8, e ≤ 0.25, margin ≥ 3σ); the effect-size precheck
rule (≥ 5σ, at most 3 strengthening iterations); the behavior-policy mix; float64 for the fixture;
the per-seed reporting requirement; the do-no-harm bound on the residual. May change with
a one-line note: horizon and α grids; residual width/depth within [1, 4] × [32, 128]; learning
rates (halve on divergence, clip 1.0); CG tolerances; footprint dimension D; number of confusion-
graph pairs [3, 10].

A.9    Budgets and global stop conditions
C0–C3 are CPU-only; C4 ≤ 12 GPU-hours; C5 ≤ 4 GPU-hours; hard programme ceiling 3
GPU-days including reruns. Stop and report (partial results are a deliverable) if: C0 or C1
Gate 1 fails; C2 yields no valid cell at h ≤ 5 and the operator directs a halt; cumulative compute
exceeds the ceiling; or any fix would touch a frozen choice.
