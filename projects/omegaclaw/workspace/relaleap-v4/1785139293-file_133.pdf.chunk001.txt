                                                Abstract
       The co-learned global causal critic program (RelaLeap V1–V3) attempted to learn, from
   decision-time summary features, a randomized paired counterfactual estimate of which module-
   level gradient intervention would improve a retention–plasticity tradeoff. V2 failed every dis-
   crimination and calibration gate; V3’s positive controls lost their declared orderings along the
   optimizer trajectory before confirmation could open. This document argues, on the basis of
   the General Causal-Continual-Learning (CCL) theorem, that both failures were predictable
   consequences of the problem formulation rather than of the causal question: (i) the summary
   features used were not sufficient statistics for the estimand, which to second order in the step
   size is an explicit functional of local gradients, curvature, and the trajectory’s linear-response
   propagator; and (ii) the estimand is intrinsically state-dependent, so any trajectory-invariant
   “declared ordering” must eventually fail, and the V3 audit criterion — not the fixture —
   was the defective component. We then propose the repair: a grey-box commutator critic
   whose backbone is measured at decision time (per-module gradient inner products, module-
   restricted Hessian-vector products, and a tangent-propagated linear-response term computed
   by forward-mode automatic differentiation along the common-random-number baseline roll-
   out), with a small learned head restricted to the residual that the second-order theory does
   not capture. Pair synergy becomes an exact cross-curvature identity, resolving the global-
   versus-additive comparator ambiguity; the positive control becomes a block-quadratic fixture
   whose ground truth is recomputed analytically at every state, so orderings cannot “break,”
   only the expansion’s validity radius can shrink — and that radius becomes a measured gate.
   We give the mathematics, the conceptual reframing, a software design that reuses the V3
   paired-restoration machinery, specializations to Transformer head/channel modules (with
   HDC-footprint confusion-graph sparsification of the pair-action space) and to CAROM in
   its scheduled, fixed-point, and itinerant semantics (tangent propagation, implicit-function-
   theorem sensitivities, and measured discrete commutators respectively), and an appendix
   specifying a staged, gated, agent-executable experimental programme with concrete numer-
   ics chosen to preclude the shortsighted choices — unmeasured noise floors, under-powered
   effect sizes, leaky standardization, single-precision fixtures — that would otherwise corrupt
   the results. A toy-scale sandbox implementation (Section 10; a NumPy block-quadratic
   suite with exact closed-form truth, and a JAX MLP-plus-Adam learner with CRN paired
   rollouts) validates every backbone mechanism where truth is exact — including the synergy
   identity, the provably-aligned null, the constructive insufficient-statistics demonstration, and
   the margin-decay reinterpretation of the V3 audit failure — and yields concrete protocol
   amendments, the sharpest being that differentiating through the Adam moment recursions
   (exact-D) is load-bearing rather than a refinement.

1     Introduction and diagnosis
1.1   What V1–V3 tried
The V-series program targets a genuinely causal object. At learner state St , candidate action
a (attenuate one module’s incoming-task gradient, or a pair’s, before the optimizer step), or-
dinary action a0 , and horizon h, the estimand is the common-random-number (CRN) paired
counterfactual effect
                            τh (St , a) = E[ Yt+h (a) − Yt+h (a0 ) | St ] ,                (1)
with Y a vector of outcomes (retained-task loss LA , incoming-task loss LB , and diagnostics),
measured by executing the learner from an exact snapshot under both arms with identical
randomness. This estimand, the randomized behavior policy, the exhaustive action tables, and
the fail-closed gate discipline are all correct and are retained unchanged in what follows.
    What failed was the estimator. V2 asked a three-branch MLP to map summary features —
gradient norms, cosines, support ratios, activation statistics, Adam second moments — to τh ,
and obtained Spearman correlations below 0.065 in every family. V3 enriched the features and
scaled the data tenfold, but stopped earlier: at the final audit checkpoint, every positive-control
family had lost the intervention ordering declared for it at construction time.

1.2   Two failure theorems, informally
Both outcomes are consequences of structure the CCL theorem makes explicit.




                                                  3
Failure 1: the features are not sufficient statistics. To second order in the step size, τh
is an explicit functional of the local gradients gA , gB , the local Hessians HA , HB (through their
module-restricted blocks), the optimizer preconditioner, and the trajectory’s linear-response
propagator (Section 3). Norms, cosines, and moment summaries quotient out precisely the
curvature-weighted inner products on which the estimand depends: two states can agree on
every V2 feature and have τh of opposite sign (Proposition 1). A critic trained on a non-sufficient
statistic converges, at best, to the conditional mean over the induced equivalence class; near-zero
rank correlation and amount-heads inventing their own output scale are exactly the predicted
symptoms.

Failure 2: the estimand has no trajectory-invariant ordering. τh depends on the state
through gA (θt ) and gB (θt ). As incoming-task optimization converges, the perturbation injected
by a gate shrinks proportionally to ∥gB ∥, and the retained-side alignment term can change sign
as the trajectory crosses level sets of LA (Proposition 2 gives the closed form in the quadratic
case, including the geometric decay rate of the action margin). A “declared family ordering” is
therefore an implicit claim of trajectory invariance that the estimand does not possess. The V3
audit did its job — it detected that the criterion, not the physics, was wrong.

1.3   The repair in one sentence
Do not train a critic to rediscover τh from summary features; measure the first- and second-
order terms that the CCL theorem says constitute τh in the expansion-valid regime, and train a
small model only on the residual — with a positive control whose ground truth is recomputed
analytically per state, so that the only thing that can fail is the expansion’s validity radius,
which is itself turned into a measured, reportable quantity.

                                                             5
Why CRN makes this the right object. Because the two arms share minibatches, opti-
mizer state, and RNG streams, the realized paired difference is the response of the (stochastic)
baseline trajectory to the perturbation ut (a) under the same noise realization. The linear re-
sponse along the realized baseline path is therefore the exact first-order value of the paired
estimand, with no additional Monte Carlo variance beyond that of the baseline rollout itself.
The CRN design of V1–V3, built to control variance, turns out to be precisely the design under
which the backbone is computable.

Computation. Equation (9) is one forward-mode tangent pass: initialize tangent v ← ut (a),
and at each baseline step propagate v ← Jk v, which costs one Hessian-vector product (HVP) of
the minibatch loss (a jvp through the gradient computation) plus the elementwise preconditioner
action. For A candidate actions, stack tangents into a d × A matrix and batch the JVPs (vmap
over tangent columns). Total cost: O(A · h) HVPs per decision state, i.e. roughly 2Ah extra
backward-equivalents — for the Phase-0 scales below, tens of milliseconds per state on CPU,
and easily amortized on GPU. Crucially, no learned component participates: the backbone is a
measurement.

3.3   Local commutator form and the CCL connection
When retained-task replay is interleaved with incoming updates (rather than LA merely being
evaluated), the deviation between action arms over a window is governed, per the CCL theorem’s
continuous specialization, by the gradient field commutator
                         [XA , XB ](θ) = HB (θ) gA (θ) − HA (θ) gB (θ),                    (10)
restricted through the module blocks that the gate touches. The propagator form (9) is the
trajectory-exact refinement of the Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff second-order bound in CCL The-
orem 1: where the CCL theorem bounds forgetting by Kij hi hj εcomm uniformly, the backbone
evaluates the corresponding bilinear form on the realized trajectory. Two corollaries worth
