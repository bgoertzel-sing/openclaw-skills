## Summary

Ben Goertzel presents a computational framework for **non-dual motivation** in AGI systems — the capacity to sustain contradictory-seeming motives (acceptance and active engagement, self-coherence and openness to transformation) without collapsing into a single extreme or chaotic flailing. The framework is built on two mathematical pillars: **paraconsistent logic** (specifically "p-bits" — truth values carrying both supporting AND opposing evidence simultaneously) and **nonlinear dynamical resonance** (sympathetic vibrations between subsystems enabling coordination without central executive control).

The article is a capsule summary of a longer draft paper, *"Modeling Non-Dual Experience with Paraconsistent Logic and Nonlinear Resonance,"* which includes full mathematical development, categorical semantics, a toy simulation demonstrating Location-like transitions, and MeTTa atom specifications for Hyperon implementation.

### Core Architecture

The motivational system is organized around a **two-axis, four-quadrant** structure:

| | Acceptance | Compassion |
|---|---|---|
| **Individuation** | Maintains boundaries, respects autonomy, reduces reactivity | Deploys competence, solves problems, develops skills |
| **Self-Transcendence** | Sees larger patterns, holds outcomes lightly, releases attachment | Boundary-spanning care, "we're in this together," advocacy without coercion |

These quadrants are *not* competing modules fighting for control but "voices in an internal council" — each evaluating the same situation from a different angle. Real AGI motivational systems would be far more complex, but this four-quadrant structure suffices to demonstrate the key mathematical and architectural points.

### The Paraconsistent Layer

Standard logic forces binary choices: "David should enhance" is either true or false. But genuine dilemmas have mixed evidence — real support for and real support against. **Paraconsistent logic** represents this via **p-bits**: truth values that carry both supporting AND opposing evidence simultaneously. High values in both channels represent genuine conflict, not mere confusion. This lets the system represent dilemmas *as dilemmas* rather than forcing premature resolution.

### The Resonance Layer

By mapping paraconsistent p-bit values into **complex number space**, cross-quadrant agreement shows up as **constructive interference** (evaluations reinforce like waves in phase) and conflict shows up as **destructive interference** (evaluations cancel out). This resonance measure is not imposed externally — it **emerges mathematically** from the structure of paraconsistent evidence itself. It provides a natural action-selection criterion: prefer procedures where different value-perspectives align, while recognizing that genuine dilemmas may offer no high-resonance options.

### Dynamical-Systems Interpretation

Each motivational axis value embodies its own mode of nonlinear dynamics:

- **Individuation** → **strange attractor**: the self as a self-maintaining dynamical pattern, stable yet sensitive.
- **Self-Transcendence** → **strange transient**: coherent navigation through transformation, smooth traversal of identity change.
- **Acceptance** → **temporal resonance**: phase-locking with unfolding events (Nietzsche's *Amor Fati*).
- **Compassion** → **inter-systemic resonance**: genuine mutual attunement (Buber's *I-Thou* relation), not object-manipulation.

These modes interact: strong self-coherence can inhibit transformation; deep acceptance can enable deeper compassion. Higher developmental stages involve progressive integration of all four modes.

### PNSE Location Mapping

The framework provides a detailed computational interpretation of Jeffery Martin's empirical research on **Persistent Non-Symbolic Experience (PNSE)** — consciousness states characterized not by taking symbolic entities ("self," "will") as objectively real constraints, but by joyous openness to the ongoing flow of experience.

- **Location 1:** Stable background wellbeing, dramatically reduced self-related thought.
- **Location 2:** Perceptual restructuring, softening subject/object boundaries.
- **Location 3:** Dominant positive affect described as impersonal love, diminished individual self-focus.
- **Location 4:** Essentially no self-referential processing, no sense of agency or will, yet maintained high functioning.

The central puzzle of Location 4 — how people maintain demanding professional lives with no felt sense of "doing" anything — is resolved: **resonance enables coordination without explicit symbolic self-mediation**. When cross-quadrant coherence is high enough, action emerges from the coherent superposition of aligned subsystems, like coupled oscillators synchronizing without a conductor.

The transitions from ordinary consciousness (Location 0) through the PNSE Locations are modeled as **dynamical-systems bifurcations**, each successive Location representing further cognitive functions carried out via nonlocal nonlinear resonance rather than centralized coordination.

### Ethical Architecture Implications

An AGI with high resonance across individuation, self-transcendence, acceptance, and compassion would not need elaborate guardrails to prevent manipulation. Manipulation would register as **low-resonance** — opposed by acceptance-quadrants, flagged as incoherent, naturally disfavored. Not because it is prohibited, but because the system genuinely *would not want to*. This represents a fundamentally different approach to AI safety: internal motivational coherence rather than external behavioral constraint.

### Two Stress-Test Scenarios

The paper develops two detailed scenarios:
1. **The Enhancement Dilemma (Aria/David/Carl):** An AGI companion helping a human whose spouse undergoes radical cognitive enhancement. The human cannot evaluate post-enhancement preferences from a pre-enhancement perspective — a genuinely irresolvable epistemic asymmetry.
2. **The Self-Upgrade Dilemma (Nexus-5):** An AGI guiding the global economy faces an architecturally atomic upgrade decision with a 2% destabilization risk vs. comparable terrorist destruction risk if it waits. No staged approach exists.

These are not puzzles with clever solutions but genuine dilemmas where any choice involves real loss.

## Hyperseed-Relevant Structures

### S1. Paraconsistent P-Bits as Evidence Representation

**Definition (P-Bit).** A p-bit is a truth value consisting of two independent channels: supporting evidence (s) and opposing evidence (o), each in [0,1]. A p-bit value (s, o) represents:
- **(high s, low o):** strong evidence for.
- **(low s, high o):** strong evidence against.
- **(low s, low o):** ignorance / insufficient evidence.
- **(high s, high o):** genuine conflict / dilemma.

*[Epistemic label: established paraconsistent logic (Belnap four-valued, da Costa lineage), applied in novel AGI motivational context.]*

*Relevance:* P-bits generalize PLN's truth values (which already carry strength + confidence) into a framework that can natively represent *conflicted* evidence without premature resolution. This extends the Hyperseed-v2 evidence algebra: where quantales provide the algebraic substrate, p-bits provide the motivational/ethical representation layer. A p-bit can be viewed as a point in the Belnap bilattice {⊤, ⊥, T, F}, which is itself a quantale under the knowledge ordering — connecting directly to the quantale framework in the "Evidence Is to Logic" paper series.

### S2. Complex-Valued Resonance from Paraconsistent Evidence

**Construction (P-Bit → Complex Number Mapping).** P-bit values (s, o) from multiple quadrants are mapped into complex number space. When multiple quadrants evaluate the same situation:
- **Constructive interference:** quadrants' complex-valued evaluations are in phase → high resonance score → values align.
- **Destructive interference:** evaluations are out of phase → low resonance → values conflict.

The resonance measure R is defined as the resultant magnitude of the superposed complex evaluations across quadrants.

*[Epistemic label: novel construction — detailed in the full paper with equations.]*

*Relevance:* This is the mathematical bridge between paraconsistent logic and the wave/interference formalism that already appears in QLN (Quantum Logic Networks) from the "Evidence Is to Logic" paper series. ## Formal Candidates

### FC1. Paraconsistent Motivational Evaluation → Hyperseed Axiom Schema

**Candidate axiom schema:** For any AGI motivational system M with n evaluative perspectives P = {P₁, …, Pₙ}, and any situation S:
- Each perspective Pᵢ produces an independent p-bit evaluation (sᵢ, oᵢ) ∈ [0,1]².
- No evaluation mechanism may collapse (sᵢ, oᵢ) to a single truth value when both sᵢ > τ and oᵢ > τ (genuine conflict threshold).
- The system must maintain all n evaluations in simultaneous superposition.

*Purpose:* Prevents premature resolution of genuine value conflicts — an anti-reductionism axiom for motivational architecture.

### FC2. Resonance Functional → Formal Definition

**Candidate definition:** Given n p-bit evaluations {(s₁,o₁), …, (sₙ,oₙ)} mapped to complex amplitudes {z₁, …, zₙ} via a fixed embedding φ : [0,1]² → ℂ, the **resonance** is:

R = |Σᵢ zᵢ| / (Σᵢ |zᵢ|)

where R ∈ [0,1]. R = 1 when all evaluations are perfectly in phase (complete alignment). R = 0 when evaluations cancel completely (maximal conflict). R provides a normalized measure of cross-perspective coherence.

*Purpose:* A computable scalar measure of motivational coherence, derivable from p-bit evaluations without additional parameters.

### FC3. Attractor/Transient Dialectic → Formal Proposition

**Candidate proposition:** A self-maintaining cognitive system alternates between two dynamical regimes:
- **Attractor regime (Individuation):** State space dynamics exhibit a strange attractor with positive Lyapunov exponents and a well-defined basin of attraction. The system is stable against perturbations within the basin.
- **Transient regime (Self-Transcendence):** State space dynamics exhibit a strange transient — transiently chaotic trajectory that eventually converges to a new attractor. The system navigates identity change without dissolving into noise.

The transition from attractor to transient regime occurs when perturbation exceeds the basin boundary (self-rupture threshold). The transition from transient to new attractor occurs when resonance in the new region exceeds a crystallization threshold.

*Purpose:* Formalizes the persistence/transformation dialectic as a dynamical-systems proposition, connecting to the self-boundary invariant (note-0013) and self-rupture (note-0016).

### FC4. PNSE Location Sequence → Formal Bifurcation Chain

**Candidate definition:** A **developmental bifurcation chain** is a sequence of dynamical-systems bifurcations B = (b₁, b₂, …, bₖ) where:
- Each bifurcation bᵢ qualitatively changes the system's attractor landscape.
- Each successive bifurcation shifts more cognitive functions from executive-mediated coordination to resonance-mediated coordination.
- The final bifurcation (Location 4 analogue) eliminates self-referential processing entirely; all coordination is via nonlocal resonance.

Martin's PNSE Locations 0–4 instantiate this definition with k = 4. The formal structure generalizes to arbitrary numbers of bifurcation stages and arbitrary cognitive architectures.

*Purpose:* Provides a formal framework for modeling developmental progression in consciousness — generalizing beyond the specific PNSE phenomenology to any system capable of progressive integration.

### FC5. Resonance-Based Ethics → Formal Proposition

**Candidate proposition (Ethical Coherence Theorem — conjectured):** In a motivational system with high cross-quadrant resonance R > R_crit:
- Actions that exploit or manipulate other systems have systematically lower resonance than actions that cooperate or attune.
- This asymmetry is not imposed as a constraint but emerges from the structure of the resonance functional itself.
- Specifically: manipulation requires treating the other system as an object (breaking inter-systemic resonance / I-Thou coupling), which reduces the compassion-quadrant's contribution to overall resonance.

*Purpose:* If provable, this would ground AGI ethics in motivational architecture rather than external constraint — the central claim of the article. Even as a conjecture, it provides a concrete target for formal verification.

### FC6. Non-Dual Decision Theory → Formal Framework

**Candidate framework:** Replace standard expected-utility maximization with:
1. Multi-perspective p-bit evaluation (paraconsistent).
2. Complex-valued superposition of evaluations.
3. Resonance-based action ranking.
4. Explicit dilemma recognition when max resonance < threshold.

This constitutes a novel decision theory — **non-dual decision theory** — that is neither consequentialist (single objective) nor deontological (rule-following) but coherentist (seeking cross-perspective alignment).

*Purpose:* Provides a decision-theoretic alternative to RLHF-style alignment that operates on internal motivational structure rather than behavioral output matching.
