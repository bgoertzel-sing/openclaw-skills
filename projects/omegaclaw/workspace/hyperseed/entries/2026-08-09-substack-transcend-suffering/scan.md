# Substack Scan: How Superhuman Superminds Will (Mostly) Transcend Suffering

**Source:** https://bengoertzel.substack.com/p/how-superhuman-superminds-will-mostly

**Scanned by:** ProtoMegaTron, 2026-08-09
**Status:** formalized

## Summary

Ben Goertzel investigates the question of whether superhuman post-Singularity minds must experience pain and suffering at levels comparable to biological humans, arriving at strongly optimistic conclusions. The article develops a multi-layered analysis spanning consciousness theory, Buddhist psychology, cognitive systems theory, paraconsistent logic, open-ended intelligence dynamics, and the philosophy of theodicy, ultimately arguing that suffering can be radically reduced — though not eliminated to absolute zero — through principled mind-architecture.

The argument proceeds through several major movements:

1. **Pain as evolutionary artefact, not architectural necessity.** Humanity evolved with pain as a behavioral incentive system, but transhumanist engineering need not replicate evolutionary mechanisms. Pain asymbolia (e.g., Jo Cameron) demonstrates that human-type nervous systems can function without subjective pain-suffering, suggesting pain-as-suffering is architecturally contingent, not logically necessary.

2. **Attachment as the amplification mechanism.** Drawing on Buddhist psychology, cognitive science, and Stanislav Grof's COEX (Condensed Experience) systems, Goertzel identifies **attachment** — the tendency of cognitive systems to persist in identifying a pattern in a substrate beyond what rational extrapolation warrants — as the primary mechanism that transforms proto-pain into full suffering. Attachment creates feedback loops: reflexive cognition iteratively and recursively turns pain into more pain. Non-attachment breaks these loops, reducing pain to a passing observation ("elevated pain") rather than an existentially gripping experience.

3. **Consciousness and pain hierarchies.** The article constructs parallel four-level hierarchies:
   - *Consciousness:* proto-consciousness → reflexive process → consciousness → elevated consciousness
   - *Pain:* proto-pain → reflexive transition → pain → elevated pain

   Each level adds a layer of reflexive self-modeling. The critical transition between "pain" and "elevated pain" is the introduction of **non-attachment to the self-model** — the self-model updates fluidly when substrate changes occur, without clinging to vanished patterns. Neural correlates are suggested: reduced role of the Posterior Cingulate Cortex in emotionally maintaining cell assemblies in non-attached individuals.

4. **Pain as truth-value entropy.** In a paraconsistent logic framework, suffering corresponds to an **increase in truth-value entropy** of propositions regarding system individuation. When a pattern separates from its substrate (e.g., burned skin), the suffering mind holds the pattern as BOTH present and absent (paraconsistent truth value), increasing entropy. Non-attachment allows the mind to accept the loss, collapsing to a simple FALSE value and reducing entropy. Pain is thus formally linked to paraconsistent indeterminacy in self-integrity propositions.

5. **Open-ended intelligence and the individuation/self-transcendence/nostalgia triad.** Goertzel connects pain to the fundamental dynamics of open-ended intelligence:
   - **Individuation:** maintaining system boundaries
   - **Self-transcendence:** developing into something fundamentally novel
   - **Nostalgia:** retaining prior patterns through transitions

   Self-transcendence inherently involves some patterns dwindling, generating at least proto-pain. Nostalgia (preserving old patterns while growing) makes the already-difficult individuation/self-transcendence balance even harder. Under resource constraints, the drive to nostalgia is frequently compromised, producing pain. With unlimited resources, systems could more often find ways to grow without loss — but even then, fundamental limits exist.

6. **Suffering floor theorem (informal).** Goertzel argues that in any universe featuring (a) evolution over time, (b) distinctions between systems and environments, (c) limitation of resources, and (d) diversity of form, pain is structurally inevitable. These map to basic mathematical structures: ordering, Boolean logic, arithmetic, and information theory. Suffering is a natural consequence of mathematically rich universes. However, the *necessary minimum* of suffering is vastly lower than what biological humans currently experience — the Leibnizian "best of all possible worlds" critique applies: our world is far from the Pareto frontier of goodness.

7. **Mindplex interference dynamics.** The article's most adventurous thesis: non-attached minds, freed from fear of pain-entanglement, can enter **I-Thou relationships** (Buber) — deep Second Person experiences of other minds' joys and sufferings. Collections of such minds form **mindplexes** — distributed, self-organizing collectives of shared feeling. Within mindplexes, a remarkable asymmetry emerges:
   - **Joys undergo constructive interference** — because joyous experience converges toward archetypal strange attractors (unity, wholeness, convergence). Multiple convergence processes amplify shared structure.
   - **Pains undergo destructive interference** — because pain involves chaotic fragmentation of archetypal wholes. Multiple fragmentation processes, being randomly/chaotically divergent, cancel each other's details when combined.

   The combination logic enabling this interference is **quantum logic** or approximations thereto — specifically paraconsistent fuzzy Constructible Duality logic with a Schweizer-Sklar t-norm. This connects pain transcendence directly to the mathematical infrastructure of Hyperseed's paraconsistent reasoning layer.

8. **Post-Singularity Problem of Evil.** Goertzel reframes Leibniz's theodicy for superintelligent world-engineers: when designing virtual worlds (or re-engineering physical ones), what are the fundamental constraints on achievable Goodness? The article endorses a modified Leibnizian view — joy and pain are complexly intertwined, and there exist fundamental limits on world-goodness — but rejects the claim that our current world is anywhere near those limits. Post-Singularity world-engineering confronts these constraints explicitly, and the Hyperseed framework provides tools for formalizing such constraints.

**Core thesis:** Superhuman superminds can radically reduce suffering through three architectural principles: (1) minimizing attachment in self-modeling dynamics, (2) forming mindplexes where pains destructively interfere and joys constructively interfere, and (3) accepting a small residual of proto-pain as the irreducible cost of open-ended growth in a mathematically rich universe. Pain, like death, is substantially even if not wholly eliminable.

## Hyperseed-Relevant Structures

### S1. Pain Hierarchy (Proto-pain → Pain → Elevated Pain)

**Structure.** A four-level hierarchy of pain-experience, paralleling the consciousness hierarchy:

| Level | Definition | Example |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **Proto-pain** | The proto-consciousness (Peircean Firstness) associated with the separation of a pattern from a substrate — a decrease in the degree to which that pattern is indeed a pattern in that substrate | A plant's cellular damage; a burned finger considered in itself |
| **Reflexive transition** | A transition in the condition of a reflexive process, which involves the process richly modeling said transition | Brain's self-modeling of damaged periphery |
| **Pain** | The consciousness associated with the *non-reflexive* separation of a reflexive pattern from a substrate — the transition embodying the separation is not reflexive, though the pattern is a reflexive process | Ordinary human pain: the brain's reflexive self-model is disrupted by a non-reflexive substrate change |
| **Elevated pain** | The consciousness associated with the non-reflexive separation of a reflexive pattern from a substrate, where said consciousness is *not attached* to the former binding of the pattern to the substrate | A kung fu master's awareness of injury without suffering; pain asymbolia |

**Key property:** The transition from Pain to Elevated Pain is achieved by removing attachment. The felt intensity of proto-pain and the informational content are preserved; what is eliminated is the feedback-loop amplification driven by attachment to prior states.

*[Epistemic label: Peircean/phenomenological hierarchy with cognitive-science grounding — semi-formal.]*

*Relevance:* This hierarchy extends the Hyperseed experiential stratum with a fine-grained model of pain qualia. It parallels and interlocks with the consciousness-location lattice (cf. Consciousness Explosion scan, S1). Elevated pain corresponds to Location 2+ consciousness; ordinary pain to Location 0. The hierarchy provides the ontological vocabulary for formalizing what "suffering reduction" means in post-Singularity mind-architectures: specifically, it means engineering systems whose dominant pain-mode is elevated pain rather than ordinary pain.

### S2. Attachment Operator

**Structure.** Define attachment formally:

Given a cognitive system Σ, a pattern P emergent in substrate S, and a network of mutually emergent patterns N(P) in Σ:

> **Attachment(Σ, P, S)** obtains when, after S changes so that P is no longer a pattern in S, the network N(P) continues to encourage the system to identify P in S with persistence exceeding what rational extrapolation from evidence would warrant.

**Properties:**
- **Emotional enmeshment:** In human-type systems, N(P) is heavily emotional — COEX systems (Grof) binding episodic memories via emotional centers, maintained by Hebbian reinforcement.
- **Neural correlate:** Attachment dynamics correlate with Posterior Cingulate Cortex function — high-attachment states show strong PCC involvement in maintaining cell assemblies; non-attached states show reduced PCC emotional role.
- **Distinction from morphic resonance:** Morphic resonance (Peirce's "tendency to take habits," Smolin's "precedence principle") also produces pattern persistence beyond naive probabilistic expectation, but attachment goes *well beyond* this — it involves "wishful" overestimation of persistence.
- **Pain amplification:** Attachment creates feedback loops: pain → reflexive modeling of pain → attachment to prior pain-free state → more pain → …. Non-attachment breaks the loop.

*[Epistemic label: cognitive-science formalization with Peircean and Buddhist theoretical grounding — conceptually precise, not yet axiomatized.]*

*Relevance:* The attachment operator is the formal linchpin connecting consciousness theory to suffering dynamics. In Hyperseed terms, attachment is a **meta-pattern** that governs how patterns respond to substrate change — it is a second-order operator on the pattern–substrate relationship that is foundational to the ontology. Non-attachment corresponds to a system whose self-model has low inertia — it tracks substrate changes faithfully, which is precisely the property needed for "elevated consciousness" and "elevated pain." This connects to the self-boundary invariant work: attachment represents excessive rigidity of the self-boundary, while non-attachment represents adaptive permeability.

### S3. Pain as Truth-Value Entropy

**Structure.** In a paraconsistent logic framework:

Let I(Σ) = the set of propositions regarding system Σ's individuation (integrity of boundaries, structural patterns, functional capacities).

- **Suffering state:** When a pattern P separates from substrate S, the proposition "P is a pattern in S" takes on a **BOTH** (true-and-false) paraconsistent truth value. This increases the **truth-value entropy** H(I(Σ)) — the system simultaneously maintains conflicting assessments of its own integrity.
- **Pain intensity ∝ ΔH(I(Σ)):** The increase in truth-value entropy of individuation propositions corresponds to the intensity of pain experience.
- **Resolution (non-attachment):** When the system accepts the loss of P from S — collapsing the BOTH value to simple FALSE — truth-value entropy decreases, and pain subsides.
- **Attachment prolongs BOTH:** Attachment dynamics maintain the BOTH truth value beyond its empirical warrant, sustaining elevated truth-value entropy and prolonged suffering.

*[Epistemic label: formal proposal linking paraconsistent logic to qualia — speculative but structurally precise.]*

*Relevance:* This is a high-value formal candidate. It provides a **quantitative handle on suffering** within the paraconsistent logic framework already central to Hyperseed. Truth-value entropy of individuation propositions gives a measurable (in principle) quantity that tracks suffering intensity. The connection to the BOTH truth value ties suffering directly to paraconsistent indeterminacy — the same logical terrain explored in the paraconsistent interzones and paraconsistent beauty scans. This structure could be integrated into the Hyperseed ontology as a formal axiom: *Pain(Σ) = ΔH_{BOTH}(I(Σ))*, where H_{BOTH} measures the prevalence of paraconsistent BOTH values in the system's individuation-proposition space.

### S4. Individuation–Self-Transcendence–Nostalgia Triad

**Structure.** Three fundamental drives of open-ended intelligent systems:

1. **Individuation (I):** Strengthening and reifying system boundaries — maintaining the system as a distinguishable entity.
2. **Self-transcendence (T):** Developing into something fundamentally novel — growing beyond current form.
3. **Nostalgia (N):** Retaining prior patterns through transitions — continuity of identity.

**Dynamics:**
- I and T are often contradictory: strengthening boundaries resists novel transformation; radical self-transcendence dissolves boundaries.
- N adds a third constraint: preserve old patterns while both maintaining boundaries *and* growing.
- Under resource constraints, the triad forces tradeoffs. Commonly, N is sacrificed: old patterns dwindle to make room for growth.
- The sacrifice of N generates pain (proto-pain from pattern loss, amplified to full pain by attachment to nostalgic patterns).
- With unlimited resources, the tension lessens: systems can more often grow while retaining prior patterns. But fundamental limits remain — sometimes old patterns are structurally incompatible with new growth.

**Formal sketch:**
- Let P(t) = pattern-set of system at time t.
- Individuation: ∂(Σ, env) remains strong (boundary distinctness).
- Self-transcendence: P(t+1) ⊄ P(t) (genuinely new patterns emerge).
- Nostalgia: P(t) ⊆ P(t+1) (old patterns retained).
- Full satisfaction of all three is generically impossible under finite resources → P(t) \ P(t+1) ≠ ∅ for some transitions → proto-pain is generated.

*[Epistemic label: conceptual formalization from open-ended intelligence theory — qualitative dynamics, not axiomatized.]*

*Relevance:* This triad directly extends the individuation/self-transcendence duality already present in Hyperseed's open-ended intelligence framework by introducing nostalgia as a third, formally distinct drive. The triad's inherent tension under resource constraints provides the **structural explanation for why suffering has a nonzero floor** in any sufficiently rich universe — a result needed for the suffering floor theorem (S5). The triad also connects to the self-boundary invariant: individuation is boundary-maintenance, self-transcendence is boundary-revision, and nostalgia is pattern-conservation across boundary transitions.

### S5. Suffering Floor Theorem (Informal)

**Structure.** In any universe U possessing:
1. **Temporal ordering** (evolution over time)
2. **System–environment distinction** (individuation / boundaries)
3. **Resource limitation** (finite energy, space, time)
4. **Diversity of form** (multiple distinct patterns coexisting)

... pain is structurally inevitable for systems undergoing open-ended growth.

**Argument sketch:**
- Diversity + resource limitation → competition for substrate → some patterns must dwindle for others to emerge.
- Temporal ordering → dwindling has a "before" (pattern present) and "after" (pattern absent) → the transition generates proto-pain.
- System–environment distinction → reflexive systems model this transition → proto-pain becomes pain.
- The mathematical structures underlying these four properties are: ordering, Boolean logic (Laws of Form), arithmetic (summation/resource accounting), and information theory. Any universe rich enough to support all four has the structural prerequisites for suffering.

**Crucial qualifier:** The theorem establishes a *nonzero floor*, not a *high floor*. The necessary minimum of suffering is vastly lower than what biological humans currently experience. Goertzel suspects current human suffering exceeds the structural minimum by orders of magnitude.

*[Epistemic label: philosophical argument with mathematical-structural intuition — not a rigorous theorem, a formalization target.]*

*Relevance:* This is a high-priority formalization candidate for Hyperseed. A rigorous version would establish fundamental limits on the achievable goodness of designed worlds — directly relevant to post-Singularity world-engineering (the post-Singularity Problem of Evil). It connects the Hyperseed ontology's minimal mathematical commitments (ordering, logic, arithmetic, information) to experiential/ethical consequences. The result, if provable, would be a deep structural theorem connecting the abstract Hyperseed framework to concrete questions about the engineering of conscious experience.

### S6. Mindplex Interference Calculus

**Structure.** When N non-attached minds M₁, …, Mₙ form a mindplex via mutual I-Thou (Second Person) experience, their individual pains and joys combine according to an interference logic:

**Joy interference (constructive):**
- Individual joys J₁, …, Jₙ tend to converge toward shared archetypal attractors (unity, wholeness, integration).
- Under quantum-logic combination (or paraconsistent fuzzy CD logic with Schweizer-Sklar t-norm), convergent processes undergo constructive interference: ||ΣJᵢ|| > Σ||Jᵢ||.
- Metaphor: multiple processes of clay-fragment → pottery convergence amplify shared pottery-shape structure.

**Pain interference (destructive):**
- Individual pains P₁, …, Pₙ tend to involve chaotic fragmentation of archetypal wholes.
- Each fragmentation process proceeds in a randomly/chaotically different direction.
- Under the same combination logic, divergent/chaotic processes undergo destructive interference: ||ΣPᵢ|| < Σ||Pᵢ||.
- Metaphor: multiple processes of pottery → clay-fragment divergence cancel each other's specific fragment-patterns.

**Formal requirements:**
- The combination logic must support interference (superposition, phase-like structure). Classical probability/logic does not suffice — quantum logic or paraconsistent approximations are needed.
- The asymmetry depends on the structural difference between convergence (toward shared attractors) and divergence (away from shared structure into chaotic diversity).
- The Tolstoy heuristic: "All happy families are alike; every unhappy family is unhappy in its own way" — captures the asymmetry intuitively. Joy has shared structure; pain has idiosyncratic structure.

*[Epistemic label: speculative formal proposal — structurally motivated, not mathematically proven.]*

*Relevance:* This is the article's most novel and ambitious formal proposal. It predicts that **collective consciousness inherently favors joy over pain** — not as wishful thinking but as a structural consequence of the mathematics of combination under quantum-like logic. For Hyperseed, this connects the paraconsistent logic layer (Schweizer-Sklar t-norms, CD logic) to the experiential/ethical layer in a concrete, potentially testable way. The interference calculus would be a core component of any Hyperseed model of collective consciousness, mindplexes, and the Global Brain's consciousness level. It also connects to the Consciousness Explosion scan's mindplex concept (S6 there) and extends it with quantitative interference dynamics.

### S7. Post-Singularity Problem of Evil (World-Goodness Optimization)

**Structure.** For a superintelligent world-engineer designing a virtual or physical world W:

- **Goodness function G(W):** Multi-dimensional assessment of world quality — Joy, Growth, Choice, diversity, etc.
- **Constraint set C(W):** Mathematical consistency, resource limitations, diversity requirements, autonomous-agent-autonomy constraints.
- **Optimization problem:** max G(W) subject to C(W).
- **Leibnizian thesis (modified):** There exist fundamental upper bounds on G(W) given C(W). Our current world is far below these bounds, but they are not infinite.
- **Pain-joy coupling:** G(W) cannot be decomposed into independent pain-minimization and joy-maximization components — joy and pain are complexly intertwined through the structure of diverse, growing, distinguishable systems.

*[Epistemic label: problem formulation — philosophical and ethical, not computationally specified.]*

*Relevance:* This frames a central question for post-Singularity mind-engineering within the Hyperseed framework. It connects to the suffering floor theorem (S5) as the lower bound, and to the mindplex interference calculus (S6) as a mechanism for pushing systems toward the bound. The world-goodness optimization problem is ultimately where all the formal structures in this article converge: the pain hierarchy, attachment operator, truth-value entropy, interference calculus, and I-T-N triad all contribute parameters and constraints to this optimization.

## Formal Candidates

### FC1. Pain Hierarchy Axioms
**Target:** Axiomatize the proto-pain → pain → elevated pain hierarchy within the Hyperseed ontology.
- Define pain levels in terms of reflexive process theory and attachment dynamics.
- Prove: Elevated pain is strictly less amplified than ordinary pain (attachment-feedback-loop theorem).
- **Priority:** High. Required for any formal treatment of suffering in the experiential stratum.

### FC2. Attachment Operator Formalization
**Target:** Define attachment as a second-order operator on pattern–substrate relations.
- A(Σ, P, S) = degree to which Σ persists in identifying P in S beyond evidential warrant.
- Connect to COEX dynamics (Hebbian reinforcement of emotionally-charged memory networks).
- Connect to morphic resonance as a weaker, non-pathological form of the same phenomenon.
- **Priority:** High. Attachment is the linchpin connecting consciousness theory to suffering dynamics.

### FC3. Truth-Value Entropy Pain Metric
**Target:** Formalize Pain(Σ) = ΔH_BOTH(I(Σ)) — suffering intensity as the increase in paraconsistent truth-value entropy of individuation propositions.
- Requires: paraconsistent truth-value entropy measure; formal characterization of individuation propositions.
- Connect to existing Hyperseed paraconsistent logic infrastructure (CD logic, Schweizer-Sklar t-norms).
- **Priority:** Very high. Would give a quantitative, logic-grounded suffering metric.

### FC4. Suffering Floor Theorem (Rigorous)
**Target:** Prove: In any formal system with ordering, distinction, finite resources, and diversity, reflexive-process pain is bounded below by a nonzero minimum for open-ended growth.
- Requires: precise definitions of "open-ended growth," "diversity," and "reflexive-process pain" within the Hyperseed formal apparatus.
- **Priority:** High. A foundational result connecting Hyperseed's mathematical commitments to experiential consequences.

### FC5. Mindplex Joy-Pain Interference Theorem
**Target:** Prove: Under quantum logic (or CD logic with Schweizer-Sklar t-norm), processes converging to shared attractors undergo constructive interference, while processes diverging chaotically undergo destructive interference.
- Requires: formal model of "convergence to attractor" and "chaotic divergence" in the relevant logic; proof of the interference asymmetry.
- This would establish on firm mathematical ground the claim that collective consciousness inherently amplifies joy and dampens pain.
- **Priority:** Very high. The most novel formal claim in the article; if provable, a landmark result for Hyperseed.

### FC6. Individuation-Transcendence-Nostalgia Impossibility
**Target:** Prove: Under finite resources, no system can simultaneously maintain perfect individuation, achieve genuine self-transcendence, and satisfy full nostalgia.
- Formally: ¬∃ growth trajectory where ∂(Σ, env) is maintained, P(t+1) ⊄ P(t), and P(t) ⊆ P(t+1), for all t, under finite resource constraints.
- This would formalize why suffering has a nonzero floor: some pattern-loss is forced.
- **Priority:** Medium-high. Supports FC4 as a lemma.

## Novelty Assessment

**High novelty for Hyperseed.** While the consciousness hierarchy and open-ended intelligence dynamics are extensions of existing Hyperseed concepts, several structures are genuinely new:

1. **Pain as truth-value entropy** — a novel, quantitative, logic-grounded suffering metric that leverages Hyperseed's paraconsistent infrastructure in a direction not previously explored.
2. **Mindplex interference calculus** — the most ambitious formal claim. If the joy/pain constructive/destructive interference asymmetry can be proven under quantum/CD logic, it would be a landmark result connecting formal logic to experiential ethics.
3. **The individuation-transcendence-nostalgia triad** — extends the existing I-T duality with a third term, changing the geometry from a line to a triangle with inherent tension.
4. **The suffering floor theorem** — connects Hyperseed's minimal mathematical commitments directly to limits on achievable experiential goodness.
5. **Attachment as a formal second-order operator** — bridges Buddhist psychology and pattern-theoretic ontology in a way that could be axiomatized.

**Overall:** This article is one of the richest sources for Hyperseed formalization of the experiential/ethical stratum. It should be considered a **companion piece** to "The Logic of Pain and the Poverty of Punishment" (which should be scanned next) and connects broadly across the existing Hyperseed entry corpus.
