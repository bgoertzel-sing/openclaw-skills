## Summary

Ben Goertzel presents a speculative unified physics program whose central chain
runs: **causal sets + Occamistic precedence → pregeometric world-crystal →
spacetime + quantum theory + gauge fields + general relativity**. The article is
a public-facing summary of the full paper *"Quaternionic World-Crystals as
Pregeometric Middleware"* (Google Drive).

The program synthesizes five previously separate lines of work:

1. **Causal set theory** (Bombelli, Lee, Meyer, Sorkin) — spacetime as a
   partially ordered set of discrete events.
2. **Occamistic Precedence** (Goertzel's modification of Smolin's Precedence
   Principle + Occam's Razor bias) — a selection principle biasing causal-set
   growth toward simple, historically reinforced motifs.
3. **Planck-Kleinert Crystal (PKC)** (Danielewski, building on Kleinert) —
   vacuum as an fcc-like crystal at Planck scale, with particles, EM waves, and
   gravity emerging as collective excitations, deformations, and defects.
4. **Quaternionic Cauchy dynamics** (Danielewski & Sapa 2020) — reformulation of
   Cauchy elasticity in quaternionic terms, producing a quaternionic Schrödinger
   equation from elastic deformation (one scalar compression + three
   twist/torsion components → quaternionic field).
5. **Quantale weakness / Wu Wei geodesics** (Goertzel's AGI-25 keynote and
   related papers) — a generalized simplicity/effort geometry unifying least
   action, Occam's razor, entropic optimal transport, and Schrödinger bridges.

The **key conceptual move** is making the world-crystal *pregeometric*: it is not
a crystal *in* spacetime but a causal-motif structure *from which* spacetime
emerges by meta-crystallization. This sidesteps the standard "preferred-frame
ether" objection that would otherwise be fatal. The proposed ontological stack:

| Layer | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **1. Bare causal order** | Discrete events + causal precedence |
| **2. Occamistic selection** | Simple, repeated, low-weakness motifs favored |
| **3. Causal-PKC motifs** | Pregeometric Planck-Kleinert crystal structures emerge |
| **4. Meta-crystallized spacetime** | Smooth Lorentzian spacetime as continuum approximation |
| **5. Quaternionic Cauchy dynamics** | Elastic deformation dynamics → quantum/field behavior |
| **6. Particles, gauge fields, gravity** | Localized excitations, motif holonomies, defects |

The article explicitly positions this as *motivated by the Hyperseed ontology*
but insists the mathematical physics must stand or fall on empirical merits
independently.

### Empirical Pressure Points Identified

- Analog Sp(1)-lump experiments in engineered media.

### S2. Causal Sets as Occasions + Precedence

**[Observed]** Causal set theory starts from a partially ordered set of discrete
events. In Hyperseed terms: **occasions** (irreducible experiential events)
ordered by **causal precedence** (the temporal/causal "before" relation). This
is the deepest substrate — "where ontology bottoms out from a physics
perspective."

*Relevance:* Direct mapping to Hyperseed's foundational concepts of
**Occasion** and **Distinction**. The partial order is a distinction-structure
(which pairs of events are ordered vs. unordered). The causal set *is* the
Hyperseed event-web at the physics level.

### S3. Occamistic Precedence = Simplicity × Habit

**[Observed]** Smolin's Precedence Principle: "when some physical situation
recurs, the outcomes that have previously occurred become more likely to recur."
Goertzel's twist: "among repeated or possible patterns, the simpler ones are
favored more." Combined informal principle: **"What has happened before is
favored; but among repeated or possible patterns, the simpler ones are favored
more."**

Formal version: **"Nature prefers histories that are simple,
precedent-supported, and minimally forced."**

*Relevance:* This is the direct physics avatar of Hyperseed's trio:
- **Repetition / habit / morphic resonance** → precedent reinforcement
- **Simplicity / Occam's razor** → complexity bias
- **Wu Wei / minimal effort** → dynamical favorability

These three Hyperseed concepts are unified into a single selection principle for
pregeometric dynamics.

### S4. Quantale Weakness as the Selection Geometry

**[Observed]** Causal-set histories and world-crystal motifs are scored by a
**quantale weakness measure** that combines: (a) dynamical effort to realize;
(b) descriptive simplicity in a compact representational format; (c) strength of
reinforcement by precedent; (d) how well it supports stable physical laws
downstream.

The causal-PKC world-crystal is characterized as a **low-weakness attractor** in
the space of possible pregeometric histories — "something that, given the rules
of the game, you might actually expect to see crystallize out due to its uncommon
simplicity according to the relevant simplicity-measurement quantale."

*Relevance:* This is the same quantale weakness machinery developed in
Goertzel's AGI-25 keynote and used in the "Evidence Is to Logic What Energy Is
to Physics" paper series. The quantale is the algebraic substrate that unifies
Kolmogorov complexity (bit-length simplicity), dynamical effort (action
minimization), and precedent reinforcement (morphic resonance) into a single
composable measure. The physics program is literally *scored by the Hyperseed
weakness quantale*.

*Relevance:* Wu Wei is an explicit Hyperseed-v2 concept (noted as an "eccentric

### S6. Quaternionic Structure: Why ℍ?

**[Observed]** Ordinary QM uses complex amplitudes (ℂ). Quaternionic QM (ℍ)
extends this with three imaginary units i, j, k. Danielewski & Sapa's innovation:
quaternions arise from the physical structure of an elastic continuum — one
scalar compression + three twist/torsion components naturally packaged as a
quaternionic field.

In the world-crystal reinterpretation: the quaternionic field describes **local
deformation of the emergent crystal-like substrate**. In a fixed-phase sector,
the quaternionic Schrödinger equation collapses to the familiar complex
Schrödinger equation. In the full Sp(1)-phase version, the internal quaternionic
orientation yields something that "starts to look surprisingly like a nonabelian
gauge structure."

**Core claim:** *"Complex quantum mechanics may be a projection of a richer
quaternionic deformation dynamics of the pregeometric world-crystal."*

*Relevance:* This connects directly to the "Evidence Is to Logic" paper's
epistemic reconstruction of QM. There, Solèr's theorem (+ local tomography)
selects ℂ from the division algebras ℝ, ℂ, ℍ as the unique evidence algebra
satisfying situatedness + conservation + minimality + local tomography. The
physics paper suggests ℂ may be a *projection* of a deeper ℍ-structure — which
would mean the local tomography axiom (which selects ℂ over ℍ) is an
*approximation*, valid in the fixed-phase sector but broken in the full
Sp(1)-phase regime. This is a potentially deep tension or synthesis point between
the two programs.

## Formal Candidates

### FC-1: Pregeometric Ontological Stack (Architectural Definition)

**Claim:** Physics can be organized as a six-layer stack where each layer
emerges from the one below via a specific mathematical mechanism:

1. (C, ≤) — causal set (partial order of discrete events)
2. W_Occ: C → Q — Occamistic weakness scoring on causal sets, valued in a
   quantale Q
3. PKC motifs — local minima of W_Occ in the space of causal substructures
4. (M, g) — Lorentzian manifold obtained by coarse-graining / continuum limit
   of PKC motif network
5. Ψ: M → ℍ — quaternionic deformation field satisfying Cauchy-type dynamics
6. Particles, gauge fields, gravity — localized excitations, holonomies, and
   defects of Ψ and the motif network

**Formalization target:** Make the inter-layer maps precise. Layer 2→3 requires
defining the space of causal substructures and showing PKC motifs are local
weakness minima. Layer 3→4 requires a continuum limit theorem. Layer 4→5 requires
deriving quaternionic Cauchy dynamics from the lattice. Layer 5→6 requires
classifying stable excitations and connecting holonomies to gauge groups.

### FC-2: Occamistic Precedence Selection (Conjecture)

**Claim:** Under Occamistic precedence (simplicity bias + Smolin-style
precedent reinforcement), causal-set growth favors manifold-like structures
(specifically, PKC-like regular motifs) over generic random causal sets.

**Formalization target:** Define Occamistic precedence as a probability measure
μ_Occ on the space of causal-set extensions, parameterized by a quantale
weakness function W. Prove or simulate: under μ_Occ, the measure concentrates
on causal sets whose local motif structure is PKC-like (fcc-like with Planck
spacing), as opposed to the "Kleitman-Rothschild" dominance of layered posets
in the uniform measure.

### FC-3: Wu Wei Physics Principle (Axiom Candidate)

**Claim:** Physical evolution follows paths of minimal representational effort
— formally, geodesics in a weakness-geometric space that unifies: (a) dynamical
action; (b) descriptive complexity; (c) precedent reinforcement.

**Mathematical form:** Given a quantale (Q, ≤, ⊗, ⋁) and a weakness functional
W: Histories → Q combining the three components, physically realized histories
are critical points of W. The least-action principle, entropy production bounds,
and Occam's razor are all special cases (different choices of quantale and
component weighting).

**Formalization target:** Show that the Euler-Lagrange equations of the Standard
Model + GR can be recovered as critical-point conditions of an appropriate W.
(This is the hardest FC in this scan — essentially the whole physics program in
one conjecture.)

### FC-4: Quaternionic Projection Theorem (Conjecture)

**Claim:** The complex Schrödinger equation is the fixed-phase-sector
restriction of a quaternionic Cauchy deformation equation on the pregeometric
world-crystal. Specifically, there exists a projection π: ℍ → ℂ (restricting to
a fixed Sp(1)-phase sector) such that π ∘ (quaternionic Cauchy dynamics) =
(complex Schrödinger dynamics) on π(Ψ).

**Formalization target:** Define the Sp(1)-phase sector precisely (as a
subalgebra condition or a gauge-fixing condition). Derive the complex
Schrödinger equation from the quaternionic Cauchy equation under this
restriction. Show that the restriction breaks down when the full Sp(1) phase
is active, yielding nonabelian gauge-like structure.

**Connection to "Evidence" paper:** The epistemic reconstruction (Solèr +
local tomography → ℂ) selects the fixed-phase sector. The full ℍ theory
is "pre-epistemic" — deeper than what a locally-tomographic observer can access.
Formalizing this connection would unify the physics and epistemology programs.

### FC-5: Gravity as Weakness-Stationarity of Defect Geometry (Conjecture)

**Claim:** The Einstein field equations emerge as stationarity conditions of a
weakness/defect/entropic action on the pregeometric motif network. Specifically,
the Ricci curvature of the coarse-grained metric corresponds to the density of
motif closure failures (defects), and the Einstein-Hilbert action is the
continuum limit of the total weakness of the defect distribution.

**Formalization target:** Define motif closure failure precisely (as a holonomy
defect of the motif orientation transport). Show that in the continuum limit,
the closure-failure density reduces to Ricci curvature. Derive the
Einstein-Hilbert action from the total weakness of the defect field.

### FC-6: Gauge Fields from Motif Holonomy (Conjecture)

**Claim:** SU(3)-like gauge fields arise from motif holonomies in decorated
causal-PKC structures. Specifically, motif adjacencies carry Sp(1) ≅ SU(2)
internal orientation variables; Wilson loops of these variables yield gauge
curvature; and the full gauge group may emerge from the decoration algebra.

**Formalization target:** Define the motif adjacency graph with Sp(1)-valued
decorations. Define Wilson loops and curvature in this discrete setting. Show
that the Yang-Mills action emerges in the continuum limit. Identify the
mechanism by which SU(3) (rather than SU(2) alone) appears — possibly from a
tensor or product structure on the motif decorations.

### FC-7: Lorentz Restoration (Critical Conjecture)

**Claim:** The pregeometric world-crystal, despite having discrete lattice-like
structure, does not produce detectable Lorentz violations at low energies. The
meta-crystallization process restores Lorentz invariance in the continuum limit.

**Formalization target:** This is the theory's critical empirical constraint.
Define "Lorentz restoration" precisely: in the continuum limit of the causal-PKC
motif network, the emergent metric is Lorentzian and the emergent dynamics is
Lorentz-covariant to within current experimental bounds (Planck-suppressed
corrections are allowed). Prove or give strong evidence (analytical or
numerical) that meta-crystallization achieves this.

### FC-8: Particle Stability from Solitonic Excitations (Conjecture)

**Claim:** Stable, localized, recurrent excitations of the quaternionic
world-crystal (soliton-like lumps, defects, knots, domain-wall structures)
correspond to known particle species. The classification of stable excitations
reproduces (at least the rough features of) the Standard Model particle
spectrum.

**Formalization target:** Classify the topological defects and solitonic
excitations of the quaternionic Cauchy dynamics on a PKC-like lattice. Show
that stable excitations carry quantum numbers (charge, spin, color) matching
particle species. The 3D quaternionic simulations mentioned in the article are
a starting point; larger-scale simulations with realistic parameters are needed.

### FC-9: Local Tomography as Approximation (Bridge Conjecture)

**Claim (inferred, not explicitly stated):** The local tomography axiom that
selects ℂ over ℍ in the epistemic QM reconstruction ("Evidence Is to Logic")
is an *approximation* valid in the fixed-phase sector of the pregeometric
world-crystal. In the full Sp(1)-phase regime, local tomography fails and the
evidence algebra is quaternionic.

**Formalization target:** Define what "local tomography failure" means physically
— what kind of experiment would detect quaternionic effects that violate local
tomography? Connect to the "quaternionic phase-order effects in precision quantum
systems" identified as an empirical pressure point. If correct, this would mean
the epistemic reconstruction derives the *approximate* theory (complex QM) while
the physics program derives the *exact* theory (quaternionic pre-QM).
