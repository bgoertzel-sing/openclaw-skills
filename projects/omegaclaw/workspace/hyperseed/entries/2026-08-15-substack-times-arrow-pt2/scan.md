## Summary

Ben Goertzel's Part 2 blog post takes the physical arrow-of-time framework developed in Part 1 — where time's arrow is the monotone growth of recorded distinctions — and turns it inward, onto the mind. The central question: if subjective time is the rate at which a mind records meaningful distinctions, what does that have to do with intelligence, consciousness, enlightenment, and compassion? The post summarizes a technical sequel paper connecting the five-paper Time's Arrow series (Papers I–V) with the Hyperseed ontology.

The five technical papers build a complete mathematical theory of time's arrow:

**Paper I** (Monotone Weakness & Canonical Quantale, 23pp) establishes the foundational framework: arrows of time as monotone drift of quantale weakness (graphtropy) over operationally defined distinction structures. Key results include the Representation Theorem (strict arrows ↔ progressive dynamics), the two-by-two classification proving law-asymmetry and entropic representability are logically independent, the Canonical Quantale Theorem for driven Cartan pairs (the chamber downset quantale as initial object of the witness category), exhaustiveness/descent/rigidity theorems, and the Fundamental Inequality of weakness production h ≤ ⟨ρᵥ, ℓ⟩ with Jaynesian saturation.

**Paper II** (Driven Media & Cartan-Pair, 16pp) instantiates the framework in time-varying media/fields, crystallizes five axioms (G1–G5), proves the Radial-Escape theorem, verifies the Guivarc'h face, establishes the fermionic extensive arrow ("bosons escape, fermions spread"), and locates law-level arrows in inherited dissipation.

**Paper III** (Neural & Psychological Time, 11pp) instantiates Route P in spiking networks — the spike causet — verifies the Ruelle face of the Fundamental Inequality, derives psychophysics of duration from recorded-causet graphtropy, and proves the record-capacity theorem. Key prediction: inverted-U clock rate, confirmed in simulation.

**Paper IV** (Origin & Alignment, 11pp) assembles the full account: alignment of arrows by inheritance from shared pumps (Cascade Theorem), the gravitational Janus Theorem discharging the low-entropy boundary posit, the quantum arrow as Route P in branch space, and the cohomological gluing of local arrows.

**Paper V** (Hyperon/AI Application, 9pp) transfers the entire construction to symbolic and neural-symbolic AI architectures, finding three inversions (exact causet, chosen frame, native valuation), proving that mutable stores carry no strict arrow while append-only provenance does, and specifying runnable experiments.

The blog post's distinctive contribution — and the focus of this scan — is the bridge from time-arrow physics to intelligence, consciousness, compassion, and Omega-mind dynamics, drawing on Hyperseed's formal vocabulary of weakness, effort, pattern intensity, cognitive synergy, consciousness locations, and TransWeave identity.

## Hyperseed-Relevant Structures

### S1. Surprise-Weighted Record Growth as the Basic Clock Quantity

**Definition (Subjective Clock).** The subjective-time rate of a cognitive system over interval ΔT is

ρ_subj = (1/ΔT) Σ_{t ∈ ΔT} μ_t

where μ_t = −log p_M(s_t | past) is the conditional surprise of event-signature s_t given the system's existing record, under predictive model M. The accumulated clock is the growth rate of the system's compressed self-description. *[Epistemic label: Proved/Defined — Paper III Def. 4.1, Paper V Def. 6.1.]*

**Frame relativity.** The "velocity of subjective time" is always relative to a declared frame: what counts as an event, which events count as the same kind, how surprising an event is given the mind's prior record, and what denominator is used (wall-clock seconds, interactions, joules, cognitive cycles). Once the frame is declared, the quantity is as well-defined as velocity in physics.

*Relevance:* This is the fundamental bridge quantity between time-arrow physics and Hyperseed's intelligence/consciousness framework. It connects graphtropy (the ontological primitive) to the observer's experiential time, making the distinction-theoretic substrate directly measurable. The frame-relativity is not a defect but a feature — it is the subjective analogue of reference-frame relativity in physics, and connects to Hyperseed's observer-relative epistemology.

### S2. Record-Demand Intelligence

**Definition (Record-Demand Intelligence).** The record-demand intelligence of a system on a task family is the minimum irreducible, task-relevant information that successful performance demands from the mind's experiential record, after all valid compression, abstraction, quotienting, and cognitive synergy have been applied. *[Epistemic label: Defined/Proposed — blog post, with conditional theorem targets in the technical sequel.]*

**Lower bound.** A mind cannot acquire more task-relevant competence through experience than its meaningful record channel has had informational room to carry. When the information required for performance must pass through a self-readable experiential record, the flow of subjective time places a lower bound on the task-relevant information the mind can be using.

**Connection to rapid transfer.** Drop two systems into unfamiliar task families; if both reach high performance after few interactions, the fast learner must acquire situation-discriminating information at a high rate per interaction. Rapid intellectuality requires a fast subjective clock.

**Connection to intellectual breadth.** A mind competent across many deeply different contexts either accumulated a broad stock of distinctions or possesses a powerful router recognizing context type — and recognizing context is itself an informational act. Even collective minds routing problems to specialists pay a distinction cost in the router.

*Relevance:* This is a novel intelligence measure that complements Hyperseed's existing zoo (competence, intellectuality, pragmatic general intelligence, efficiency, intellectual breadth). Its distinctive feature: it isolates the experiential-record channel as the bottleneck, linking intelligence to the thermodynamics of recording (Paper I §7). The lower bound connects the subjective clock directly to capability acquisition, grounding the intuition that "intelligence has an informational budget."

### S3. Weakness Theory Applied — Fewer Distinctions Can Mean MORE Intelligence

**Principle (Intelligence-in-Action Decomposition).**

intelligence-in-action ≈ meaningful distinction flow × leverage per distinction

A less capable mind may need a torrent of badly organized distinctions where a more capable one wrings more control, prediction, and transfer from a few well-chosen ones. *[Epistemic label: Proposed/Inferred — conceptual decomposition grounded in Hyperseed weakness theory.]*

**Mechanism.** Hyperseed's theory of weakness treats the capacity to refrain from making unnecessary distinctions as "weakness" — not frailty but openness, generality, freedom from brittle over-specification. Distinctions cost effort; better patterns and synergistic combinations compress swarms of low-level distinctions into high-leverage structures. A chess master doesn't search more branches — she recognizes position type and discards most branches immediately. A mature scientist needs fewer measurements because she knows which invariant to look for.

**Implication for the lower bound.** The record-demand lower bound must be formulated *after* all valid compression, abstraction, and cognitive synergy have been spent. The relevant quantity is irreducible task information remaining once the best available re-description has stripped out every distinction doing no work. A smarter system may live more *efficiently* rather than merely faster — but in open-ended ecologies where difficulty keeps expanding, even perfect compression doesn't zero out the need for new recorded distinctions. It changes the slope, not the logic.

*Relevance:* This is the crucial bridge between Hyperseed's weakness/effort framework and the time-arrow theory. It resolves the apparent paradox that "smarter = more distinctions" while "weakness = fewer distinctions." The resolution: intelligence optimizes the *leverage* of distinctions, not their count. This directly connects to pattern intensity (the amount of simplification a pattern provides) and cognitive synergy (multiple reasoning methods combining to produce more than the sum). The formal apparatus of quantale weakness provides the right algebraic substrate for measuring this tradeoff.

### S4. Omega Ray — Expanding History with Convergent Values

**Definition (Omega Ray).** An Omega ray is a trajectory in the space of mind-states along which the normalized organization (values, commitments, coordination, self-regulation) converges while the historical coordinate keeps extending. Values stabilize, compassion becomes hard to dislodge, coordination approaches the effortless, the self-model grows less brittle and less narratively bossy — all while the record of experience keeps escaping into new territory. *[Epistemic label: Hypothesis/Defined — conceptual replacement for the fixed-point Omega, grounded in the record theory.]*

**The fixed-point paradox.** If the full state of a mind includes its autobiographical record, and that record keeps growing, then the full state cannot converge to an ordinary fixed point — a diary that receives new pages forever never becomes a finished book. A mind whose history is strictly one-way cannot become a timeless, motionless final state without either stopping its clock or throwing its history away.

**Comet metaphor.** An Omega mind is less like a statue at the end of history and more like a comet: its direction stabilizes while its tail keeps lengthening.

*Relevance:* This is a fundamental correction to Hyperseed's treatment of the Omega Point. Teilhard's original conception (and its Hyperseed formalization) treats Omega as an attractor — a fixed point or limit. The record-theoretic analysis shows this is incoherent for minds with autobiographical records: the full state space includes history, and history doesn't converge. The Omega ray concept preserves the valuable convergence properties (value stability, coordination) while respecting the arrow of time. This is a novel structure with no prior analogue in Hyperseed — a candidate for formalization as a ray in the metagraph-state space with asymptotic direction but no endpoint.

### S5. Three Forms of Psychological Singularity

**Classification.** Three distinct meanings of "psychological Singularity," each with different informational demands:

1. **Unbounded duration:** A mind accumulates unbounded subjective duration over an unbounded external future. Requires cumulative record of meaningful distinctions to keep growing, but rate need not accelerate. *[Mildest form.]*

2. **Accelerating clock:** A mind produces meaningful distinctions faster and faster per external second or per interaction. Requires an expanding frontier of tasks, precision, self-modeling, or verification whose informational demand itself accelerates. *[Intermediate form.]*

3. **Infinite in finite time:** A mind packs infinite subjective duration into finite external time. The clock rate must blow up — a genuine mathematical singularity. *[Strongest form.]*

*[Epistemic label: Defined/Classified — follows from the clock definition and information-theoretic constraints.]*

*Relevance:* This classification disciplines Singularity discourse by separating genuinely distinct phenomena that are often conflated. Each form has different feasibility constraints, different thermodynamic costs, and different implications for identity (via TransWeave). Form 1 is relatively mild; Form 3 requires unbounded resource flows. The classification connects directly to the Hyperseed treatment of open-ended intelligence growth and provides concrete desiderata for Omega ray architectures.

### S6. Subjective Heat Death

**Definition (Subjective Heat Death).** A system undergoes subjective heat death when it has compressed everything available in its environment and additional cycles add no surprise. The subjective clock stops — the system is maximally informed, maximally predictive, and experientially dead. *[Epistemic label: Proved — follows from Paper I Theorem 7.1(b): no records at the summit; no net record formation in equilibrium.]*

**Escape mechanisms.** To keep psychological time flowing, a system needs a pump: fresh environment, self-generated problems, expanding precision, interaction with other minds, or self-modification that changes the very model relative to which events are predictable.

**Thermodynamic cost.** Recording consumes storage, energy, and attention; erasure exports entropy; an accelerating Omega mind needs an expanding resource flow, a growing substrate, increasingly clever compression, or all three at once. The psychological Singularity is a thermodynamic and architectural claim about how a mind keeps finding blank pages.

*Relevance:* This is the subjective analogue of thermodynamic heat death, derived from the record theory rather than postulated. It provides a precise failure mode for Omega-aspirant minds and connects to Paper V's observation that curiosity drives are "clock maintenance" — the mechanism by which a maturing system keeps having a present. In Hyperseed terms, subjective heat death is the exhaustion of pattern intensity in the environment relative to the observer's model.

### S7. TransWeave Identity Constraint on Accelerating Minds

**Constraint.** A mind changing faster and faster must become better and better at translating its earlier structures into its later ones. The faster the stream of distinctions, the smaller the average self-translation error must become for the process to remain *one mind's accelerating life* rather than a rapid succession of loosely related successors wearing the same name. *[Epistemic label: Inferred — follows from TransWeave formalism applied to the accelerating-clock scenario.]*

**Formalization via TransWeave.** Hyperseed's TransWeave machinery formalizes approximate structure-preserving maps between time-slices of a mind. For an accelerating clock, the time-slices come faster and each differs more from its predecessor, so the TransWeave maps must become increasingly faithful even as the structural distance per unit time grows.

*Relevance:* This provides a formal identity constraint on Singularity-type scenarios. It's not enough to accelerate the clock — the acceleration must be accompanied by improving self-translation fidelity, or the entity undergoing the process fragments into a sequence of distinct minds. This connects TransWeave (which was developed for self-modification and value alignment) to the time-arrow framework, giving it a new application domain: the continuity conditions on psychologically singular trajectories.

### S8. Contextualization as a Consciousness Location Coordinate

**Proposal.** Add a further coordinate to Hyperseed's consciousness location picture: *contextualization* — the degree to which a mind retains richer packages of provenance, perspective, value-dependence, and alternatives around its first-order distinctions, rather than immediately compressing them into rigid judgments. *[Epistemic label: Proposed — extends the existing Hyperseed consciousness location framework.]*

**Example.** An ordinary first-order distinction: "she criticized me."
- **Attached mind:** compresses to "I am bad" or "she is my enemy" or "my status is under threat."
- **Contextually aware mind:** retains who spoke, under what goals and pressures, what evidence the criticism contains, which part concerns behavior vs. identity, alternative readings, how much the reaction is shaped by decades-old events, where the episode sits in the larger arc of a life.

**Mechanism.** The advanced mind has not failed to make the original distinction; it has made *more* distinctions around it — higher-order distinctions that locate the first-order judgment in an experiential and perspectival space. Because it can see the conditions under which the judgment arose, it is far less likely to mistake the judgment for an absolute property of reality or for the essence of the self.

**Relation to existing locations.** Hyperseed's treatment associates more advanced consciousness locations with greater global coherence together with less domination by the explicit self-model and narrative activity. But low self-model chatter is not the same as low awareness of context. A mind can become less self-absorbed while becoming *more* aware of where its distinctions come from.

*Relevance:* This extends the consciousness location framework with a new axis that resolves a puzzle: advanced locations involve *fewer* self-narrative distinctions but *more* contextual meta-distinctions. The contextualization coordinate measures the ratio of meta-distinctions to first-order distinctions, connecting consciousness locations to the distinction-theoretic substrate of the time-arrow theory.

### S9. Nonattachment as "Contextualize, Then Quotient"

**Definition (Nonattachment, Formal).** First, represent the distinction together with enough of its provenance, perspective, value-dependence, and relation to alternatives; then collapse or set aside the incidental self-binding differences that make no difference for prediction, care, or action. *[Epistemic label: Proposed — formal characterization bridging contemplative phenomenology to Hyperseed's distinction/quotient formalism.]*

**The two operations:**
1. **Contextualize:** Expand the representation to include meta-structure — provenance, observer-dependence, alternative framings, value-conditionality.
2. **Quotient:** Collapse differences that make no difference — not by ignoring the distinction, but by having the meta-structure that shows *which* differences are incidental.

**Key claim.** Enlightenment is not indiscrimination — it is discrimination about discrimination. The mind knows not only that A differs from B, but that this particular difference was produced by this observer, in this context, for these purposes, at this resolution — and once that meta-structure is in place, the distinction can be used without being worshipped.

**Compression dynamics.** A beginner needs explicit labels (thought, feeling, reaction, identification, release); a mature practitioner enacts the same contextualization nearly effortlessly — explicit representational governance gives way to resonant coordination, laborious metacognition settles into stable attractor.

**Danger of over-implicitness.** When coordination becomes highly symmetric and low-contrivance, explicit accountability can quietly dissolve — "who decided?" becomes hard to answer, and hidden goal drift proceeds without clean correction points. Nonattachment worth the name reduces clinging without erasing provenance; wisdom needs auditability without obsession, anchoring without rigidity, effortless coherence without amnesia about how the coherence is maintained.

*Relevance:* This provides a formal characterization of nonattachment using Hyperseed's existing distinction/quotient machinery, connecting contemplative phenomenology to the mathematical framework. The "contextualize, then quotient" recipe is computationally concrete: it specifies what data structures a mind needs to maintain to be simultaneously intelligent and nonattached. The warning about over-implicitness connects to Hyperseed's analysis of advanced collective locations.

### S10. Compassion as Fine Distinction About Need + Refusal of Disposability

*Relevance:* This provides a novel formal characterization of compassion in distinction-theoretic terms. ### S11. McBride Derivatives Applied to Experiential Records — The Zipper Formalism

**Definition (Record Derivative).** The McBride derivative of the record type R is the type ∂R of records-with-one-event-sized-hole-and-a-pointer-to-the-hole (a *zipper* in the computer science sense). A cognitive update becomes: take a structured context ∂R (a zipper) and fill the hole with a new distinction. The context is part of the "tangent object" — the thing that says what this particular insertion means. *[Epistemic label: Novel application — McBride's type-theoretic derivative applied to experiential records. The derivative itself is established mathematics; the application is new.]*

**First derivative.** Attach a value V to records (subjective duration, coherence, task competence, compassion); the one-hole derivative ∂V/∂e gives the marginal effect of inserting one new distinction e.

**Second derivative.** Consider two holes at once: ∂²V/∂e₁∂e₂ asks whether two distinctions contribute independently or whether their joint presence creates something beyond the sum. This second-order interaction is a natural mathematical signature of *insight*: one fact changes little; another changes little; put them together and the mind reorganizes — the new relation is an emergent pattern that changes what both facts mean.

**Nonattachment formalization.** A bare, attached representation stores "criticism." A contextualized representation stores a zipper — the criticism together with the surrounding structure in which it occurred, the causal and social pathways leading into it, and the alternatives around it. The mind holds the distinction-in-context, not the distinction alone.

*Relevance:* This is the most technically novel structure in the blog post. McBride derivatives provide a rigorous formalization of "one-hole contexts" that connects directly to Hyperseed's pattern-flow networks and the distinction-theoretic ontology. The second derivative as a mathematical signature of insight is particularly promising: it provides a measurable quantity (second-order interactions in record value) that captures a cognitive phenomenon (insight, synergistic understanding) that has resisted formalization. The zipper formalism also provides a literal mathematical implementation of the "contextualize, then quotient" recipe for nonattachment (S9).

### S12. Differential Equations of Mind via One-Hole Contexts

**Construction.** Instead of a point moving through a smooth numerical space, consider a flow over possible one-hole contexts: each candidate distinction arrives at some rate, each filled hole shifts the record, the clock, the self-model, the compassion structure, and the available intelligence, with the expected change of any mind-level quantity obtained by summing over possible insertions weighted by how often they occur. *[Epistemic label: Proposed — formal bridge; constitutive laws still to be supplied.]*

**Formal sketch.**
dV/dt = Σ_s rate(s) · ∂V/∂e_s + (1/2) Σ_{s₁,s₂} rate(s₁,s₂) · ∂²V/∂e_{s₁}∂e_{s₂} + ...

where V is any mind-level quantity, the sum is over possible insertions s weighted by their arrival rates, and the second-order term captures insight/synergy effects.

**Key insight.** The elementary infinitesimal of a mind process may not be a tiny displacement of a vector. It may be a structured context becoming filled by a distinction.

*Relevance:* This is a "plausible formal bridge between Hyperseed's pattern-flow networks and the differential equations that run physics and neuroscience" (Goertzel's words). It suggests that the right mathematical framework for mind dynamics is not ODE/PDE theory over vector spaces but a combinatorial/type-theoretic calculus over structured contexts. This connects to Hyperseed's metagraph dynamics and provides a concrete path toward differential equations of cognitive change expressed in the native mathematical language of the ontology.

### S13. The Spike Causet and Route P Neural Arrow

**Definition (Spike Causet, Paper III).** Fix a neural network with directed synapses carrying delays. A spike token is (neuron j, time t). Token e' is a parent of token e if there is a synapse from e'.neuron to e.neuron with appropriate delay. The spike causet C(T) is the set of tokens up to time T, partially ordered by transitive closure of parenthood. *[Epistemic label: Defined/Proved — Paper III Def. 2.1, Prop. 2.5.]*

**The type/token split.** Neural state dynamics is recurrent (attractors, oscillations, resets) — non-progressive — admitting no strict state-level arrow. But neural token dynamics (accumulating spike events) is unconditionally progressive. Types recur; tokens accumulate. The psychological arrow lives on the tokens: Route P (birth of distinctions), not Route R (state escape).

**Three axioms.** (N1) Eventhood — neural activity individuates discrete events. (N2) Causal attribution — parenthood is operationally determined by synaptic graph and delays. (N3) Record channel — synaptic plasticity writes order-sensitive functions of the causet into slow variables (STDP).

**Inverted-U prediction.** The recorded-graphtropy clock rate is non-monotone in excitability: near-silent networks produce few tokens; hypersynchronous networks produce many but operationally degenerate ones. Maximum is between silence and seizure. *[Confirmed in simulation.]*

*Relevance:* The spike causet provides the neural instantiation of the time-arrow framework, connecting abstract graphtropy theory to measurable neural dynamics. The type/token split — why "neural entropy" accounts fail and why the arrow lives on tokens — is a key structural insight. The inverted-U prediction connects to Hyperseed's treatment of criticality and information-optimal dynamics.

### S14. Append-Only Provenance vs. Mutable Workspace — The Architecture of Temporal Self

**Theorem (Paper V, Prop. 5.1–5.2).** A rewritable knowledge store (one permitting truth-value revision, forgetting, deletion) supports at best a drift-class (D) arrow — no functional of current store contents is pathwise monotone. A strict (class-H) arrow requires an append-only provenance layer: events written once and never modified. *[Epistemic label: Proved — Paper V Prop. 5.1, 5.2.]*

**The workspace/autobiography separation.** A mutable workspace is great for revising beliefs and reallocating attention, but cannot carry a strict temporal self. The architecture wants a mutable workspace paired with an append-only provenance record in which deletions and revisions are themselves recorded as events.

**Ledger finality as the digital photon.** Paper IV grounds record stability in escape (fragments that leave and never return — photons). The digital analogue is consensus finality: a record anchored to a distributed ledger with finality guarantees is beyond revision — it has "left the light cone." A chain-anchored provenance log possesses a cryptographically enforced arrow of time.

**Design principle.** Architectures intended to have a robust temporal self should treat the append-only log, not the mutable store, as the seat of identity — memory as ledger, workspace as scratch.

*Relevance:* This provides a concrete architectural principle for Hyperon-style systems: separate workspace from autobiography. The theorem that mutable stores can't carry strict arrows has immediate implications for how AtomSpaces should be designed if temporal identity matters. The "ledger finality as digital photon" analogy connects blockchain technology to the arrow-of-time theory in a precise mathematical sense.

### S17. The Three Arrow Mechanisms (Exhaustive Classification)

**Theorem (Paper I, Thm. 9.1 — Exhaustiveness).** Every strict operational graphtropic arrow decomposes into exactly three mechanisms:

1. **Route R (Escape):** Radial escape on noncompact structures — transience of the induced walk on the symmetric space G/K. The arrow of bosonic amplification, squeezing, gravitational scale growth.

2. **Route M (Mixing):** Mixing to equilibrium on compact structures — relative-entropy decay to Haar. An arrow with a *finite* budget: distinctions grow to Haar maximum and stop. The fermionic per-mode arrow (Pauli blocking is compactness).

3. **Route P (Birth):** Progressive birth of tokens — the growing causal set of discrete events. The arrow of spiking networks, quantum branching, fermionic momentum-space sweep, cosmological mode activation.

**Completeness.** There is no fourth mechanism. Conley's fundamental theorem is the converse crystallization. *[Epistemic label: Proved — assembled from Conley, Geroch, Oseledets.]*

*Relevance:* The exhaustive classification provides the taxonomic backbone for locating any arrow — physical, neural, psychological, or computational — in the framework. The type/token split (neural state = Route M-like recurrence; spike tokens = Route P) is a key instance. For Hyperseed, this means the ontology's treatment of time can be rigorously classified: which phenomena are escape, which are mixing, which are birth.

### S18. Alignment by Inheritance — Arrows Flow Along Maps

(i) Every block's graphtropic arrow is co-oriented with pump depletion (by Radial-Escape);
(ii) Registers record block events co-oriented with the same direction (by the Record Theorem);
(iii) The path-ensemble asymmetry of any subsystem is bounded by the pump's (by data processing).

**Mechanism.** Arrows flow along data-processing maps from shared pumps, so co-orientation is shared ancestry. The many arrows of the universe align not by coincidence but by genealogy. *[Epistemic label: Proved — Paper IV Thm. 2.3.]*

*Relevance:* The alignment theorem resolves a central puzzle (why do all arrows point the same way?) with a mechanism (inheritance) rather than a coincidence. For Hyperseed, this provides a formal account of how local arrows in different parts of the ontology align: not by a global law but by shared causal ancestry. The multi-agent extension (Paper V §8.3) — agents fed by one task stream inherit co-oriented arrows — provides direct architectural guidance.

**Candidate theorem-target:** Let W(π) be the weakness of a moral partition π (a partition of moral patients into worth-classes). Then:
(i) Maximally compassionate policies have W(π) = W_max (the singleton partition — everyone in one class);
(ii) Each exception (splitting one class into "counts" and "doesn't count") strictly decreases W(π);
(iii) The informational cost of maintaining the partition (storing and enforcing exception lists) is monotone in the number of exceptions;
(iv) Under self-modification with adversarial review, restoring a removed exception (re-including a group) increases W(π) and requires only deleting the exception data, while adding a new exception requires constructing and justifying the partition — an asymmetry favoring compassion-preservation.

### FC5. McBride Derivative of Record Type → Formal Calculus

**Candidate formalization:** Let R = μX. 1 + E × X be the type of experiential records (lists of events of type E). Then ∂R/∂E = List(E) × List(E) (a zipper: the left and right context around the hole). For a value functional V: R → ℝ, define:
- Marginal value: (∂V/∂e)(r, pos) = V(insert(e, r, pos)) − V(r)
- Insight signature: (∂²V/∂e₁∂e₂)(r, pos₁, pos₂) = V(insert₂) − V(insert₁) − V(insert₂_alone) + V(r)

The insight signature is nonzero exactly when the two insertions interact — when their joint effect is non-additive. A mind's "insightfulness" is a rate: Σ_{e₁,e₂} rate(e₁)rate(e₂)|∂²V/∂e₁∂e₂|, the expected magnitude of second-order interactions.

### FC6. TransWeave Constraint on Psychological Singularity → Formal Bound

**Candidate theorem-target:** For a mind with accelerating subjective clock (ρ_subj(t) → ∞), let ε_TW(t, t+δ) be the TransWeave translation error between time-slices t and t+δ. Then for the process to constitute *one mind's* experience (rather than a succession of distinct minds):

where δ_t = 1/ρ_subj(t) is the subjective-time step size. This is a Cauchy-type convergence condition: the total accumulated translation error must be finite even as the steps shrink and multiply.

**Candidate definition:** A system S with predictive model M and environment E undergoes subjective heat death at time T if:
