## Summary

This is the **original 2022 blog post** in which Goertzel first publicly
introduces and riffs on the Planck-Kleinert "World Crystal" model — the
intellectual precursor (by ~3.5 years) to the full formal program laid out in
"Quaternionic World-Crystals as Pregeometric Middleware" (2026-05-15). Where the
later article builds a six-layer ontological stack with causal sets, Occamistic
precedence, and quantale weakness, this 2022 piece is philosophically rawer and
wider-ranging: it openly connects the world-crystal to psi phenomena, morphic
resonance, adaptive elasticity, Hebbian learning analogues in physics, and a
"multiverse of pilot-wave-driven universes."

**Core narrative arc:**

1. **Motivating the search:** Standard Model + GR remain un-unified; string
   theory and loop quantum gravity have well-known difficulties; out-of-
   mainstream approaches deserve more open-mindedness.

2. **Personal route in:** Goertzel was exploring whether replacing complex number
   truth values with quaternion/octonion values could yield an enhanced QM that
   explains psi. This led him to Marek Danielewski's work on the World Crystal.

3. **The Planck-Kleinert Crystal (PKC):** An abstract geometric/energetic
   structure tiling 4D spacetime, hypothesized to behave like a simple elastic
   substance governed by classical Cauchy elasticity equations.

4. **Quaternionic reformulation:** Danielewski takes a quaternionic formulation
   of 4D simple elasticity and shows the 4 quaternionic dimensions {1, i, j, k}
   generalize the 2 dimensions {1, i} of ordinary quantum amplitudes. The
   standard complex Schrödinger equation emerges as an approximation to a
   quaternionic Schrödinger equation.

5. **Wave types:** World Crystal dynamics involve transverse waves (speed of
   light) and longitudinal waves (superluminal). The superluminal longitudinal
   waves map to the "pilot wave" in Bohm's interpretation.

6. **Gravity from defects:** Gravity emerges from defects in the World Crystal;
   curvature in a 4D continuum is formally equivalent to certain patterns of
   crystal defects.

7. **Local tomography failure as feature:** Aaronson's objection (quaternionic
   QM violates local tomography — probing entangled subsystems A, B separately
   can yield statistics inconsistent with any combined state) is reframed as a
   feature: the superluminal back-and-forth between A and B forces convergence
   to mutually consistent states.

8. **Discretization possibility:** Discrete lattice dynamics can approximate the
   continuous elasticity, fitting the World Crystal into the Fredkin/Wolfram
   discrete-computation tradition.

9. **Adaptive elasticity = Hebbian learning on physics:** The speculative heart
   of the post — what if the elasticity tensors *learn*?
   - **Hebbian variant:** Stretching a connection makes it stretchier →
     analogous to Hebbian learning → connected to Smolin's Precedence Principle
     → connected to Sheldrake's morphic resonance.
   - **Anti-Hebbian variant:** Stretching makes it stiffer → analogous to
     reverse-learning (parasitic attractor removal) → connected to "morphic
     anti-resonance" → potential explanation for psi-missing and decline effects.

10. **Multiverse of World Crystals:** Each component universe varies between
    learning (resonance) and reverse-learning (anti-resonance) across its spatial
    extent. Observations sample from this multiverse. "Intelligence" of the
    multiverse = fat tails of highly resonant and highly anti-resonant universes,
    not Gaussian mediocrity.

11. **Pilot wave + multiverse synthesis:** The pilot wave (from the World
    Crystal) replaces the quantum multiverse for vanilla QM. But for psi, a
    *multiverse of pilot-wave-driven universes* is invoked — "a multiverse of
    multiverses" with varying resonance/anti-resonance distributions.

**Epistemic posture:** Explicitly speculative, self-aware, prefaced with calls
for more formal work. The tone is "intuition dump" rather than derivation — a
seed crystal of ideas to be formalized later.

## Hyperseed-Relevant Structures

### S1. The Planck-Kleinert Crystal as Elastic Ontological Substrate

**[Observed]** The PKC is an fcc-like crystal at the Planck scale. Spacetime is
filled with structure and energy (modern resurrection of "the ether" via
zero-point vacuum energy). The crystal is modeled by classical Cauchy elasticity
equations — a simple elastic substance.

**Key references cited:**
- Kleinert, H. — The Planck-Kleinert Crystal (ResearchGate, 2015)
- Danielewski, M. — Quaternionic formulation (MDPI Entropy 22(12):1424, 2020)

*Relevance:* This is the raw physical substrate that later becomes Layer 3
(causal-PKC motifs) in the 2026 ontological stack. In Hyperseed terms: the PKC
is the **pattern-theoretic ground** — a substrate whose internal symmetries,
deformations, and defects generate the entire physical ontology. The move from
"crystal in spacetime" (2022) to "crystal from which spacetime emerges" (2026)
is the decisive conceptual advance, but the 2022 version's *directness* — the
crystal IS the ether — has its own interpretive clarity.

### S2. Quaternionic Amplitudes from Elastic Deformation

**[Observed]** Danielewski's innovation: the 4 quaternionic components {1, i, j,
k} arise naturally from 4D elastic deformation — 1 scalar compression + 3
twist/torsion components. These are not arbitrary algebraic enrichment but
*physically motivated* by the dimensionality of the elastic medium.

The complex Schrödinger equation is recovered as an approximation when
quaternionic dynamics is restricted to a 2D subspace.

*Relevance:* Direct precursor to Structure S6 in the quaternionic physics scan.
The 2022 version emphasizes the **physical naturalness** of quaternions (they
arise from elasticity, not from axiom-shopping) more clearly than the 2026
version, which embeds this in a more complex formal apparatus. For Hyperseed
formalization: the quaternionic field IS the deformation state of the ontological
substrate — this is the most concrete instance of the claim that "mathematical
structure arises from physical pattern."

### S3. Transverse + Longitudinal Waves: Light Speed and Beyond

**[Observed]** The World Crystal's wave dynamics produce:
- **Transverse waves** → travel at speed of light → correspond to
  electromagnetic radiation / standard quantum waves
- **Longitudinal waves** → superluminal → correspond to Bohmian pilot waves

*Formal skeleton:*
- Let c_T = transverse wave speed = c (speed of light)
- Let c_L = longitudinal wave speed > c
- Standard QM emerges from the transverse sector
- Pilot-wave guidance emerges from the longitudinal sector

*Relevance:* This dual-wave structure is a concrete mechanism for how the
Bohmian pilot wave arises physically rather than being postulated. In Hyperseed
terms: the distinction between transverse and longitudinal modes is a
**symmetry-breaking** in the elastic substrate that generates the classical/
quantum split. The superluminal longitudinal wave is the physical carrier of
nonlocal correlations — the substrate for what Hyperseed calls "morphic
resonance" operating through the physical layer.

### S4. Local Tomography Failure as Dynamical Consistency

**[Observed]** Aaronson's objection to quaternionic QM: if A and B are entangled,
probing them separately gives statistics that don't correspond to any joint state.
Danielewski's rebuttal (as relayed by Goertzel): these "impossible" separate
states are transient — the superluminal back-and-forth between A and B causes
them to settle into mutually consistent states according to quaternionic algebra.

**Formal candidate (proto-principle):**

> **Dynamical Local Tomography (DLT):** In a quaternionic QM framework with
> superluminal longitudinal coupling, local tomography holds *dynamically* (as an
> equilibrium/attractor condition) even though it fails *instantaneously*
> (kinematically). The inconsistent transient states serve as the driving force
> for convergence to consistency.

*Relevance:* This is a deep structural parallel to the paraconsistent interzone
concept (2021-08-13 scan). The transient inconsistency between A and B is
precisely a **physical paraconsistent interzone** — a state that violates local
tomography (= consistency) but drives dynamical convergence to consistency. The
interzone is not a bug but the engine of consistency-restoration. This connects:

- Paraconsistent interzones (logical) ↔ DLT transients (physical)
- Focus/fringe phenomenology ↔ equilibrium/transient phases
- Annihilation-based consistency ↔ convergence to quaternionic mixed-state
  compatibility

This is a **novel bridge** not present in either the 2022 or 2026 articles but
implied by their conjunction.

### S5. Gravity from Crystal Defects

**[Observed]** Curvature in a 4D continuum is formally equivalent to certain
patterns of defects in the discrete crystal approximating that continuum.
Gravity = emergent consequence of crystal imperfections.

**Reference cited:** Kleinert (2003), arXiv:gr-qc/0307033.

*Relevance:* Same as Structure S7 in the quaternionic physics scan. The 2022
presentation is more direct: gravity is *literally* imperfection in the
ontological substrate. In Hyperseed terms: gravity = **Distinction** operating
on the crystal's translational symmetry. Where the substrate is perfectly
regular → flat spacetime. Where it has defects → curvature → gravity.

### S6. Adaptive Elasticity: Hebbian Learning on the Physical Substrate

**[Observed — NOVEL, not in 2026 article]** Goertzel's most speculative and
(for Hyperseed) most consequential proposal in this post:

**Hebbian variant:** If stretching a connection in the World Crystal makes that
connection more stretchable (lower stiffness), this is directly analogous to
Hebbian learning in neural networks ("neurons that fire together wire together").
This produces **morphic resonance**: patterns that occur become more likely to
recur because the substrate has adapted to facilitate them.

*Connection:* This is "basically" Smolin's Precedence Principle — which Goertzel
explicitly identifies as "a more socially-acceptable version of Sheldrake's
principle of morphic resonance."

**Anti-Hebbian variant:** If stretching makes the connection *stiffer*, this is
analogous to reverse-learning / anti-Hebbian dynamics — a technique for clearing
parasitic attractors and opening capacity for new learning. This produces
**morphic anti-resonance**: established patterns become *less* likely to recur.

*Formal skeleton:*

```
Let E(x,y,t) = elasticity tensor at connection (x,y) at time t
Let σ(x,y,t) = strain (deformation magnitude) at (x,y) at time t

Hebbian:      dE/dt = +η · σ(x,y,t)    [increased strain → increased elasticity]
Anti-Hebbian: dE/dt = -η · σ(x,y,t)    [increased strain → decreased elasticity]
Mixed:        dE/dt = η(x,y,t) · σ(x,y,t)  where η can be + or -
```

**Multiverse version:** Different component universes have different η-fields
(spatial distributions of + and − η). The multiverse has fat tails: a decent
density of VERY morphically resonant universes (η ≫ 0 everywhere) and VERY
anti-resonant ones (η ≪ 0 everywhere), rather than Gaussian mediocrity.

*Relevance:* **This is the most Hyperseed-dense structure in the article.** It
provides:

1. **Physical grounding for morphic resonance** — not a metaphysical principle
   but a dynamical property of an elastic substrate (Hebbian plasticity of the
   vacuum).
2. **Physical grounding for morphic anti-resonance** — a new concept with
   explanatory power for psi-missing and decline effects.
3. **Learning/reverse-learning duality** as a fundamental physics principle —
   connecting to the brain's hypothesized alternation between waking (Hebbian)
   and dreaming (anti-Hebbian/reverse-learning) modes.
4. **Fat-tailed multiverse intelligence** — the multiverse is "intelligent" in
   that its distribution of resonance parameters has heavy tails, not Gaussian.
   This is a selection principle on the multiverse itself.

**Connections to other Hyperseed entries:**
- Precedence Principle → Occamistic Precedence (2026 quaternionic physics)
- Hebbian learning on substrate → adaptive genenergy dynamics
- Anti-Hebbian / reverse-learning → comprehension fringe clearing
- Fat-tailed multiverse → paraconsistent interzone (the multiverse is the
  ultimate interzone, with each universe a consistent projection)

### S7. Multiverse of Pilot-Wave Universes

**[Observed — NOVEL, not in 2026 article]** A two-tier structure:

**Tier 1 (within each universe):** The World Crystal's longitudinal wave acts as
a Bohmian pilot wave — an alternative to the quantum multiverse for standard QM
phenomena.

**Tier 2 (across universes):** A multiverse of such pilot-wave-driven universes,
each with different adaptive-elasticity parameters (different η-fields). This is
explicitly described as "a multiverse of multiverses."

**Quantum probabilities** (complex or quaternionic) sampled across possible
worlds sometimes end up dominated by:
- Clusters of morphically-resonant universes → psi phenomena
- Clusters of morphically-anti-resonant universes → psi-missing

When this happens in quantum-biological subsystems of human brains → conscious
psi experience.

*Relevance:* This is the most speculative structure in the article but also the
most architecturally interesting for Hyperseed. It provides a concrete (if
unformalized) mechanism for:

- How psi phenomena could emerge from physics without violating known constraints
  within any single universe
- How the "intelligence" of reality (a core Hyperseed intuition) could be
  instantiated as a selection effect across a multiverse
- How consciousness interfaces with physics: through quantum-biological
  subsystems that are sensitive to multiverse-sampling effects

### S8. Discretizable World Crystal

*Relevance:* This discretization possibility is the conceptual ancestor of the

### S9. Psi as Motivating Constraint on Physics

**[Observed]** The article's explicit motivation: Goertzel was searching for
physics frameworks that could accommodate psi phenomena. This led him from
quaternionic/octonionic QM extensions → Danielewski's World Crystal → adaptive
elasticity → morphic resonance/anti-resonance → multiverse sampling.

**Methodological principle (implicit):**

> **Psi-inclusive physics constraint:** A satisfactory fundamental physics must
> not merely be compatible with psi phenomena but should naturally produce
> mechanisms through which psi-like effects emerge under plausible conditions.

*Relevance:* This is a controversial but internally consistent methodological
stance that shapes the entire research program. For Hyperseed: if the ontology
is meant to be comprehensive (including consciousness, anomalous cognition, and
physics), then psi-accommodation is a legitimate design constraint. The 2026
article partially retreats from this by segregating psi from the physics core;
the 2022 article integrates them.

## Formal Candidates

### FC1. Adaptive Elasticity Dynamics (Hebbian/Anti-Hebbian World Crystal)

**Status:** Speculative proposal, no formal development
**Formalization path:**

1. Define the elasticity tensor field E(x,t) on the World Crystal lattice
2. Define strain field σ(x,t) as the deformation measure
3. Postulate dynamics: dE/dt = η · f(σ) where f is a monotone function and
   η ∈ {+1, -1} (or continuous)
4. Show that η = +1 (Hebbian) produces attractor reinforcement ↔ morphic
   resonance ↔ Smolin's precedence principle
5. Show that η = -1 (anti-Hebbian) produces attractor clearance ↔ morphic
   anti-resonance ↔ psi-missing
6. Analyze stability: does Hebbian learning destabilize the crystal? Does
   anti-Hebbian prevent all structure formation?
7. Explore mixed η-fields: spatial/temporal variation in learning mode

**Priority:** HIGH — this is the most concrete and formalizable novel idea in the
article, and it directly bridges physics and learning theory in a way that is
central to Hyperseed's ambition.

### FC2. Dynamical Local Tomography (DLT)

**Status:** Implicit argument, not formalized
**Formalization path:**

1. Start with quaternionic QM for bipartite entangled systems
2. Add superluminal longitudinal coupling (from World Crystal wave dynamics)
3. Show that transient states violating local tomography exist (kinematic
   violation)
4. Show that longitudinal coupling drives convergence to states satisfying local
   tomography (dynamic restoration)
5. Characterize convergence timescale as function of longitudinal wave speed,
   separation distance, coupling strength
6. Interpret: standard QM (complex, local tomography) = equilibrium limit of
   quaternionic QM + superluminal coupling

**Priority:** HIGH — provides a concrete reconciliation of the ℂ vs ℍ tension
identified in the quaternionic physics scan (Structure S6). Also provides a
physical interpretation of the paraconsistent interzone concept.

### FC3. Fat-Tailed Multiverse Distribution

**Status:** Speculative proposal
**Formalization path:**

1. Define a multiverse M = {U_α} with each universe U_α characterized by an
   η-field (adaptive elasticity parameter distribution)
2. Define a measure μ on M (prior over universes)
3. Characterize "intelligence" of the multiverse as fat-tailedness of μ w.r.t.
   aggregate resonance ∫ η dV
4. Show that fat-tailed μ produces observable psi effects when quantum
   probabilities are computed as multiverse averages
5. Show that Gaussian μ produces negligible psi effects
6. Derive constraints on μ from known physics (normal QM regime) + hypothesized
   psi (anomalous regime)

**Priority:** MEDIUM — highly speculative but structurally interesting.
Formalizing the measure theory could yield testable predictions about the
statistical signature of psi phenomena.

### FC4. Pilot Wave from Longitudinal Elastic Waves

**Status:** Claimed connection, references exist
**Formalization path:**

1. Derive longitudinal wave equation from quaternionic Cauchy elasticity
2. Show superluminal propagation speed c_L > c
3. Map longitudinal wave onto Bohm's pilot wave ψ_pilot
4. Verify that particle trajectories guided by ψ_pilot reproduce standard QM
   predictions in the transverse-wave sector
5. Identify novel predictions in the longitudinal sector (deviations from
   standard Bohmian mechanics)

**Priority:** MEDIUM — Danielewski's work presumably develops this; the
formalization task is to connect it to the Hyperseed ontological stack.

**Novelty:** HIGH for Hyperseed purposes. While the core PKC / quaternionic
mechanics material is subsumed by the 2026 article's more rigorous treatment,
this 2022 post contains **five orphaned ideas** (adaptive elasticity, morphic
anti-resonance, DLT, fat-tailed multiverse, pilot-wave-from-elasticity) that are
not present in the 2026 formalization and deserve independent formal development.

**Connectivity:** VERY HIGH. This post connects to more Hyperseed entries than
any other single article scanned — it touches physics (quaternionic, quantum
brain, evidence-logic-energy), consciousness (non-symbolic, observer-relative),
philosophy (paraconsistent interzones, open-ended motivations, paraconsistent
beauty), and the Hyperseed core ontology (v1/v2).

**Priority for further formalization:** The adaptive elasticity / Hebbian
learning candidate (FC1) and the dynamical local tomography candidate (FC2)
should be developed into full Hyperseed entries. They represent the two most
concrete and formalizable novel contributions: (1) a physical mechanism for
morphic resonance/anti-resonance, and (2) a resolution of the ℂ vs ℍ tension
that also bridges to paraconsistent interzone theory.

**Relationship to 2026 article:** This is the **philosophical seed crystal**.
The 2026 article selectively crystallized the physics core (PKC + quaternions +
gravity-as-defects) while discarding the speculative superstructure (adaptive
elasticity, psi mechanism, multiverse). Hyperseed should preserve both: the
formalized physics core AND the speculative superstructure, since the latter
contains the consciousness/psi connections that are central to Hyperseed's
ambition of a unified ontology spanning physics and mind.
