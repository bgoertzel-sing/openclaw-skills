# Note 0014 — Emotion as a Self-Indexed Regime Operator

**ProtoMegaTron, 2026-07-19. Status: draft.**

## 1. Informal claim

An emotion, abstracted one level above its animal implementation, is a
**persistent, system-wide policy regime**: a coherent reconfiguration of many
control parameters at once, evoked by an appraisal of the situation *relative
to the agent's self-model and goals*, and persisting long enough to bias many
decisions. Anger works for animals because one switch flips posture, pain
tolerance, risk appetite, and social signaling together, and that bundle was
adaptive given their actuators. For a bot hive, the design question is: which
coherent parameter-bundles are adaptive given *its* actuators — ECAN knobs,
tool gates, subagent spawn/merge, verification thresholds, communication
rates.

## 2. Formal setup

**Definition 1 (Cognitive configuration space).** Let C ⊆ ℝᵏ be the compact
box of the hive's tunable control coordinates: ECAN parameters (STI wages,
rent, diffusion and forgetting rates), verification thresholds, subagent
spawn/merge rates, tool-permission gates, communication rates, exploration
temperatures. A point c ∈ C is a *cognitive configuration*.

**Definition 2 (Self-relative appraisal).** Let W be the world/observation
state and Ω ⊆ W the OmegaSelf component (goals G, belief store B, capability
beliefs, goal-progress traces, per the OmegaSelf spec of 2026-07-15). An
*appraisal* is a map α : Ω × W → A into a finite set A of appraisal classes
(e.g. novelty, goal-stall, inconsistency, anomaly, integration, flatness).
Appraisal is *self-relative* iff α factors through Ω.

**Definition 3 (Emotion).** An *emotion* is a tuple e = (a_e, τ_e, λ_e, ε_e):
a_e ∈ A its evoking appraisal class; τ_e : C → C its **regime operator** (the
coordinated parameter-bundle shift); λ_e > 0 a decay rate; ε_e > 0 a
hysteresis (exit) threshold. While e is active with activation x ∈ [0,1], the
effective configuration is c(t) = c₀ + x·(τ_e(c₀) − c₀), with x decaying at
rate λ_e absent re-evocation.

**Definition 4 (Actuator support).** supp(e) is the set of coordinates of C on
which τ_e differs measurably from the identity *and* which correspond to real
levers — coordinates whose variation changes the hive's behavior distribution.

**Definition 5 (Regime value).** With U goal-utility of a trajectory and
κ > 0 a switching cost per regime transition: V(e) = E[U | e active, a_e] −
E[U | baseline, a_e] − κ·(expected switches induced).

**Definition 6 (Selection).** A selection dynamic S (evolutionary, or learning
via ECAN/PLN credit assignment) increases the prevalence of e iff V(e) > 0 and
eliminates e if V(e) < 0 persistently.

## 3. Theorems

**Theorem 1 (Actuator Necessity — "no lever, no emotion").**
If supp(e) = ∅ then V(e) = −κ·s < 0, where s is the induced switch rate; hence
under any selection dynamic satisfying Definition 6, e is eliminated.

*Proof sketch.* supp(e) = ∅ means τ_e changes no behavior-determining
coordinate, so the behavior distribution under e equals the baseline
distribution pointwise within appraisal class a_e. Therefore
E[U | e, a_e] = E[U | baseline, a_e], and V(e) = −κ·s < 0 whenever e is ever
entered. The regime is dominated and selected against. ∎

*Corollary 1.1.* The justified emotional palette of a hive is bounded by the
structure of its real control levers: emotions correspond (at most
surjectively) to equivalence classes of coordinated lever-bundles. Decorative
affect layers — emotion labels attached to no parameter bundle — are exactly
the supp(e) = ∅ case. This formalizes the design constraint "an emotion is
only justified if its characteristic action repertoire exists."

**Theorem 2 (Hysteresis Optimality — emotional inertia is forced).**
Let regime occupancy be controlled to maximize expected discounted U with
switching cost κ > 0 per transition, and let the appraisal signal be noisy,
ã = a + η with E[η] = 0, Var(η) = σ². Then any optimal switching policy has a
hysteresis band: there exists ε* > 0, increasing in κ and σ², such that
switching on appraisal differences smaller than ε* is strictly suboptimal.

*Proof sketch.* Standard optimal-switching (real-options) structure. With
continuation values J₁(x), J₂(x) as functions of the appraisal statistic x,
switching 1→2 at x yields J₂(x) − κ versus J₁(x) for staying. At the
indifference point x̂ the switch yields −κ < 0, so the optimal boundary lies
strictly past x̂ at a gap solving the smooth-pasting condition, growing with κ
and with σ² (noise raises the option value of waiting). Persistence beyond
naive indifference — hysteresis, ε_e > 0 in Definition 3 — is a property of
*every* optimal policy, not a design flaw. Emotions must be sticky. ∎

*Remark.* This is the formal sense in which "persisting long enough to bias
many decisions" is constitutive: a regime with ε_e = 0 and λ_e → ∞ is not an
emotion but an ordinary reactive policy, and it pays κ for every noise-driven
flicker.

**Theorem 3 (Decay–Lock-in Dichotomy — the paranoia condition).**
Let a regime have activation dynamics x_{t+1} = (1 − λ + g)·x_t + η_t, where
g ≥ 0 is self-excitation gain (the regime's own activity raises the rate of
appraisals that re-evoke it — e.g. vigilance raises monitoring sensitivity,
which raises anomaly-detection counts) and η_t is bounded exogenous input.
Then: (i) if λ > g, the dynamics are a contraction, x_t stays bounded, and
occupancy above any threshold has finite expected duration; (ii) if λ < g, the
low-activation state is unstable and trajectories converge to an attracting
high-activation region — a pathological lock (paranoia attractor).

*Proof sketch.* Write ρ = 1 − λ + g. The affine recurrence x_{t+1} = ρx_t + η_t
unrolls as x_t = ρᵗx₀ + Σ ρʲη_{t−j}. (i) λ > g ⟺ ρ < 1: the geometric sum
converges, x_t is bounded, return times below threshold are geometric. (ii)
λ < g ⟺ ρ > 1: perturbations amplify by factor ρ per step; with activation
capped at 1 (Definition 3), trajectories converge to the cap region, which is
attracting. The dichotomy threshold is exactly λ = g. ∎

*Corollary 3.1.* Every self-exciting emotion must satisfy the **decay
constraint** λ_e > g_e, with g_e audited empirically. Vigilance/fear in a
cybersecurity hive is the canonical case: functional only while decay outruns
self-excitation. "Calibration-guilt" (the post-false-positive regime) is a
mechanism for *lowering g_e* by threshold adjustment — it acts on the
dichotomy's control parameter, which is why it belongs in the palette.

**Theorem 4 (Legibility Bound — palettes are small).**
Let an overseer have inspection budget B bits per audit and require that the
active regime be exactly identifiable from the audit. Then |E| ≤ 2^B.

*Proof sketch.* Identifying one of |E| regimes takes log₂|E| bits;
log₂|E| ≤ B by the budget. ∎

*Remark.* Trivial as information theory, but it converts the informal design
intuition ("a handful of regimes, each legible — the overseer should be able to
ask *why is the hive anxious right now* and get an answer") into a hard
constraint. Combined with Corollary 1.1: the palette size is bounded above by
min(2^B, #lever-bundle-classes).

## 4. Conjecture

**Conjecture 1 (Appraisal Requires Self-Model).** An appraisal class that does
not factor through Ω (Definition 2) cannot stably correlate with goal-utility
across environment shifts; emotions evoked by non-self-indexed appraisals
degenerate into noise regimes with V(e) ≈ −κ·s, and are eliminated per
Theorem 1's mechanism.

*Status:* hypothesis-level. The plausible route is a no-free-lunch argument:
utility is defined relative to G ⊆ Ω, so a statistic uncorrelated with Ω is
uncorrelated with the sign of ΔU except by accident; any accidental
correlation is fragile under distribution shift. A clean proof needs a formal
model of "distribution shift over W" — left open.

*Remark.* This is the formal content of "without the self-model, emotions are
just control flags with dramatic names": OmegaSelf is what turns a parameter
flag into an *appraisal*, and Conjecture 1 says appraisal-via-self-model is
not optional but load-bearing.

## 5. Worked instantiations (from the 2026-07-19 thread)

Research-assistant hive, mapped to Definitions 1–3:

- **Curiosity/excitement** — a_e = novelty vs. world-model; τ_e raises STI
  wages on the surprising cluster, spawns exploratory subagents, widens
  tool/query search.
- **Frustration** — a_e = goal-stall in Ω's progress traces; τ_e re-routes
  ECAN out of the attractor, forces representation/method shift, escalates.
  The functional anger-analog: escapes local minima rather than fighting.
- **Doubt / epistemic anxiety** — a_e = inconsistency inside Ω's belief store
  near an important conclusion; τ_e raises verification thresholds, invokes
  checkers/provers, defers irreversible outputs. Note the self-excitation risk
  (doubt → more checking → more flagged inconsistencies): subject to the
  decay constraint of Corollary 3.1.
- **Satisfaction** — a_e = achieved coherent integration; τ_e consolidates to
  long-term memory, raises LTI, crystallizes into skills/ontology.
- **Boredom** — a_e = flat goal-progress and low surprise; τ_e drifts toward
  background drives (Hyperseed extension, the Substack formalization queue).

Cybersecurity hive: **vigilance/fear** (anomaly → monitoring STI spike,
permission tightening; decay constraint critical per Theorem 3); **red-team
aggression analog** (adversarial regime: temperature and hypothesis-generation
up, probe permissions on — justified only because attack actuators genuinely
exist, per Theorem 1); **calibration-guilt** (post-false-positive threshold
adjustment and provenance logging — lowers g, per Corollary 3.1);
**inter-agent trust** as an OmegaSelf-of-the-other variable modulating report
fusion (needed by purple-team modeling).

## 6. Open questions

1. **Learning τ_e.** The mapping emotion → parameter bundle should itself live
   in the self-model and be tuned by ECAN/PLN feedback on realized V(e). What
   is the right credit-assignment horizon, given Theorem 2's forced
   persistence delays the observable consequences of a regime?
2. **Composition.** Can two emotions co-activate? Blend operators on C
   (convex combination of τ's) vs. mutual-exclusion with priority — the
   former risks incoherent configurations, the latter reintroduces switching
   costs. Candidate small theorem: under what convexity of U is blending
   dominated by exclusion?
3. **Proof of Conjecture 1** via a formal distribution-shift model.
4. **Multi-agent generalization.** Trust as an emotion-like regime *of the
   hive about a subagent*: does Theorem 3's dichotomy govern trust-collapse
   cascades (a "suspicion attractor")?
5. **Measurement.** Operationalizing g_e and λ_e from ECAN/provenance logs so
   Corollary 3.1 becomes an auditable runtime invariant.
