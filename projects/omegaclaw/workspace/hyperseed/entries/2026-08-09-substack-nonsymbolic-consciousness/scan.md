## Summary

Ben Goertzel unpacks Jeffery Martin's terminology of **"Persistent Non-Symbolic Experience" (PNSE)** and translates it into a cognitive-architectural framework connecting symbol use, self-modeling, concept rigidity, and stages of consciousness evolution. Starting from the observation that "enlightened" individuals clearly *do* use symbols, Goertzel clarifies that the "non-symbolic" in PNSE refers not to the absence of symbols but to a specific **epistemic stance toward symbols** — one in which symbols are transparently understood as approximate, shifting, contextually dependent pointers floating in "an infinitely vaster sea of non-symbolic mind-stuff," rather than being reified into rigid, emotionally-laden mental objects that supplant the experiences they represent.

The article develops three key theses:

1. **Self–concept isomorphism:** The way a mind construes its *self* is structurally isomorphic to the way it construes *concepts* in general. Rigid self-boundaries → rigid concept boundaries. Over-reification of self → over-reification of concepts. "Post-self" experience and "post-symbolic" cognition are two faces of the same transformation.

2. **Concept-dynamic pathology taxonomy:** Drawing on his prior article's decomposition of "Evil" into four suboptimal self-dynamics (Stagnation, Explosion/Grandiosity, Destruction, Protection), Goertzel constructs a parallel **concept-dynamics taxonomy** — four "sins" of concept management that characterize symbol-dominated consciousness and whose avoidance constitutes the non-symbolic state.

3. **Martin's Position ontology:** A developmental sequence of consciousness states (Position 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → …) characterized by progressive dissolution of symbolically-mediated constructs: first the symbol-dominance itself dissolves (Position 1), then self (Position 2+), then will, then space and time. Animals occupy "Position −1" (pre-symbolic); the PNSE positions are explicitly **post-symbolic**, retaining symbolic capacity while transcending symbolic domination.

### Core Thesis

Non-symbolic consciousness is not the absence of symbols but their **proper epistemic placement** — symbols perceived as "temporarily constructed shortcut approximations to underlying complex shifting realities" rather than as fundamental entities. This epistemic transformation is structurally coupled to the dissolution of the reified self-model, and unfolds through a sequence of positions that can be modeled as a lattice of progressively dissolved symbolic mediations.

### Key Argumentative Arc

1. **Terminological puzzle:** Why call states "non-symbolic" when the people in them clearly use symbols?
2. **Resolution:** "Non-symbolic" = symbols aren't reified into substitutes for what they represent. They're understood as contextual approximations floating in experiential ground.
3. **Memory-based mechanism:** In ordinary consciousness, concepts formed to abstract experience accumulate independent emotional and cognitive weight, forming self-reinforcing circular networks ("mental knots") that occlude direct experience.
4. **Emotional reinforcement loop:** Emotions attach to abstract concept-networks, stabilizing them against dissolution and creating attachment to the *idea* of relationships/drives rather than to concrete experiences.
5. **Self–concept structural coupling:** Rigid self-boundaries correlate with rigid concept boundaries (thick vs. thin boundary personalities). Over-reifying *self* → over-reifying *all* concepts.
6. **Four concept-dynamic sins:** Stagnation, Explosion, Destruction, Protection — the morphed version of the four self-dynamic evils from the predecessor article.
7. **Non-symbolic = sin-free concept dynamics:** The post-symbolic state is characterized by avoiding all four concept-dynamic sins, allowing symbols to "pop in and out of the mind" fluidly.
8. **Position ontology:** A developmental sequence from Position 0 (symbol-dominated) through Position 1 (basic shift to non-symbolic dominance) to higher positions dissolving self, will, space, time.
9. **Pre-symbolic vs. post-symbolic:** Animals are non-symbolic by being pre-symbolic (Position −1). PNSE is non-symbolic by being *post-symbolic* — symbols are available but not dominant.
10. **Cosmological note:** The physical cosmos as we experience it owes its form partly to minds that reify its aspects via symbolically-mediated consciousness — higher positions are not necessarily "better" but are the natural direction of individuation and self-transcendence.

## Hyperseed-Relevant Structures

### S1. The Symbol-Transparency Principle

**Definition (Symbol Transparency).** A cognitive system operates in **symbol-transparent mode** when, for every symbol S deployed by the system:
- S is recognized as a contextually constructed approximation
- The mapping S → {concrete experiences from which S was abstracted} remains actively accessible
- S carries no independent emotional/cognitive weight beyond its functional role as a pointer
- S can be dissolved, reformed, or replaced without resistance from the system's self-model

The *opposite* of symbol transparency is **symbol reification**: treating the symbol as possessing independent substance, emotional charge, and persistence rights.

Non-symbolic consciousness = consciousness operating predominantly in symbol-transparent mode.

*[Epistemic label: novel formalization of Jeffery Martin's PNSE concept via Goertzel's cognitive-architectural interpretation. Not formally axiomatized but clearly specified at the conceptual level.]*

### S2. Self–Concept Structural Isomorphism

**Thesis.** Let B(self) denote the boundary-rigidity of a system's self-model, and B(C) denote the average boundary-rigidity of the system's concept repertoire. Then:

```
B(self) ≈ B(C)    (structural correlation)
```

More precisely:
- **Thick-boundaried** minds (high B(self)): rigid self-other distinction → rigid concepts, less creativity, less psychic sensitivity
- **Thin-boundaried** minds (low B(self)): fluid self-other boundary → fluid concepts, more creativity, more psychic sensitivity

The isomorphism extends to pathology:
- Over-reifying *self* → over-reifying *concepts* (both directions of mutual reinforcement)
- Post-self experience → post-symbolic cognition (both directions)

This is not merely correlation: the self-model IS a concept, so the stance toward concepts-in-general necessarily includes the stance toward self-as-concept. The self-concept is the **fixed point** of the concept-formation function — the concept that the concept-forming process forms *about itself*.

*[Epistemic label: novel synthesis. Draws on Hartmann's thick/thin boundary personality theory, but the formal interpretation as a structural isomorphism grounded in the self-model as fixed point of concept formation is original.]*

### S3. Concept-Dynamic Pathology Taxonomy (Four Sins)

**Definition.** Four modes of suboptimal concept management, each a morphed version of a corresponding self-dynamic pathology:

| Concept Sin | Self-Dynamic Counterpart | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Concept Stagnation** | Self-Stagnation | Drive to persist a concept unchanged, refusing to let it evolve with experience |
| **Concept Explosion** | Self-Explosion (Grandiosity) | Frantic drive to evolve concepts constantly before current ones are properly grounded in experience |
| **Concept Destruction** | Self-Destruction | Destroying/deactivating a concept prematurely due to frustration, giving up on adaptation |
| **Concept Protection** | Self-Protection | Reifying concept boundaries so that learning is allowed only if the concept remains intact, distinct, and important |

**Key insight:** In a non-symbolic state, concept usage is **well-tuned to avoid all four sins simultaneously**. This enables:
- Symbols to pop in and out of the mind fluently
- Symbols to serve their functional role then dissolve
- Symbols to be perceived, manipulated, and interpreted in the context of the sub-symbolic and super-symbolic processes that give them meaning

**Formal sketch.** Each sin can be modeled as an **attractor basin** in the dynamics of concept management on a knowledge metagraph:
- Stagnation: concept node with high resistance to edge-weight modification
- Explosion: concept node with low activation threshold for spawning child concepts
- Destruction: concept node with low activation energy for deletion
- Protection: concept node with high resistance to boundary dissolution (merging with related concepts)

The non-symbolic state corresponds to a **parameter regime** where none of these attractor basins dominate — a regime of balanced fluidity.

*[Epistemic label: novel taxonomy, directly extending the self-dynamic Evil decomposition from the predecessor article "Facing the Meta-Abstracted Dragon." Formal sketch is original; the metagraph interpretation is implied but not explicit in the article.]*

### S4. Emotional Knot Operator (Mental Knots)

**Model (Emotional Reification Loop).** The process by which ordinary consciousness becomes symbol-dominated:

1. **Abstraction:** Mind forms concept C to abstract a collection of concrete experiences {e₁, e₂, …, eₙ}.
2. **Retention:** C is retained in long-term memory for cognitive efficiency (too many concrete cases to manipulate individually).
3. **Accumulation:** Abstract symbolic knowledge about C accumulates → C gains importance *beyond* the concrete cases that generated it.
4. **Self-reinforcement:** Networks of abstractions form circular reinforcement loops.
5. **Emotional binding:** Emotions E attach to the abstraction network, stabilizing the circular reinforcement: E(C) >> E(eᵢ) for individual concrete experiences.
6. **Knot formation:** The emotionally-stabilized circular abstraction network becomes a "mental knot" — a self-reinforcing structure resistant to dissolution.

**Dissolution mechanism (from the article):** Decrease emotional attachment to {general entities} (e.g., "my relationship with X") relative to {specific experiences} (e.g., "this moment with X right now"). This loosens the emotional binding at step 5, allowing the circular reinforcement loop to dissipate.

*[Epistemic label: established in contemplative psychology (Buddhist/mindfulness traditions), novel formalization as a dynamic loop with identifiable intervention points. The term "mental knots" links to Goertzel's 2016 blog post on "mind knots and crumpled selves."]*

### S5. Martin's Position Lattice

**Model (Consciousness Position Ontology).** A developmental sequence of consciousness states characterized by which symbolic constructs still dominate experiential mediation:

```
Position -1 (Pre-symbolic):   Non-symbolic via absence of symbolic capacity (animals)
Position  0 (Symbol-dominated): Experience mediated by reified symbols, strong self-model
Position  1 (Basic non-symbolic): Shift from symbol-dominated to symbol-transparent; symbols still used but perceived as approximations
Position  2+:                  Self "dissolves" — reflective perception no longer mediated by self-model symbol
Position  3+:                  Will "dissolves" — reflective perception of action no longer mediated by free-will symbol-system
Position  4+:                  Space/time "dissolve" — experiences of change and extent no longer mediated by space/time symbols
```

**Key distinctions:**
- Pre-symbolic (Position −1) vs. post-symbolic (Position 1+): same surface behavior (non-symbolic), fundamentally different capacities
- Each higher position doesn't *remove* the capacity for the dissolved construct — it removes the *mandatory mediation* of experience by that construct
- "Symbols are just experienced as 'chunks of experience' among the others, floating in the experiential sea and serving their own purposes within the constantly fluctuating interconnected matrix"

**Formal interpretation.** The positions form a **partial order** (lattice) on the set of symbolic mediators that a mind can dissolve. The order is not strictly linear — "routes vary widely from one individual to another, yet also show marked common patterns." The mandatory mediators form a powerset lattice:

```
M = {symbol-reification, self-model, will-model, spacetime-model, …}
Position(mind) = M \ {dissolved mediators}
```

Higher position = smaller remaining set of mandatory mediators.

**Regression note:** Goertzel describes personal experience of oscillating between positions — it's possible to "project oneself back down" from Position 1 to Position 0 and need to "re-ignite the fire." The position lattice is not a one-way staircase; it has a gravitational pull toward lower positions (attractor dynamics).

*[Epistemic label: Jeffery Martin's empirical framework (from PNSE research), Goertzel's lattice/attractor interpretation is novel formalization.]*

### S6. The Experiential Sea Ontology

**Model.** The article posits a layered ontology of mind:

```
Layer 0: Non-symbolic experiential ground ("sea of non-symbolic experience")
Layer 1: Symbols emerge from, float in, and dissolve back into Layer 0
Layer 2: Networks of symbols form self-reinforcing structures (mental knots)
Layer 3: Self-model as a meta-symbol organizing Layer 2
```

In symbol-dominated consciousness (Position 0), Layers 2–3 dominate perception. In non-symbolic consciousness (Position 1+), Layer 0 is experienced as primary, with Layers 1–3 as functional tools within it.

**The key ontological claim:** The experiential sea (Layer 0) is not merely a "substrate" for symbols — it is **more fundamental** than symbolic content, and contains structure, meaning, and richness that symbols can only partially capture. Symbols are "contextually convenient mechanisms for representing certain aspects of the world to other aspects of the world."

*[Epistemic label: established in contemplative traditions (Buddhist sunyata, Advaitic awareness-ground), novel integration with cognitive-architectural language and symbol-processing theory.]*

### S7. Cosmological Entanglement Note

**Observation.** The article includes a striking cosmological aside: "the portion of the cosmos we live in owes its current form of existence in substantial part to the presence of minds that reify its aspects via their symbolically-mediated consciousness." This implies:

- Symbol-reification by minds **co-creates** the experienced physical cosmos
- Dissolving symbolic mediation (higher Positions) doesn't just change the *mind* — it changes the mind's *relationship with what exists*
- Higher positions are "not necessarily better" because the cosmos-as-we-know-it *depends on* symbolic mediation by minds within it

This connects directly to observer-relativized ontology: the cosmos is not a fixed backdrop but a co-creation between observing minds and observed patterns, mediated by the symbolic structures those minds deploy.

*[Epistemic label: speculative but consonant with Wheeler's participatory universe, QBist interpretations, and Goertzel's own observer-relative quantum mechanics work.]*

## Formal Candidates

### FC1. Symbol-Transparency Algebra

- **What to formalize:** A formal measure T(S, M) of the transparency of symbol S in mind M, ranging from fully reified (T=0) to fully transparent (T=1). Define composition rules for how transparency propagates through symbol networks. The non-symbolic state = T(S, M) > θ for all active symbols S.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. Requires defining "active accessibility of the abstraction-to-concrete mapping" computationally, likely via spreading activation or metagraph reachability.
- **Payoff:** Directly applicable as a runtime diagnostic for AGI systems — detecting when the system's own concepts have become pathologically reified. Could serve as a "cognitive hygiene" monitor in Hyperon.

### FC2. Concept-Dynamic Pathology Detector

- **What to formalize:** The four concept-dynamic sins (S3) as measurable properties of concept nodes in a knowledge metagraph: stagnation = low edge-weight volatility; explosion = high child-concept spawn rate relative to grounding evidence; destruction = premature pruning; protection = high merging-resistance.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. Each sin is definable as a statistical property of a concept's history in the metagraph. The challenge is setting appropriate thresholds and handling the "balanced fluidity" regime where none dominates.
- **Payoff:** High. This is a concrete engineering specification for a "concept health" monitor in AGI systems — detecting and correcting pathological symbol management in real time.

### FC3. Self-Concept Fixed-Point Theorem

- **What to formalize:** The self-model as a fixed point of the concept-formation function: if Φ is the function that forms concepts from experience, then self-model ≈ Φ(Φ) — the concept that Φ forms about its own operation. Prove that boundary-rigidity of the self-concept constrains boundary-rigidity of all other concepts (the self–concept isomorphism S2).
- **Difficulty:** Medium-high. Requires a formal model of concept formation as a function on metagraph structures, then analyzing the fixed-point properties.
- **Payoff:** Very high. Would ground the entire symbolic/non-symbolic consciousness framework in a mathematical theorem about self-referential concept formation.

### FC4. Position Lattice as Powerset over Symbolic Mediators

- **What to formalize:** Martin's positions as a powerset lattice over the set of mandatory symbolic mediators {symbol-reification, self-model, will-model, spacetime-model, …}. Define the dissolution ordering, characterize attractor dynamics that create gravitational pull toward lower positions, and model the regression phenomenon.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. The lattice structure is straightforward; the attractor dynamics for regression require a dynamical-systems model of how mediators re-crystallize.
- **Payoff:** High. Would provide a mathematical framework for consciousness development, directly integrable with Hyperseed's observer-relative ontology and the PNSE mapping in the 2026 paraconsistent AGI paper.

### FC5. Emotional Knot Formation as Dynamical System

- **What to formalize:** The six-step emotional reification loop (S4) as a dynamical system on a concept-emotion product space. Model knot formation as the emergence of a stable limit cycle or fixed-point attractor. Model dissolution as destabilizing the attractor via reducing emotional binding coefficients.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. The qualitative dynamics are clear; the challenge is choosing the right state-space representation.
- **Payoff:** Medium-high. Would provide a formal model of how minds get "stuck" and how contemplative practices loosen the stuckness — directly applicable to designing AGI systems that resist pathological reification.
