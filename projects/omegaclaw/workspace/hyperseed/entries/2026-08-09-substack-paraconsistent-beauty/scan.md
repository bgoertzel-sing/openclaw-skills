## Summary

Ben Goertzel develops a **paraconsistent theory of aesthetic beauty** grounded in the twin Open-Ended Intelligence (OEI) processes of **individuation** and **self-transcendence**. Starting from the classical musicological observation that beauty involves "surprising fulfillment of expectations" — which he recognizes as inherently contradictory — he maps the concept into paraconsistent logic where both terms can be simultaneously TRUE and FALSE. The article then extends this aesthetic framework into three major applications: (1) **computational creativity** and AI art generation, (2) **mathematical beauty** as a guide for automated theorem-proving, and (3) **engineered enlightenment** — modeling awakened consciousness as a "mind-foam" of coupled chaotic oscillators arising from systematic paraconsistentization of a mind's relational network.

The article is an early (2021) articulation of ideas that were later formalized more fully in the 2026 paraconsistent AGI paper. It introduces several novel concepts — **syntension**, **synerduction**, **mind-foam**, and the **paraconsistentization operator** — that form critical bridges between aesthetics, creativity, consciousness, and AGI ethics.

### Core Thesis

Beauty = individuation + self-transcendence of the pattern-system invoked in the observer's mind by the beautiful entity. "Surprising fulfillment of expectations" is a convenient but incomplete information-theoretic proxy. The deeper structure is paraconsistent: the most richly beautiful objects are those for which "A ⟹ individuation" and "A ⟹ self-transcendence" both have truth value BOTH TRUE AND FALSE simultaneously.

### Key Argumentative Arc

1. **Starting point:** Beauty as "surprising fulfillment of expectations" (musicological tradition).
2. **Recognition of contradiction:** Surprise and expectation-fulfillment are opposed → paraconsistent modeling required.
3. **OEI mapping:** Expectation-fulfillment ≈ individuation; surprise ≈ self-transcendence.
4. **Asymmetric implication:** (individuation + self-transcendence) ⟹ (surprising expectation-fulfillment), but the reverse holds only loosely.
5. **Paraconsistent beauty:** The richest beauty occurs on the Pareto frontier where both implications are BOTH TRUE AND FALSE.
6. **Schmidhuber critique:** Compression-progress correlates with beauty but misses embodied, motivational grounding (Moshe Looks' corrections are special cases of the OEI-beauty thesis).
7. **Mathematical beauty:** Theorems as fixed-point attractors of a derivation dynamic on the individuation/self-transcendence Pareto frontier.
8. **Syntension:** A novel third mode of conceptual content (beyond extension and intension), arising from constructive interference among chaotic time-series unfolded from paraconsistent category relationships.
9. **Enlightenment as mind-foam:** Systematically paraconsistentizing all relationships in a mind, then chaotizing the resulting recursions, produces a system of coupled chaotic oscillators = "mind-foam" = enlightened consciousness.
10. **AGI ethics via beautiful minds:** Rather than training specific values, build cognitively beautiful architectures that naturally perceive and create beauty, generating compassionate ethics emergently.

## Hyperseed-Relevant Structures

### S1. Paraconsistent Beauty Operator

**Definition (Paraconsistent Aesthetic Valuation).** For entity A perceived by observer B, the degree of beauty is modeled via two paraconsistent implications:

- `A ⟹_B individuation` with truth value in {T, F, B, N} (true, false, both, neither)
- `A ⟹_B self-transcendence` with truth value in {T, F, B, N}

where the subscript B denotes observer-relativity. Maximum "clean" beauty: both implications TRUE. Maximum "textured/ironic" beauty: both implications BOTH TRUE AND FALSE. The latter is characteristic of postmodernist art and pre-civilized traditions unconcerned with logical consistency.

*[Epistemic label: novel formalization, explicitly sketched but not fully axiomatized. Extends Belnap four-valued logic into aesthetic domain.]*

### S2. Individuation as Causal Explanatory Power

**Definition (Individuation via Causal Inference).** A system S is individuated relative to observer O to the degree that explanations involving S-as-entity have greater causal power than explanations involving S's components or S's supersystems.

Formal grounding: algorithmic Markov processes (Goertzel, arXiv:2005.04589). Causal inference is intrinsically observer-relative, dependent on an assumed Universal Turing Machine behind program-length measures.

Mereological grounding: part-hood conceived independently from space-time, as a formal algebraic notion with Heyting mereology composition rules.

*[Epistemic label: established (algorithmic information theory + mereology), novel synthesis for aesthetics.]*

### S3. Self-Transcendence as Pattern-Emergence

**Definition (Self-Transcendence).** A system S self-transcends when it generates patterns strongly distinct from the implications of any of its prior component-patterns individually or in combination, yet emergent from the prior pattern-substrate plus environmental inputs or internal noise.

In mathematics: a theorem manifests self-transcendence if its ready implications are strongly distinct from the implications of any of its lemmas or sub-clauses individually or in combination.

*[Epistemic label: established OEI concept, applied here to mathematical and aesthetic domains.]*

### S4. Beauty as Fixed-Point Attractor of Derivation Dynamics

Application: Auto-generating beautiful conjectures → feeding to automated theorem-provers → generating training data → recursive improvement → superhuman AGI mathematician.

### S5. Syntension — A Third Mode of Conceptual Content

1. Unfold each paraconsistent relationship as a chaotic time-series.
2. Identify constructive interferences between these time-series.
3. The superposed time-series can be reverse-engineered to discover new paraconsistent relationships not present in the original extension or intension.

Syntensional properties emerge from synergetic interaction (syn = synergetic) between extension and intension under paraconsistent dynamics. Syntension is the mechanism by which self-transcendence arises — it is the creative engine.

**Example:** A Jimi Hendrix song that is SF-themed and atonal → constructive interference of the corresponding time-series → syntensional association with vocoders, despite no vocoder audio being present and vocoders not yet invented.

Connection to concept blending: Syntension subsumes intensional blending but captures additional structure that purely intensional models miss.

Connection to natural language: Syntension provides a "poetic/quantum semantics" complementing ordinary individuating semantics. Via the paraconsistent-quantum logic morphism, this is a quasi-quantum aspect of NL semantics.

*[Epistemic label: novel concept. Highly speculative but well-motivated. Critical bridge between paraconsistency, creativity, and quantum-logic semantics.]*

### S6. The Paraconsistentization Operator

**Definition (Paraconsistentization).** An operator P that maps probabilistic truth values to paraconsistent truth values:
```
P: [0,1] → {(s, o) | s, o ∈ [0,1]}
```
where s = supporting evidence, o = opposing evidence.

Applied across all relationships in a mind-network, P transforms ordinary inference into paraconsistent inference, enabling the system to hold contradictory perspectives simultaneously.

*[Epistemic label: named and characterized but not fully specified. Key operator for the enlightenment model.]*

### S7. The Chaotization Operator

**Definition (Chaotization).** An operator χ that maps paraconsistent recursions into chaotic time-series:
```
χ: ParaconsistentRecursion → ChaoticTimeSeries
```

When composed with P across a mind-network, χ ∘ P transforms the mind into a system of **coupled chaotic oscillators** = **"mind-foam"** (by analogy with spin-foam models in physics).

Properties of mind-foam:
- Large-scale nonlinear dynamics emerge from local coupling.
- The system can "look at all sides of all issues" — contradictions are represented, not suppressed.
- Constructive interference between chaotic time-series enables **synerduction** (syntensional reasoning).
- When quantum computing resources are available, χ becomes quantum-chaotization, giving syntension/synerduction a different flavor.

*[Epistemic label: highly speculative. The mind-foam metaphor is evocative but under-specified. The quantum variant is even more speculative.]*

## Formal Candidates

### FC1. Paraconsistent Aesthetic Valuation Algebra
- **What to formalize:** The four-valued beauty operator (S1) as an algebraic structure over Belnap lattice, with composition rules for combining aesthetic valuations across observer perspectives.
- **Difficulty:** Medium. The Belnap lattice is well-established; the aesthetic interpretation needs axiomatization.
- **Payoff:** Directly applicable to computational creativity systems and Hyperon aesthetic modules.

### FC2. Syntension as Constructive Interference on Paraconsistent Time-Series
- **What to formalize:** The full pipeline: category → paraconsistent unfolding → chaotic time-series → constructive interference → reverse-engineering of emergent relationships.
- **Difficulty:** High. Requires specifying the chaotization map, defining constructive interference for irregular time-series, and proving the reverse-engineering step recovers meaningful paraconsistent relationships.
- **Payoff:** Very high. Syntension is the proposed mechanism for creativity and self-transcendence. Formalizing it would ground computational creativity and concept-blending in paraconsistent dynamics.

### FC3. Beauty-Guided Derivation Search
- **What to formalize:** The derivation dynamic on theorem-space with individuation and self-transcendence as objective functions, Pareto frontier characterization, fixed-point/limit-cycle/strange-attractor classification.
- **Difficulty:** Medium-high. Individuation (causal explanatory power) and self-transcendence (implication novelty) need computable measures.
- **Payoff:** High. Direct engineering application: beauty-guided automated theorem-proving → recursive AGI mathematician.

### FC4. Mind-Foam Architecture Specification
- **What to formalize:** The full P → χ → coupled-oscillator pipeline as a Hyperon metagraph operation, including the pruning problem and synerduction as inference control.
- **Difficulty:** Very high. Requires specifying P and χ concretely, characterizing the resulting dynamical system, and proving synerduction can offset the combinatorial explosion.
- **Payoff:** Foundational. This is the computational architecture for engineered enlightenment.

### FC5. Beauty-Theoretic AGI Ethics
- **What to formalize:** The claim that architecturally beautiful minds (high internal paraconsistent coherence) naturally produce compassionate behavior. Requires defining "architectural beauty" computationally and proving or demonstrating the compassion-emergence thesis.
- **Difficulty:** Very high (partially philosophical).
- **Payoff:** Foundational for AI alignment via internal coherence rather than external constraint.
