## Summary

This is the **canonical founding article** for Hyperseed-1 — the original public introduction of the ontology. Goertzel presents a "semi-formal core ontology" of ~90 concepts designed not as a complete formalization of commonsense knowledge (à la Cyc) but as an **inference guidance scaffold** for early-stage AGI minds, specifically OpenCog Hyperon running PLN (Probabilistic Logic Networks).

The article:
1. Traces the intellectual lineage from Leibniz → Carnap → Chalmers → Hyperseed
2. Critiques prior knowledge-formalization efforts (Cyc, SUMO)
3. Articulates Chalmers' PQTI framework as theoretical backing
4. Defines the specific, novel purpose of Hyperseed-1: not logical reductionism but **practical inference control**
5. Lays out philosophical commitments (panpsychism, non-well-founded emergence, Buddhist phenomenology, paraconsistency, transhumanism)
6. Provides three worked example concepts with full formalizations: Experiential Truth, Affective State, Blend
7. Describes the "hallucinated perspectives" experiment (17 thinkers' perspectives on each concept via LLMs)

**Version designation:** Alpha1 (Goertzel's own label)

## Hyperseed-Relevant Structures

### 1. Intellectual Lineage

| Thinker | Contribution | Relation to Hyperseed |
|---------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Leibniz** | "Universal Characteristic" — formalization of commonsense in Boolean/propositional logic | Founding ancestor; same aspiration, different scope |
| **Bertrand Russell** | Logical formalization of knowledge | Philosophical lineage |
| **Rudolf Carnap** | *Aufbau* — minimal language with binary "recollected similarity" predicate; all truths deducible from minimal base | Direct methodological inspiration; Chalmers builds on this |
| **David Chalmers** | *Constructing the World* — PQTI framework; argues small set of basic truths suffices for a priori derivation of all truths | Primary theoretical framework; Goertzel "pretty much agrees" |
| **Anna Wierzbicka** | Natural Semantic Metalanguage (implied via semantic primitives tradition) | Not cited in this article but part of the broader Hyperseed lineage (see v2 article) |
| **C.S. Peirce** | "Matter is just mind hide-bound with habit" | Goertzel quotes approvingly re: P/Q distinction |
| **Gilles Deleuze** | "Difference and repetition" | Goertzel notes shades of this in Carnap's "recollected similarity" |

### 2. Chalmers' PQTI Framework (Theoretical Foundation)

Chalmers argues: "There is a small set of truths, with conjunction D, such that for all truths S, ⌜If D then S⌝ is a priori."

The set D = P ∪ Q ∪ T ∪ I where:

- **P** = Truths of fundamental physics + physical properties of macrophysical objects (size, shape, velocity, etc.)
- **Q** = Phenomenal truths — "what it is like to be a given entity"
- **I** = Indexical truths — spatiotemporal location of the subject
- **T** = Totality sentence — asserts nothing critical omitted from P, Q, I

**Goertzel's gloss:** If a mind assumes (1) basics of physical universe, (2) basics of subjective experience, (3) basics of its situation, and (4) nothing critical omitted — then everything it needs follows.

**Goertzel's departure:** Hyperseed-1 concepts fit within PQTI but intentionally **mix** elements of P, Q, and I in complex ways. The most useful ontological core for practical inference control is **not** the most minimal/foundational — it includes pragmatically useful higher-level concepts (Society, Emotion) even though these aren't theoretically irreducible.

### 3. Critique of Prior Formalization Attempts

| System | Lesson Learned |
|--------|---------------|
| **Cyc (Cycorp)** | (a) Too many logical statements needed for human-based encoding; (b) Useful reasoning on massive KB is hard; (c) Uncertainty layered as afterthought doesn't work — must be baked in |
| **SUMO** | More elegant/concise than Cyc but succumbs to same fundamental issues |

### 4. The Novel Purpose: Inference Control / Inference Guidance

**Key insight:** Hyperseed-1 is NOT meant as:
- A complete formalization of commonsense knowledge
- A tool for semantic communication between humans
- A logically reductionist foundation

It IS meant as:
- **An inference guidance scaffold** for an early-stage AGI mind doing uncertain logical inference
- Specifically for OpenCog Hyperon running PLN

**Inference control strategy (the core proposal):**

> Ask an inference engine to proceed heuristically via:
> 1. Connecting each new batch of logical statements, hypotheses or questions to a certain ontological core of concepts
> 2. Spending a nontrivial percentage of its inferential energy on reasoning that uses these ontologically core concepts

**Theoretical requirement (weaker than reductionism):** Approximately expressing practical knowledge in terms of the core ontology must lead to **inference sketches** that provide useful guidance for detailed practical inferences.

### 5. Philosophical Commitments Underlying Hyperseed-1

1. **Panpsychism** — everything has a certain element/type of consciousness
2. **Non-privileged reality** — physical, mental, and intersubjective reality emerge from each other in a **non-well-founded** way (no single base layer)
3. **Buddhist-style phenomenology** — complex aspects of mental/perceived reality built from low-level experiential atoms
4. **Paraconsistent & non-dual frameworks** — Taoism, Constructible Duality Logic
5. **Postmodernist multi-perspectivism** — deep relativism, ties to Weaver-style Open-Ended Intelligence, complex self-organizing dynamical systems
6. **Transhumanism** — *A Cosmist Manifesto* (2010), *The Consciousness Explosion* (2023)

**Meta-commitment (Nietzsche):** "The will to a system is a lack of integrity" — intentionally not fully systematic.

### 6. Design Principles of the Ontology

| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Scale** | ~90 concepts in core ontology (Alpha1) |
| **Format** | Clear English + loose predicate-logic-ish formalizations |
| **Formal tools used** | Predicate logic, probabilistic/fuzzy expressions, non-well-founded sets, categories & toposes, paraconsistent logic |
| **Target formalism** | MeTTa (Hyperon) — designed for moving between logical/mathematical formalisms |
| **Incompleteness** | Intentional — avoids Cyc-like rabbit holes |
| **Messiness** | Some unintentional inconsistency from being "fast and loose"; PLN handles this by design |
| **LLMs used in creation** | GPT-4o, o1-mini, o1-preview, Llama 3.5, Claude Sonnet — but heavy human participation required |
| **Origin timeline** | Bits and pieces since 2014; formalized with LLM assistance ~2024 |

### 7. Concept Infrastructure List

A superset of the ~90 core ontology concepts plus additional concepts with "more simply empirical or mathematical meanings" that didn't need formal specification in Hyperseed-1. Serves as the vocabulary for expressing formalizations.

### 8. Hallucinated Perspectives Experiment

Each of the ~90 concepts reformulated from 17 perspectives:
- Jean Baudrillard, Ludwig Wittgenstein, Krishnamurti, Friedrich Nietzsche, Octavio Paz, Lao Tzu, Rumi, Tyson Yunkaporta, Mary Catherine Bateson, Ray Kurzweil, Fyodor Dostoevsky, Ben Goertzel
- Archetypes: appliance manual writer, AGI-evaluation scientist, self-evaluating AGI, human-to-alien explainer, teacher for third graders

**Purpose:** Provide overlapping but diverse perspectives on core concepts; potentially useful for AI inference guidance via multi-perspectival coverage.

### 9. Role as AGI "Upbringing"

> "For an AGI system worth its salt, any core ontology of this nature should be nothing more than a partial and initial guide — something to help it guide its inferences in the early stage, and something that it can choose to ignore or to revise or overthrow as it learns and reasons."

Analogy to human childhood upbringing — sets initial course, can be overcome, but shapes trajectory.

## Formal Candidates

### FC-1: Experiential Truth

**Type:** Cognitive/phenomenological concept
**Core ontology tags:** Association, Causation, Cognition, Coherence, Consciousness, Control, Context, Individuation, Self-Transcendence, Representation, System, Transformation

**Formalization:**
```
ExperientialTruth(S, M) ↔ CausallyConnected(
  HoldingIdea(M, IdeaSatisfiedWorld(S)),
  IncreasedIndividuation(M) ∧ IncreasedSelfTranscendence(M)
)
```

**Plain English:** A statement S is experientially true for mind M iff believing "the world satisfies S" causally leads to M's increased individuation and self-transcendence.

**Key predicates:** CausallyConnected, HoldingIdea, IdeaSatisfiedWorld, IncreasedIndividuation, IncreasedSelfTranscendence

**Hyperseed significance:** Bridges representation, causation, and consciousness. Defines truth not as correspondence but as transformative cognitive effect — a distinctly panpsychist/Buddhist framing.

---

### FC-2: Affective State

**Type:** Cognitive/evaluative concept
**Core ontology tags:** Action, Context, Decision, Entity, Evaluation, Feelings, Goal, Interaction, MotivationalTendency, Process, Quality, Representation, Value

**Formalization:**
```
AffectiveState(e, a) ↔ (
  ∃s ∈ S, ∃g ∈ G, (
    EvaluativeResponse(e, s, g) ∧ ArisesFromInteraction(e, s)
  ) ∧
  ∃f ∈ F, QualitativeFeeling(a, f) ∧
  ∃m ∈ M, MotivationalTendency(a, m) ∧
  ContextDependent(a, s)
)
```

**Plain English:** Entity e is in affective state a iff there exists a situation s and goal g such that e evaluatively responds to s w.r.t. g, the state arises from interaction with s, includes a qualitative feeling, includes a motivational tendency, and depends on context s.

**Key predicates:** AffectiveState, EvaluativeResponse, ArisesFromInteraction, QualitativeFeeling, MotivationalTendency, ContextDependent

**Hyperseed significance:** Grounds affect in goal-directed evaluation rather than treating it as arbitrary qualia. Links to the inference guidance strategy — affective states guide attention/resource allocation during reasoning.

### FC-3: Blend (Fuzzy-Logic Concept)

**Type:** Mathematical/structural concept
**Core ontology tags:** Combination, Property, Emergent Patterns, Harmony, Simplicity, Structural Complexity, Pattern Recognition

**Formalization (Blend membership function):**
```
μ_blend(C; A, B) = Σ_{x ∈ Props(C)} max(δ_significant(x, A), δ_significant(x, B)) / |Props(C)|
```

**Formalization (High-Quality Blend membership function):**
```
μ_hq-blend(C; A, B) = Σ_{x ∈ EmergentProps(C; A, B)} μ_emergence(x; A, B, C) / |Props(C)|
```

where:
- `Props(E)` = properties of entity E
- `δ_significant(x, E)` = 1 if μ_prop(x, E) ≥ θ, else 0
- `EmergentProps(C; A, B)` = properties in C not significantly present in A or B alone
- `μ_emergence(x; A, B, C)` = μ_prop(x, C) − max(μ_prop(x, A), μ_prop(x, B))

**Plain English:** A blend score measures how much of C's properties derive significantly from either A or B. A high-quality blend score measures how much C exhibits novel emergent properties beyond what A or B individually contribute.

**Hyperseed significance:** This is the most mathematically rigorous of the three examples. Uses fuzzy-set membership functions — demonstrates the ontology's capacity for precise quantitative formalization alongside philosophical concepts. Directly relevant to conceptual blending in AGI reasoning.
