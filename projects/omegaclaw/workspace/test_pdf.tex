\noindent\textbf{Definition (Morphic Seed).}
A \emph{morphic seed} is a triple $\mathcal{S} = (G, \varphi, \Omega)$ where
\begin{itemize}
  \item $G$ is a generative grammar over a countable alphabet $\Sigma$,
  \item $\varphi: \Sigma^* \to \mathcal{H}$ is an embedding into a metagraph space $\mathcal{H}$,
  \item $\Omega \subseteq \mathcal{H}$ is the \emph{target attractor basin}---the region of $\mathcal{H}$ toward which iterated self-rewriting converges.
\end{itemize}
