## Purpose

Test the H0--H6 evidence ladder for causal-fibres and execute the revised
E1--E5 programme: guarded homotopy absorption, frontier/fibre emergence,
matched structured settlement, deployment-honest settlement, and bounded MORK
hosting.

## Success criteria

- Each gate has a preregistered, reproducible experiment record.
- H0 reports raw held-out task metrics, loss/accuracy teacher-gap closure,
  factor-specific counterfactual closure and spill, uncertainty, and all budget
  coordinates.
- E1 distinguishes teacher-clamped from teacher-free fidelity and directly
  compares homotopy against the archived R8/R9 pathology at matched seeds.
- E2 admits an emergence claim only against shuffled/random and dictionary
  baselines with do-intervention fingerprints.
- E3 meters actual settle work; E4 reports deployable gap recovery; E5 reports
  both numerical fidelity and honest MORK cost.
- Advancement follows the quantitative kill gates in Hyperseed Formalization
  0014; negative evidence is retained.

## Scope

### In scope

- Local CPU smoke experiments and approved larger replications.
- Frozen-interface BP capacity curves and later representation contests.
- The released `causal-fibres` package and controlled synthetic grammars.
- Revised programme E1--E5, with E1/E2 neural evidence independent of whether
  the later MORK engineering path succeeds.

### Out of scope for now

- Paid compute without explicit approval.
- Full paid E1--E5 campaigns before protocol, estimator, and resource gates.

## Current state

The 0.4.0 release and 54-test suite pass. A local CPU H0 smoke gate admitted
the toy interface (`Gamma_L=0.99143`, 95% CI `[0.99070,0.99220]`; all five
factor closures above `0.8197`).

Ben supplied the revised E1--E5 implementation programme on 2026-07-23. It
reinstates whole-model absorption through a guarded BP-anchored homotopy and
makes frontier/fibre emergence the keystone test. Phase 1 is now active:
reconcile the canonical R8/R9 source and rig identities, freeze shared harness
invariants and disjoint calibration/confirmation protocols, validate metrics
on reduced controls, and estimate runtime/storage before requesting compute.
The target H0 BP residual remains standing control C1 rather than being
discarded.

The reduced E1-SG CPU harness is now implemented on an isolated worktree based
on exact R8 commit `ecf2f79`. Four matched arms, frozen-weight settlement,
teacher-free evaluation, guarded homotopy, frontier/rank diagnostics, and
deterministic replay passed their smoke gates. The 12-update values are
operational evidence only; acceptance thresholds and the 3/5
calibration/confirmation campaign remain unfrozen and unrun.

Ben then supplied programme v1.1. It preserves v1.0 work but changes the
critical path: first run E1 post-hoc depth-profile and
settled-field-versus-BP-adjoint (M0) checks, then run E2 on both substrates.
The local smoke remains operational evidence, not the “E1 complete” result
assumed by the external programme. E4 is now explicitly a test of
inference-time information injection over the feedforward deployed default.

A first reduced v1.1 P1/P2 instrument run passed invariants but produced a
readout-local field: with eight settle steps, KD and CE both put all global
top-5% mass in block 6 and blocks 1--3 remained exactly zero. Blocks 5--6
aligned with the BP descent field, but this does not establish whole-model M0.
The next gate is therefore a fixed-weight settlement-depth sweep through
`T=128`, not E2 fingerprint unblinding.

That sweep is now complete. Depth propagated tiny nonzero fields upstream but
did not change the frontier: KD and CE assigned all global top-5% mass to block
6 at every `T` through 128. At `T=128`, blocks 4--6 aligned closely with the BP
descent field, block 3 was only moderately aligned, and blocks 1--2 remained
unobservable. Settle depth alone therefore does not reconcile the local rig
with Mesto; an exact field-definition/normalization and dynamics audit is next.

A residual-block constructed control is also complete. Identity skips
increased measurable upstream field magnitude, but KD and CE still placed
100% of global top-5% field mass in block 6 through `T=128`. Residual
connectivity alone therefore does not reproduce Mesto's early/middle depth
profile; the next substrate adds token mixing while retaining residual paths.

The minimal transformer-like token-mixing control is likewise readout-local:
all top-5% mass remains in block 6 through `T=128`. Its upstream M0 is weaker
than the residual-MLP control, so the latter is the provisional substrate for
the adjoint fast path. Neither is a valid local match to Mesto's profile.

The teacher-free adjoint fast path is now operational on the residual control.
It exposes an early profile (83.5% of top-5% mass in blocks 1--2), while the
settled field remains entirely in block 6. Global M0 depth agreement is zero,
so the two E2 tracks are independent rather than mutually validating.

E2 is now disposed as **E2-B (concentration without recovered structure)** on
both tracks. Criteria version 1.2.0 was frozen from eight-seed constructed
positive/pathology/null fixtures before three disjoint calibration and five
confirmation seeds ran. M1 and M5 show stable, observable credit fields, but
no block passes the frozen M2/M3/M4 structural conjunction. All 96 campaign
JBD fits converged. M0 remains low and Prediction 2 fails on both tracks.
Recovered fibres must not be selected for E3; programme branch 5.2 permits E3
only with supplied fibres, explicitly treated as imposed structure.

Mandatory C2 subsequently found a consequential estimator disagreement:
five-seed SAE dictionaries pass frozen M3/M4 on observable settled block 6
and all adjoint blocks, with co-location passing, despite JBD failing every
block. The operational branch remains E2-B because E2-A preregistered JBD and
C2 was not an alternative selector, but the interpretation is narrowed to
“no JBD-recoverable structure; positive dictionary structure.”

E3 has now been rerun on an Option-1 partially trained student selected
without inspecting arm outcomes. A 75-update homotopy stop met the
preregistered 70--90% aggregate pre-E3 task-accuracy target (`87.89%`), though
one calibration seed remained saturated. Three calibration and five disjoint
confirmation seeds completed. Supplied fibres failed the frozen CS bar
against DGC-20: task accuracy improved on only 3/5 paired seeds, task loss on
1/5, and factor BCE on 0/5. V3 routing was frozen ID-to-CS.

The five-seed V4 probe matched dense and BP selected-scalar work within 0.6%.
Dense settlement showed much less off-support drift than BP on three seeds,
but the advantage did not co-vary with proximal divergence (`r=-0.189`), so
Prediction 1 is not cleanly supported and effective-step matching remains
unresolved. The E4 rerun raised SC CS-loss recovery to `16.8%--23.5%` (mean
`19.83%`) but still missed the frozen 20% bar; confirmation was not launched.

A Ben-approved exploratory E4 factorial diagnosis now separates information
completeness, constraint weight, and injection channel on the same 75-update
student. After correcting the full4 per-factor normalization, full information
approximately doubles two-cap recovery; all series improve strictly through
5x; and direct-logit injection yields about 2.45x hidden-channel recovery.
Full4/5x/both reaches mean CS loss `0.97079`, accuracy `98.44%`, and
task-loss `G=4.5517`. This diagnoses the prior weakness without revising the
frozen negative E4 gate: full4 is label-equivalent diagnostic information.

Three disjoint confirmation seeds (`20801, 21903, 23017`) replicated all
five frozen thresholds (C1--C5): the full4/two-cap ratio is exactly `2.0`
across all 12 conditions, all 9 weight series remain strictly increasing,
the logit/hidden ratio is `2.16--2.18`, the both-vs-logit difference is
below `1.5e-5`, and the best G is `4.34430 ± 0.28271`. The frozen negative
E4 gate is unchanged; this is diagnostic, not deployability evidence.

E5 W1/W2 numerical kernels are eligible and pass on pinned MORK's standalone
`linalg` crate: six-layer f32 forward meets the `1e-5` relative-logit bar with
exact argmax, and five-step relaxation matches within `1e-6`. Actual in-store
hosting remains blocked by the absence of a typed tensor resource/sink adapter.

## Risks

- A toy teacher/student result cannot establish the GPT-2-small production
  interface result; scope claims explicitly.
- Factor metrics can be misleading unless counterfactual edits change exactly
  one generator factor.
- Ratio confidence intervals can be unstable if the teacher gap is small.
- Calibrating and confirming acceptance on the same replicate set would make
  the gate circular; these sets must be disjoint.
- Sparse update mass does not imply sparse computation if frontier discovery
  first computes all dense errors.
- Autonomous compute spend is USD 0.
