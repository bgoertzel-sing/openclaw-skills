                                                Abstract
          Version 1.1 revises the v1.0 programme in light of clarifications and new measurements
      from Mesto (author correspondence, July 23, 2026) that resolve both ambiguities v1.0 was
      designed to guard against, and that materially change the interpretation of the homotopy
      result. The central update: at 124M the settled ePC error field is, to high precision, the
      backpropagation adjoint field — all fidelity numbers were teacher-free feedforward all along,
      the errors are absolutely small (5.6 × 10−4 global L2), and the “error-norm ratio” curve is a
      gradient-alignment probe (∥PC grad∥/∥BP grad∥ rising η → 0.98 at per-block cosine ≥ 0.998).
      Consequences for this programme: the emergence battery (E2) gains a cheap adjoint-field track
      that requires no homotopy and starts immediately; E3 gains gradient-sparsification baselines
      and a proximal-step ablation; E4 is reframed — there is no amortization gap because nothing
      is amortized, and the entire deployment question reduces to whether test-time information
      injection beats the feedforward default; E1’s interesting content shifts from “does the homotopy
      avoid collapse” (Mesto’s depth profile says it should) to localizing what in the R8/R9 recipe
      caused collapse. Section 1 tells implementing agents exactly what to do with completed (E1)
      and in-flight (E2) work; the remainder restates the full programme so this document is self-
      contained and v1.0 can be retired.

1.1       Why the revision
Mesto’s reply corrects two misreadings of his report and adds new measurements:
 1. All fidelity numbers were teacher-free. Evaluation in his report is plain feedforward
    (ε=0) on both student and teacher; the teacher enters only the training-time settle objective.
    The v1.0 branch E1-C scenario (clamped-only fidelity) is off the table for his result.




                                                    2
  2. The rising curve is a gradient probe, not a state-space quantity. The “error-norm
     ratio” is ∥∇W EW ∥/∥∇BPW L∥ per block, teacher-free under cross-entropy: it starts at exactly
     η (the T =1 anchor identity, observed to the digit) and rises to 0.98, with per-block cosine
     between the two gradients never below 0.998. Together with the tiny error norms (5.6 × 10−4
     global L2 over 4.72M cells), the settled error field ≈ the BP adjoint field: the homotopy
     interpolates the magnitude of the update from an η-scaled BP step to a full-magnitude local
     update that never leaves BP’s direction.
  3. Depth profile (new data). Top-5% frontier mass by block (1–12, % of frontier cells): 11.1,
     18.7, 17.2, 14.8, 15.3, 11.3, 7.2, 2.8, 1.5, 0.1, 0.1, 0.0. Blocks 2–5 hold 66%; the last three
     blocks hold 0.2%. This is the opposite of the R8/R9 late-block pile-up.
  4. Objective-swap robustness (new data). Replacing the KD term with plain cross-entropy
     on data (no teacher anywhere) preserves the structure: top-5% share 91.6% vs. 90.0%, same
     depth shape (13.1, 20.8, 19.5, 15.9, 12.6, 8.2, 6.2, 2.4, 0.9, 0.2, 0.1, 0.0). Across fresh samples
     under both objectives the top-5% share stays in 82–97%; the fixed-depth Hill statistic moves
     1.38–2.00 and is a tail-weight descriptor only.
  5. Deployment is trivially resolved in his frame. With the task term absent, the settle
     energy is the pure quadratic in ε, minimized at ε=0: the deployed artifact is exactly the
     feedforward student. PC in this construction is a learning-time device; nothing is amortized.

1.2    Interpretive frame carried through the rest of the document
At the terminal homotopy, PC is a local iterative scheme that computes backpropagation’s credit
signal. This is a clean confirmation of the PC-approximates-BP correspondence at 103 × prior scale,
and it has two programme-level corollaries. (a) The concentration measurement is a measurement
of adjoint sparsity, obtainable on any trained model with plain autograd, no homotopy required
— which is why E2 gains an adjoint-field track that starts immediately. (b) If the credit signal is
the same signal, the PC parameterisation alone should confer approximately no continual-learning
advantage; any advantage must enter via frontier gating (the S3/S4 lesson) or via the one residual
structural difference, the proximal/implicitly-regularized character of a settle-based update versus
a raw gradient step. Both corollaries are pre-registered as Predictions 1 and 2 below.

3.3     Fields and substrates (new in v1.1)
Two fields are first-class artifact types, sharing one schema:
     Settled error field ε(x): the terminal-settle error tensors, per block, from an ePC student.
     Adjoint field δ(x): the plain BP adjoints ∂L/∂xℓ of a standard model (teacher or E1 student
      at ε=0), teacher-free CE, computed by autograd. No homotopy, no settle.
The harness persists both for the shared probe set; every field-level statistic (concentration, depth
profile, M1–M5) must run unmodified on either substrate.

3.4     Methodology standards (unchanged from v1.0; mandatory)
Envelope thresholds from n ≥ 3 replicates, rule fixed in advance; matched seeds and per-seed
deltas for all comparisons; pre-registered acceptance criteria in versioned JSON; dual-condition
fidelity (clamped and teacher-free) wherever a settle exists in training; per-block reporting for
all cell statistics; interventional validation for causal claims (never energy curves); full artifact
emission (config hash, seed, commit, JSONL metrics, histograms, standard figures); no silent
exclusion — runs launched = runs reported.

7     E4 (reframed): test-time information injection vs. the feedfor-
      ward default
7.1    Reframing
In the corrected picture there is no amortization gap: absent a task term the energy minimizes at
ε=0, so FF is the deployed artifact by construction. The deployment question is therefore
not “does the settle survive removal of the teacher” but purely: can a settle clamped to test-time
information the feedforward pass does not use — observed tokens, symbolic constraints — beat FF,
and where? This isolates the Stage-4 bet exactly: the symbolic cap is the only channel through
which inference-time value can enter.

7.2    Conditions (teacher-free evaluation throughout)
 1. FF: ε=0 feedforward — the deployed default and the baseline to beat.
 2. TC: teacher-clamped settle — diagnostic upper anchor only (measures headroom available
    to any information injection; never deployable; label it as diagnostic in all outputs).
 3. OT: observed-token settle (CE on the observed prefix, free continuation).
 4. SC: symbolic-constraint settle — grammar rules in MeTTa; L = λ · constraint-violation
    energy queried from the store (batched; in-store path via E5-W4).
 5. OT+SC: combined.

B       Metric definitions gathered
   Fields. εℓ (x): settled error tensors. δℓ (x) = ∂L/∂xℓ : BP adjoints at ε=0, teacher-free CE,
    autograd. Shared artifact schema; ϕ ∈ {ε, δ} below.
   Squared update mass: mi = E[ϕ2i ] over the probe set; per-sample values stored for split-
    half statistics.
   Top-k support Sc : cells in the top k fraction of mi on inputs with signature c (majority
    vote across samples).
   M0: per block, distributions of cos(εℓ , δℓ ) and ∥εℓ ∥/∥δℓ ∥; Jacc(Sℓε , Sℓδ ) at top-5%; depth-
    profile agreement.
   ∆Jacc : within-vs-between context split-half Jaccard contrast (§5).
   Effective rank (PR): ( i λi )2 / i λ2i of per-block settled-state covariance spectra.
