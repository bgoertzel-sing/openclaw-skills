                        What this says about “disentangling messy transformer representations”
                        The transformer’s hidden state may remain superposed and messy. SLT does not require that hidden
                        coordinates become human-clean features. It requires that the residual corrections become locally
                        factored in the evidence geometry. The residual columns are therefore best understood as local charts on
                        the transformer’s failure modes, not as global semantic features.
                        So the right target is not:
                        “Find the true basis of the transformer representation.”
                        It is:
                        “Find a sparse, context-dependent residual factorization whose evidence decomposes, whose interaction
                        remainder is dominated, whose causal fingerprints are separable, and whose update fields approximately
                        commute.”
                        That is a much more operational target, and it directly explains why the current orthogonal basis can look
                        good as an oracle diagnostic while failing as a deployable causal mechanism.
                        Bottom line
                        SLT can help RelaLeap most by changing the promotion criterion. The project should stop asking, “Which
                        sparse residual basis reconstructs the dense teacher best?” and ask instead:
                        Which residual factorization has the best local evidence while keeping cross-column interactions d

                        That means choosing columns by evidence/free-energy, validating them by LLC additivity or interpretable
                        LLC interactions, policing them by mixed-Hessian/commutator audits, and letting a refinement-DAG
                        controller split, merge, mediate, or demote columns when the factorization fails. The result is a principled
                        path from trial-and-error basis search toward a real causal modular residual layer.
