## Initial synthesis hypothesis

These papers appear to form a single design pattern: treat weakness as local Bayesian evidence; use LLC and additivity deviation as measurements of factorization quality; use refinement DAGs / quantale message passing to select, split, merge, and transfer modular factorizations across contexts; and interpret transformer residual layers as a concrete substrate for learned local charts on failures rather than as globally clean semantic bases.
