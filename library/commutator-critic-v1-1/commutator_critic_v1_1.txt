A Grey-Box Counterfactual Update Critic for Modular Continual

    RelaLeap V4 proposal — companion to the V1–V3 negative-results briefing

                                                Abstract
       The co-learned global causal critic program (RelaLeap V1–V3) attempted to learn, from
   decision-time summary features, a randomized paired counterfactual estimate of which module-
   level gradient intervention would improve a retention–plasticity tradeoff. V2 failed every dis-
   crimination and calibration gate; V3’s positive controls lost their declared orderings along the
   optimizer trajectory before confirmation could open. This document argues, on the basis of
   the General Causal-Continual-Learning (CCL) theorem, that both failures were predictable
   consequences of the problem formulation rather than of the causal question: (i) the summary
   features used were not sufficient statistics for the estimand, which to second order in the step
   size is an explicit functional of local gradients, curvature, and the trajectory’s linear-response
   propagator; and (ii) the estimand is intrinsically state-dependent, so any trajectory-invariant
   “declared ordering” must eventually fail, and the V3 audit criterion — not the fixture —
   was the defective component. We then propose the repair: a grey-box commutator critic
   whose backbone is measured at decision time (per-module gradient inner products, module-
   restricted Hessian-vector products, and a tangent-propagated linear-response term computed
   by forward-mode automatic differentiation along the common-random-number baseline roll-
   out), with a small learned head restricted to the residual that the second-order theory does
   not capture. Pair synergy becomes an exact cross-curvature identity, resolving the global-
   versus-additive comparator ambiguity; the positive control becomes a block-quadratic fixture
   whose ground truth is recomputed analytically at every state, so orderings cannot “break,”
   only the expansion’s validity radius can shrink — and that radius becomes a measured gate.
   We give the mathematics, the conceptual reframing, a software design that reuses the V3
   paired-restoration machinery, specializations to Transformer head/channel modules (with
   HDC-footprint confusion-graph sparsification of the pair-action space) and to CAROM in
   its scheduled, fixed-point, and itinerant semantics (tangent propagation, implicit-function-
   theorem sensitivities, and measured discrete commutators respectively), and an appendix
   specifying a staged, gated, agent-executable experimental programme with concrete numer-
   ics chosen to preclude the shortsighted choices — unmeasured noise floors, under-powered
   effect sizes, leaky standardization, single-precision fixtures — that would otherwise corrupt
   the results. A toy-scale sandbox implementation (Section 10; a NumPy block-quadratic
   suite with exact closed-form truth, and a JAX MLP-plus-Adam learner with CRN paired
   rollouts) validates every backbone mechanism where truth is exact — including the synergy
   identity, the provably-aligned null, the constructive insufficient-statistics demonstration, and
   the margin-decay reinterpretation of the V3 audit failure — and yields concrete protocol
   amendments, the sharpest being that differentiating through the Adam moment recursions
   (exact-D) is load-bearing rather than a refinement.

1     Introduction and diagnosis
1.1   What V1–V3 tried
The V-series program targets a genuinely causal object. At learner state St , candidate action
a (attenuate one module’s incoming-task gradient, or a pair’s, before the optimizer step), or-
dinary action a0 , and horizon h, the estimand is the common-random-number (CRN) paired
counterfactual effect
                            τh (St , a) = E[ Yt+h (a) − Yt+h (a0 ) | St ] ,                (1)
with Y a vector of outcomes (retained-task loss LA , incoming-task loss LB , and diagnostics),
measured by executing the learner from an exact snapshot under both arms with identical
randomness. This estimand, the randomized behavior policy, the exhaustive action tables, and
the fail-closed gate discipline are all correct and are retained unchanged in what follows.
    What failed was the estimator. V2 asked a three-branch MLP to map summary features —
gradient norms, cosines, support ratios, activation statistics, Adam second moments — to τh ,
and obtained Spearman correlations below 0.065 in every family. V3 enriched the features and
scaled the data tenfold, but stopped earlier: at the final audit checkpoint, every positive-control
family had lost the intervention ordering declared for it at construction time.

1.2   Two failure theorems, informally
Both outcomes are consequences of structure the CCL theorem makes explicit.




                                                  3
Failure 1: the features are not sufficient statistics. To second order in the step size, τh
is an explicit functional of the local gradients gA , gB , the local Hessians HA , HB (through their
module-restricted blocks), the optimizer preconditioner, and the trajectory’s linear-response
propagator (Section 3). Norms, cosines, and moment summaries quotient out precisely the
curvature-weighted inner products on which the estimand depends: two states can agree on
every V2 feature and have τh of opposite sign (Proposition 1). A critic trained on a non-sufficient
statistic converges, at best, to the conditional mean over the induced equivalence class; near-zero
rank correlation and amount-heads inventing their own output scale are exactly the predicted
symptoms.

Failure 2: the estimand has no trajectory-invariant ordering. τh depends on the state
through gA (θt ) and gB (θt ). As incoming-task optimization converges, the perturbation injected
by a gate shrinks proportionally to ∥gB ∥, and the retained-side alignment term can change sign
as the trajectory crosses level sets of LA (Proposition 2 gives the closed form in the quadratic
case, including the geometric decay rate of the action margin). A “declared family ordering” is
therefore an implicit claim of trajectory invariance that the estimand does not possess. The V3
audit did its job — it detected that the criterion, not the physics, was wrong.

1.3   The repair in one sentence
Do not train a critic to rediscover τh from summary features; measure the first- and second-
order terms that the CCL theorem says constitute τh in the expansion-valid regime, and train a
small model only on the residual — with a positive control whose ground truth is recomputed
analytically per state, so that the only thing that can fail is the expansion’s validity radius,
which is itself turned into a measured, reportable quantity.

The CRN design of V1–V3, built to control variance, turns out to be precisely the design under
which the backbone is computable.

Computation. Equation (9) is one forward-mode tangent pass: initialize tangent v ← ut (a),
and at each baseline step propagate v ← Jk v, which costs one Hessian-vector product (HVP) of
the minibatch loss (a jvp through the gradient computation) plus the elementwise preconditioner
action. For A candidate actions, stack tangents into a d × A matrix and batch the JVPs (vmap
over tangent columns). Total cost: O(A · h) HVPs per decision state, i.e. roughly 2Ah extra
backward-equivalents — for the Phase-0 scales below, tens of milliseconds per state on CPU,
and easily amortized on GPU. Crucially, no learned component participates: the backbone is a
measurement.

3.3   Local commutator form and the CCL connection
When retained-task replay is interleaved with incoming updates (rather than LA merely being
evaluated), the deviation between action arms over a window is governed, per the CCL theorem’s
continuous specialization, by the gradient field commutator
                         [XA , XB ](θ) = HB (θ) gA (θ) − HA (θ) gB (θ),                    (10)
restricted through the module blocks that the gate touches. The propagator form (9) is the
trajectory-exact refinement of the Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff second-order bound in CCL The-
orem 1: where the CCL theorem bounds forgetting by Kij hi hj εcomm uniformly, the backbone
evaluates the corresponding bilinear form on the realized trajectory. Two corollaries worth
recording:

Corollary 1 (The critic estimates the confusion graph). The matrix of backbone terms ⟨gA , DPm gB ⟩   m
and cross-curvature terms (Lemma 2) is a per-state, per-module empirical estimate of the CCL
confusion structure δc,c′ restricted to the module partition. A working commutator critic is there-

(and the CGCCT commutator loss) requires.
Corollary 2 (Policy as commutator suppression). The conservative policy “gate module m when

mechanism for the CCL hypotheses, closing the loop between the theorem (modularity implies
small forgetting) and the mechanism that maintains modularity.

3.5   Preconditioner differentiation and surrogates
Two backbone modes are implemented and compared:

  Frozen-D: treat Dk as a constant within each step’s Jacobian (7). Cheap; exact for SGD;
   an approximation for Adam whose quality is an empirical question settled at probe states.

  Exact-D: differentiate through the moment recursions (m̂, v̂) with functional-programming
   AD (torch.func); ≈ 2× the frozen cost. Used as ground truth for validating frozen-D at
   probe states, and as the default wherever the discrepancy gate fails.

Where full HVPs are too expensive (large Transformers), Gauss–Newton / empirical-Fisher
block surrogates replace HA in (6) and (11), and K-FAC-style Kronecker factors (recalled from
memory, verify) supply per-head cross-blocks; each surrogate is admitted only after passing a
cosine/magnitude agreement gate against true HVPs at probe states (Appendix, Stage C4).


4     Why the black-box critic could not work
Proposition 1 (Insufficient statistics). Let φ(S) be any feature map built from per-module norms
of gA , gB , their cosines, parameter norms, activation moments, and coordinate-wise optimizer-
moment summaries that are invariant under a simultaneous permutation π of coordinates within
a module applied to both gradients and the corresponding moment entries. In the block-quadratic
fixture of Section 6 with non-permutation-invariant within-module spectra, there exist state pairs
S, S ′ with φ(S) = φ(S ′ ) and τhA (S, a) τhA (S ′ , a) < 0 for single-module actions a and horizons
h ≥ 1. Consequently any measurable critic f (φ(S), a) satisfies

                 Var E[τh | φ] ≤ Rφ2 Var(τh ) with Rφ2 < 1 bounded away from 1,
                               


and its achievable rank correlation with τh is bounded accordingly.

    Stage C1 of the appendix instantiates this constructively (a “controlled autopsy”): the V2-
style MLP on V2-style features is retrained on fixture data where the backbone attains Spearman
≥ 0.95, and its ceiling is reported. The point is not to relitigate V2 but to convert the diagnosis
into a regression test: any future feature set must break the permutation invariance or it is
provably capped.
Proposition 2 (No trajectory-invariant ordering; margin decay). In the quadratic fixture with
LB = 21 (θ − b∗ )⊤ QB (θ − b∗ ) trained by SGD, the injected perturbation obeys ∥ut (a)∥ ≤ η(1 −
α) ∥QB ∥ ∥θt − b∗ ∥, and ∥θt − b∗ ∥ contracts geometrically at rate (1 − η λmin (QB )). Hence ev-
ery action’s effect, and every between-action margin, decays geometrically along the trajectory;
and the first-order retained-side term gA (θt+h ), (I − ηQB )h−1 ut (a) changes sign whenever the
trajectory crosses the corresponding level structure of LA . No family-level ordering declared at
construction time can be trajectory-invariant unless the construction pins the trajectory itself.
    The V3 audit failure is this proposition observed in the wild. The corrective is not a cleverer
fixture whose ordering survives — the physics forbids it — but a fixture whose truth is recomputed
at every audited state (Section 6), plus an explicit no-signal regime: when the measured between-
action margin at a state falls below the rollout noise floor, the state is labeled signal-free and
excluded from ordering claims rather than counted as a violation. This also answers the briefing’s
Question 2: re-anchoring, if used at all, should be triggered by the measured counterfactual
action margin — which the backbone supplies for free — never by retained loss or a fixed
schedule.

5.1   Architecture

5.3   Decision rule
The conservative V-series policy shape is retained but re-expressed on vector outcomes: choose
a non-ordinary action only when (i) the state’s action margin exceeds 3σnoise ; (ii) the calibrated
probability that τhA < 0 exceeds the frozen threshold with 4/5 ensemble agreement; (iii) the
predicted incoming harm τhB is below the frozen tolerance (0.02 nat retained from V3); and
(iv) the state lies inside the validity radius or the residual head has passed its out-of-radius
skill gate. Scalarization by Uh is available for reporting continuity with V1–V3 but is not the
training target.

5.4   Revised gate suite
The estimation gates become:

 1. Backbone-validity gate. ρh ≥ 0.8, eh ≤ 0.25 inside the declared operating region, per
    family, on untouched confirmation seeds; states with margin < 3σnoise excluded from order-
    ing metrics and their fraction reported.

 2. Residual-skill gate. Inside the validity radius, adding the residual changes MSE by at
    most +5% (do-no-harm); outside it, the residual improves MSE over backbone-alone by
    ≥ 20% and over the constant and support-only baselines as before. This is strictly harder
    and strictly fairer than V2’s “beat a constant”: the null model is now the theory.

 3. Synergy gate. The measured cross-curvature (11) predicts pair-minus-singles rollout dif-

7     Specialization to Transformers
7.1   Module partition and cost
Following CCL §7 and the CGCCT module inventory, modules are attention heads and feed-
forward channel groups; for GPT-2 small that is 144 heads plus FF blocks, and for the Phase-0
model of Stage C4 it is 8 heads plus 2 FF groups. Per-module gradients are obtained from the
same backward pass via parameter grouping (no extra cost); the first-order backbone term for all
M single-module actions is then M masked inner products — essentially free. The propagator
costs one batched JVP pass per decision state (Section 3.2); cross-curvature terms cost one
HVP per participating module. For large models, empirical-Fisher / GGN block surrogates and
K-FAC factors stand in for HVPs, gated by probe-state agreement.



                                                    10
7.2   Sparsifying the pair-action space with HDC footprints
Pair actions scale quadratically in M ; at 144 heads that is untenable and also unnecessary. The
HDC–CGCCT synthesis supplies the fix: encode each module’s do-footprint as a hypervector
(Eq. 5 of that note), build the module-level confusion graph by approximate-nearest-neighbor
search over footprint codes, and instantiate pair actions (and cross-curvature measurements)
only for edges of the confusion graph. The backbone then feeds measured commutator terms
back as edge weights, so the graph and the critic co-refine — the “sparse confusion graph” of
the approximate categorical CCL theorem, estimated online.

7.3   Retained-gradient estimation and CMCP
On real learners gA is estimated from replay. Duplicate or derived replay packets inflate the
effective precision of that estimate in exactly the way the CMCP calibration study characterized;
the typed innovation/provenance guard (weight-zeroing byte-identical and declared-descendant
packets) is therefore applied to the replay stream feeding the gA estimator, with the total-mass
normalization recommended in that report. This is the one place the CMCP machinery enters
the critic, and it enters as plumbing, not as a hypothesis under test.


8     Specialization to CAROM
CAROM (Content-Addressable Recurrent Operator Machine) separates control from data: opaque
command tokens route computation over a small library of reusable learned operators that read,
transform, gate, and write a slot workspace over recurrent steps, with scheduled, fixed-point
(equilibrium), and itinerant execution semantics, and a PC/EqProp local-learning variant (PC-
CAROM). This separation makes CAROM, if anything, a better host for the commutator critic
than a Transformer, because the module partition is architectural rather than discovered: opera-
tor weight blocks {Wk }, the control system, and per-slot gating parameters are natural modules,
and a context’s support is literally the set of operators its programs invoke.

  Scheduled / itinerant (BPTT-trained) semantics. The update map is an unrolled
   program; the tangent propagator of Section 3.2 applies verbatim, with Jk the Jacobian of
   one recurrent step. Actions attenuate a specific operator’s gradient during adaptation to a
   new context; the backbone predicts retained-context effects per operator. Because programs
   are short (length ∼ 6 in the sandbox series), the propagator is cheap and well-conditioned.

  Fixed-point semantics. At an equilibrium z ∗ with F (z ∗ , θ) = 0, the implicit function
                                                      −1
   theorem gives the sensitivity ∂z ∗ /∂θ = − ∂F/∂z       ∂F/∂θ; the backbone’s linear-response
   term is obtained by solving one linear system per action (conjugate gradients / Neumann
   truncation, as in deep-equilibrium practice; recalled from memory, verify), validated against
   nudged finite differences. These equilibrium intervention Jacobians are, not coincidentally,
   the do-influence estimates that causal coding wants for its gates — the critic and the causal-
   coding machinery share their most expensive measurement.

  Non-gradient components. Where dynamics are genuinely non-gradient — Lotka–Volterra
   mode arbitration, discrete routing — the smooth backbone

9.3     Numerical hygiene (binding on all stages)
 1. Precision. The quadratic fixture and all validity-gate arithmetic run in float64. Trans-

    losses. No tf32, no AMP, anywhere in Phase 0.

 2. Step-size discipline. Enforce η λmax (DH) ≤ 0.5 (power-method estimate, 10 iterations, 3

10.4   Lessons learned
 1. The effect-size precheck caught a live failure. The first synergy plant — a random-
    direction cross-block, as originally specified — landed at 0.1× the measured noise floor: a
    faithful reproduction of V2’s tiny-utility regime, in fresh code, on the first try. The precheck
    flagged it; the repair (a rank-1 cross-block aligned with the early-phase incoming gradient,
    measured on the early trajectory slice) brought the plant to 9–22× the floor. The C1
    numerics are amended accordingly, and the episode is the strongest available argument for
    keeping the precheck mandatory and unfrozen-in-spirit: it is the rule that distinguishes “the
    mechanism is absent” from “the fixture whispered.”

 2. Exact-D is load-bearing under Adam, not a refinement. At learner-typical η = 10−3 ,
    frozen-D degrades gracefully (0.85–0.97 at h ≥ 10); at η = 10−2 it collapses to noise while
    exact-D is essentially unaffected. Differentiating through the moment recursions is where
    most of the horizon-transport fidelity lives. Amendment: exact-D is the default for Adam
    learners in C2/C4; frozen-D is an optimization admitted only by the probe gate, with the
    expectation that it fails at practical learning rates.

 3. A decision-time backbone exists. The deployment-legal variant — a noise-free preview
    rollout standing in for the realized future baseline — matched validation-mode Spearman to
    three decimals in the quadratic. The critic may not need the realized future at all; C2 gains
    a preview-mode comparison arm to test this on real learners, where minibatch curvature
    fluctuation could yet separate them.

 4. Measure, don’t declare — confirmed on a real learner. In B1 the conflict is planted
    in layer 1, yet the realized retention benefit at h = 10 orders head > layer 2 > layer 1:
    Adam distributes the interfering update over all modules, and the causally efficacious gate
    is not the architecturally intended one. Any residual temptation to reintroduce declared
    per-family orderings for real-learner audits should end here.

 5. The failure propositions are now regression tests. Both propositions of Section 4
    exist as constructive, seed-stable code (feature-identical sign-flip states; the margin-decay /


                                                15
      no-signal curve). They move from post-mortem narrative to standing pytest fixtures: any
      proposed feature set or audit criterion must pass through them.

 6. The learnable residue is small at toy scale. With exact injection and exact-D trans-
    port, what remains for a residual head is 0.03–1.9% relative — consistent with the pos-
    sibility, flagged in Section 11, that the programme’s honest landing is the backbone as a
    measurement instrument, with the learned component thin or empty. C3’s do-no-harm gate
    is therefore not a formality; it is the likely binding constraint.


11      What would falsify this proposal
The proposal is fail-closed like its predecessors, and its falsifiers are sharp. If the backbone
fails its own validity gate inside the fixture (Stage C1), the implementation is wrong — stop.
If the backbone validates on the fixture but shows no validity region at all on the real MLP
learner at h ≤ 5 and strong attenuation (Stage C2), then second-order local theory genuinely
does not govern short-horizon intervention effects in trained networks at Phase-0 scale — a
real and publishable negative that would also, notably, constrain the quantitative reach of the
CCL theorem’s smooth specialization. If the backbone validates but the residual head cannot
beat it anywhere outside the radius (Stage C3), the honest conclusion is that the learnable part
of the critic problem is empty at this scale, and the program should ship the backbone as a
measurement instrument (for CGCCT commutator losses and confusion graphs) rather than as
a learned critic — which would itself be a useful landing.

A.6    Stage C5: CAROM track (CPU or ≤ 4 GPU-h)
Purpose. The three CAROM semantics, in increasing order of novelty.
   Numerics. Reuse the CAROM sandbox configuration: operator library K = 8, 16 slots,
program length 6, 4 context families; BPTT-trained scheduled variant as substrate (i); fixed-
point variant as substrate (ii); itinerant/LV arbitration as substrate (iii).
 1. Scheduled: operator-level gating actions (M = K + 1 counting the control system as a
    module); tangent propagator through the unrolled program; gates as in C2 with the same
    margin discipline; 60 decision states × 3 seeds, horizons {1, 5, 10}.
 2. Fixed-point: IFT sensitivities via conjugate gradients (tolerance 10−6 , max 200 iterations,
    warm-started); validation against nudged finite differences (nudge 10−3 , central): relative
    error ≤ 10% at 20 probe equilibria before any effect estimates are reported.
 3. Discrete commutators: 500 paired CRN measurements of d(Ui Uj , Uj Ui ) across the 6 context
    pairs from snapshots along training, d = max over per-context eval-loss differences. Gate:
    Spearman ≥ 0.5 between measured commutator magnitude and realized forgetting when the
    contexts are subsequently trained sequentially (both orders, paired). This is the CAROM-
    native critic claim; if it fails while (1) passes, the discrepancy localizes the non-gradient
    components as the obstruction — report which.

A.7    Stage C6: policy pilot (only if C2 and C4 pass)
The conservative V-series policy, re-expressed on vector outcomes and the validity radius (§5),
evaluated on the C2 test bed first: retention vs ordinary and support-score controls, incoming
loss within 0.02 nat, positive paired bootstrap (104 resamples), superiority to frequency-matched
random, ≥ 50% of oracle improvement (oracle from exhaustive tables), credit-wavefront and
effective-rank diagnostics preserved. New protocol version, fresh seeds, thresholds frozen before
the first confirmation seed is opened — V-series discipline unchanged.

A.8    Frozen choices and allowed knobs
Never change: the estimand (1) and CRN pairing; the gate definitions and thresholds once C1
completes; the validity-radius definition (ρ ≥ 0.8, e ≤ 0.25, margin ≥ 3σ); the effect-size precheck
