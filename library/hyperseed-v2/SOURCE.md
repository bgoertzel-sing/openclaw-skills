## Summary

The Hyperseed v2 article frames Hyperseed as a compact, learnable, observer-relativized semantic-primitive ontology for mind, experience, and reality, meant both as a philosophy and as an engineering scaffold for AI inference control. The article emphasizes approximate reduction of concepts into ontology terms, ontology-guided inference control via expected marginal information gain per effort cost, and ontological separation/thin-waist structure measured through an Ontological Efficiency Ratio.

The main 1454-page PDF presents Hyperseed as a mathematically grounded ontology using paraconsistent p-bit truth values, quantale-style weakness/generalization, enriched categorical structures, and resonance dynamics. The smaller PDFs connect Hyperseed to practical PLN inference-control scaffolding, typed SUMO-in-MeTTa encoding, and scientific experiment representation using SUMO/EXPO plus Hyperseed epistemic annotations.
