                                               Abstract
          Four Lean-formalized working notes (architecture cost boundaries; predictive coding ex-
      actness and cost; OEIS verified synthesis with a four-carrier campaign; GSLT typed-action
      decoding) prove boundary theorems for mechanisms the CAROM experimental program has
      been measuring empirically. This note reconciles the two bodies of work for two audiences. For
      the formal side: a ledger of which bench findings your theorems explain, which they reframe,
      and which of our empirical laws are candidates for your formalization pipeline — including one
      place where our data suggests a hypothesis your affine theory makes precise (learned commu-
      tation as a third reading of the itinerary-tau decline). For the implementation side: a probe
      catalog — delivered as running code (harness v3 probes.py) — with, for each probe, the
      theorem that licenses it, what it measures, how to call it, and how to read the numbers; plus
      the concrete rescoping of PC-CAROM, the reachability discipline for the teach loop, and the
      portfolio reframing of the execution-semantics ladder. Smoke readings on the sandbox check-
      point are included throughout, one of which is already substantive: the learned phase family
      fails schedule-uniform stability under adversarial alternation (norm growth 3.8–4.9× over 16
      phases; joint-spectral-radius proxy p90 = 1.07) while natural trajectories behave — “stability
      does not compose,” observed on our own artifact.

3     The tau three-hypothesis experiment
The audited GPU run’s central anomaly — itinerary tau falling from 0.990 to 0.492 while endpoint
accuracy rose — had two candidate readings: mechanistic drift (the model abandons itinerant
execution for distributed mixing) and metric artifact (early tau rewarded ordered subsets; demon-
strated live by harness v2’s self-test and by the smoke checkpoint: tau 0.997 with coverage 0.74,
exact-order 0.36). The commutation theory supplies a third:

      Learned commutation. Training drives the effective per-phase maps toward pair-
      wise commutation (and vanishing affine obstruction), so execution order stops mat-
      tering. The channel can then smear or reorder at zero accuracy cost: tau falls not
      because the mechanism decays but because its necessity dissolves.

    The three hypotheses are separated by measuring, per checkpoint, four quantities: repaired
itinerary metrics (coverage, exact-order, transition recall), the intervention causal gap (forced-
correct minus shuffled endpoint accuracy), the linearized pairwise interference energy E ∥[Ja , Jb ]v∥2
(commutator energy, via JVP probes — an intervention, as the identifiability results require),
and the exact nonlinear swap discrepancy ∥Φb Φa w − Φa Φb w∥/∥w∥, which is the ground truth
the affine formula [B, A]x + (Ba + b − Ab − a) linearizes. Decision table:

                                       coverage/exact   causal gap   interference/swap
                metric artifact          stays high     stays high          (any)
                mechanistic drift           falls          falls        stays high
                learned commutation         falls          falls     falls in lockstep

    Drift with high interference is the alarming case (order matters and the model gets answers
another way); learned commutation is the benign and theoretically interesting case — and if
observed, the same Gram certifies which command pairs a compiled schedule may treat as a set,
directly reducing what the Exp2 edge compiler must get right. The sweep is implemented as
tau three hypothesis sweep(ckpt paths, ...) and runs read-only on the audited run’s saved
checkpoints.


4     Probe catalog (audience B)
All probes live in harness v3 probes.py, are read-only over checkpoints, and were smoke-verified
on the sandbox artifact (checkpoint at ∼213 updates — early regime; treat the numbers as
demonstrations of the instruments, not results). Raise n/probes on GPU.

4.1   swap discrepancy / commutator energy
License: affinePhases orderIndependent iff, ...interferenceEnergy zero. Measures: exact
and linearized order-sensitivity of command-phase pairs. Implementation notes: phase map is
the differentiable single-phase executor (control clamped, amplitude 1.5, dwell 12 steps); Jacobian–
vector products via torch.func.jvp — never materialize the Jacobian. Smoke: swap median
0.106; interference mean 5.2 × 10−3 . Read: track trends across checkpoints, not absolute values;
the pairwise matrix (extendable from the mean) is the Gram that certifies reorderable schedule
pairs.

4.2   tau three hypothesis sweep
As §3. Output: one row per checkpoint with accuracy, repaired itinerary metrics, causal gap,
interference, swap discrepancy. Plot all columns against training step; apply the decision table.

4.3   schedule stability probe
License: individuallyStable switchingDiverges. Measures: (a) workspace norm growth under
adversarial two-command alternation; (b) a joint-spectral-radius proxy from random products
of phase JVPs. Smoke (substantive): alternation growth median 3.85× (max 4.85×) over
16 phases; JSR proxy median 1.0001, p90 1.074 ⇒ not schedule-uniform stable, though natural
trajectories behave. Read: natural-schedule health certifies nothing about command programs; if
the compiled channel will ever execute model-chosen or adversarial schedules, either constrain the
phase family toward a common Lyapunov certificate (commutingCommonQuadraticLyapunov crown:
commuting stable generators always admit one — commutation again) or bound schedules to the
certified set.

4.4   cross slot coupling probe
License: crossSlotHessian not identifiable — the one quantity that would justify typed slots
is provably invisible in logs; only perturbation reveals it. Measures: normalized coupling matrix
Cij (readout effect on slot i of perturbing slot j’s evidence). Smoke: off-diagonal mass 0.60 —
strongly coupled, correctly reflecting that rev/shr/cms are cross-slot operations; per-slot inde-
pendent treatment is not licensed on this task family. Read: any claimed typed-slot advantage
must ship with this matrix, or the attribution is unsupported.

4.5   decisiveness report
License: equal crossEntropy strictly ordered expectedYield. Measures: routing entropy per
command (vs. log K) and normalized control-state entropy over time. Smoke: routing entropy
1.29 against uniform 2.08. Read: report beside every loss/accuracy; loss provably underdeter-
mines guidance-like properties, and our own tau-vs-accuracy divergence was an instance.


5     Rescoping PC-CAROM (audience B, with A checking the scop-
      ing)
Our EqProp-vs-BPTT parity result stands, scoped : energy-based substrate (the EqProp-native
regime), toy scale (below the measured 1.6–15× settling cost), matched steps. The Oruži PC ver-
dict — dead as a drop-in full-network rule on synchronous hardware; alive for shallow adapters
over exactly frozen backbones, staged BP→PC ignition, rank-uniform skips, and asynchronous
hardware — dictates three changes: (1) PC-CAROM training runs start from BPTT-trained
checkpoints (staged ignition), which our checkpointing supports for free; (2) the target regime is
the cap: thin PC-trained CAROM over a frozen LLM, which is precisely the frozen-backbone-
restriction regime their theorem endorses — with the hard warning that partial freezing or shared
active normalization re-imports the backbone depth bound, so the freeze must be exact; (3) our
convergence-depth law (routing alignment collapses first at insufficient settling) is now calibrated

6    The teach loop under the consolidation theory (audience B)
Map: registering a taught skill = growing a shared action space over frozen components. Their
results give the discipline: (i) reachability first — before tuning the KL no-regression weight, check
whether “preserve old-command routing + install new-skill routing” is jointly reachable in the gate
parameters (exists faithfulConsolidation iff mem range; rank version rankBudgetReachable iff rank le):
if not, no penalty weight fixes it and the gate needs capacity, not tuning. (ii) The KL penalty
is a minimum-change update in the GEM/OGD family their reachability criterion licenses —
implement it as distillation against cached pre-registration routing distributions on a frozen old-
command corpus, not as new-slot suppression. (iii) Rehearsal from the generative teacher is
free, but rehearsed evidence must be discounted : re-counting a reused contribution overstates
by exactly one copy (reused contribution exact excess). (iv) Our paired-M2 decomposition
(routing-leak vs. operator attribution) is the measurement their “frozen weights ̸= frozen be-
havior” statement calls for; keep it mandatory at every registration.


7    Portfolio reframing and the belief-register arm
The OEIS four-carrier campaign (984 discoveries; 669 unique to one carrier; pairwise overlap 8–
16%; fixed split 399 vs. best arm 435; adaptive allocation dominant; the last-place belief arms
producing two of five showcase-proved programs) converts our scheduled/fixed-point/itinerant
ladder from a contest into a portfolio. Implementation changes: report union value and per-arm
unique coverage, not only per-arm accuracy; allocate adaptively; and add a fifth arm — belief-
register CAROM: workspace slots as natural-coordinate evidence registers (η, Λ) or counts,
the write hardwired as addition (their fusion-is-addition square: Kalman = gated interpolation
= PLN revision = addition in natural coordinates), learning concentrated where Bayes is silent
— measurement maps and gain schedules — and, under drift, fading at the derived retention
P/(P +Q) rather than a tuned constant. Expectation set by their data: likely lower raw yield,
likely complementary coverage, and calibrated per-slot confidence for free (the principled version
of our residual-as-confidence idea).


8    What flows back to the formal side (audience A)
Your affine/linear phase theory stops exactly where our program’s distinctive content begins.
Candidates for the Lean pipeline, with fixtures available from our artifacts:

(C1) Heteroclinic execution. GLV channels, saddle-value conditions ν > 1, dwell-time law
     ln(1/η)/λ, fatigue as anti-trap, entry-by-initialization — none covered by the affine results.
     Our tuning package is a conjecture set with measured constants.

(C2) The routing-bottleneck bound, sharpened. Your collapse theorem gives the update
     class; the quantitative version — realizable distinct behaviors under sharp routing = K
     vertices, with a defect bound for soft mixtures — would turn our design rule into a theorem
     with a constant.

(C3) Routing interference under action-space growth. A softmax-renormalization analog
     of your consolidation double-counting result: exact leak formula for old-command probabil-
     ity mass as a function of new-anchor geometry, with the no-regression KL as the licensed
     projection.

(C4) The convergence-depth law. EqProp update alignment as a function of settling residual,
     with routing parameters provably the most sensitive group (their gradient lives in differences
     of per-operator potentials — plausibly formalizable in your scalar-chain style).

(C5) Nonlinear order-independence. Our swap discrepancy vs. linearized-Gram gap (smoke:
     median exact discrepancy 0.106 against small linearized interference) suggests the affine ob-
     struction term dominates early; a second-order expansion of the swap formula would make
     the probe’s bias analyzable.

(C6) Itinerant containment. Clamping recovers scheduled CAROM; symmetrizing ρ recovers
     the DEQ — stated in our notes as a proposition, prov able in your framework at the linear-
     phase level.
