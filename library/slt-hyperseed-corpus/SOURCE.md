## Key cross-cutting claims

- Weakness can be identified with local Bayesian evidence; LLC is then the asymptotic exponent of weakness/free-energy volume.
- Modular/factorized learning is credible only under dominated interaction; mixed-Hessian leakage, commutators, and additivity gaps are practical warning signals.
- SLT diagnostics are structural, not semantic by themselves; the semantics-to-geometry bridge requires distinctions -> partitions -> symmetries -> singularity types.
- Regime-change and transfer decisions can use module-wise LLC/multiplicity signatures; large mismatch is evidence against near-isomorphic TransWeave-style transfer within a fixed model class.
- SubRep/goal-chaining style systems need interaction-aware LLC decomposition rather than naive additivity when planner/option/shared-representation couplings are load-bearing.
- For transformer residual learning, SLT suggests scoring residual factorizations by evidence geometry, interaction remainder, commutators, support regret, and causal fingerprints, not reconstruction alone.

- These are working papers / generated research guidance documents, not validated experiments.

- SLT structural stability is not a complete value-alignment or semantic-correctness guarantee.
