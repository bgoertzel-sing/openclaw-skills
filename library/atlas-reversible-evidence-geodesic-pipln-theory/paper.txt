                                              Abstract
      Geodesic inference control treats reasoning as a boundary-value problem: among paths
  from premise distributions to goal distributions, prefer a least-effort corridor guided by the
  product of a forward reachability factor and a backward goal-usefulness factor. Paraconsistent
  Geodesic Modal Homotopy Type Theory (PGM–HoTT) treats identity proofs as graded paths
  with separate positive and negative evidence channels. The information-geometric theory of
  energy-based continual learning decomposes forgetting into metric anisotropy and connection
  curvature, and identifies finite order-dependent forgetting with holonomy around a task loop.
  This paper integrates these ideas into an atlas-indexed, reversible, directed, evidence-fibered
  formulation of πPLN.
      The persistent cognitive state is not one posterior over one ambient sample space. It is a
  context-indexed, provenance-rich, paraconsistent evidence metagraph. Ordinary Bayesian or
  νPLN calculations occur inside temporary local charts, whose assumptions, priors, factorization
  choices, and concept boundaries are explicit. Charts export empirical positive and negative
  evidence back to the persistent layer; gluing charts into a larger probabilistic model is a theorem
  to be earned, not a global axiom.
      Mathematically, the architecture consists of an atlas-indexed family of directed higher proof
  spaces and evidence fibrations. Their Grothendieck construction contains both within-context
  inference and guarded context translation. Local Bayesian charts are modeled as reversible
  evidence/probability lenses: in the beta–Bernoulli case, subtracting the chart prior on export
  gives an exact round trip and proves that repeated chart construction cannot manufacture
  evidence. Cross-context de-duplication is permitted only after translation to a covering context.
  Probabilistic gluing is expressed as a homotopy-descent type; geodesic contractibility licenses an
  essentially unique global chart, while emptiness records genuine non-gluability. A quantitative
  descent obstruction is kept separate from curvature.
      The paper proves an atlas-level local-to-global coherence theorem whose error is the sum of
  defects of proof-rewrite cells, inference–translation naturality cells, context-composition cells,
  chart round trips, and revision cells. In the smooth reversible limit, proof curvature, context
  curvature, and mixed curvature are the bidegree components of one total evidence connection,
  and endpoint order dependence is exactly its holonomy. The full diagnostic therefore has three
  conceptually distinct parts: control-path multiplicity versus unique evidence, reversible holonomy,
  and probabilistic descent obstruction. Revision becomes a first-class higher morphism, with
  residuated fit, preservation, weakness, provenance correction, and a reversible complement.
      We derive a hybrid geodesic controller that runs Schrodinger bridges inside local Bayesian
  islands and uses quantale-valued costs for context changes, guarded translations, and revisions.
  We give curvature-, novelty-, and descent-aware BGC scores; covering-context splice tests;
  Dykstra-style chart and evidence residuals; and concrete MORK records for contexts, charts,

1    Introduction
A large uncertain-reasoning system must solve at least four problems at once.
   First, it must decide which inference to perform. Geodesic inference control addresses this
as a least-effort boundary-value problem. A good intermediate state is both reachable from the
premises and useful toward the goal; in Schrodinger-bridge language these demands are represented
by forward and backward factors f and g, with bridge mass proportional to f g [1, 9].
   Second, it must decide when two derivations carry the same evidence. A raw proof graph counts
syntactically distinct paths. But paraphrases, role permutations, rule reorderings, and repeated
projections may all transport the same primitive observations. PGM–HoTT supplies a language
for identities between proofs and for graded positive and negative identity evidence [2, 10]. An
idempotent provenance fiber then turns those higher identities into an exact de-duplication rule.
   Third, it must control order dependence. The coupling-dilemma theory separates a metric term,
caused by weighting information modes incorrectly, from a curvature term, caused by noncommuting
transport between task or solution manifolds. At finite scale the latter is holonomy [4]. Causal
coding adds the structural observation that good causal modularity makes most update pairs
commute or nearly commute, so global path dependence is controlled by a sparse graph of genuinely
confusing pairs [3].
   Fourth, it must reason without pretending that every cognitive context is a marginal of one
master probability space. The πPLN formulation treats persistent knowledge as context-indexed
positive and negative empirical evidence. Local Bayesian charts are created when a calculation needs
ordinary probabilistic coherence, and their outputs are projected back to empirical evidence. Priors
belong to charts, not to the durable evidence store. Some chart families glue; others provably do not.
Revision therefore acts on evidence packets, provenance, translations, valuations, and ontologies,
not only on one global posterior [5].
   These requirements lead naturally to an atlas-indexed architecture. Instead of postulating one
ambient proof base, associate each context with a directed higher proof space and an evidence
fibration, and combine them into a total context–proof structure:

                             one proof base with one evidence fibration
                                                  ⇓                                               (1.1)
                   a context-indexed atlas of proof bases and evidence fibrations

Locally, the de-duplication, proof-surface, reversible-dilation, and holonomy results apply inside each
context. Globally, two additional questions arise. Does inference commute with context translation?
And do the local probability charts admit any coherent global gluing?
   The central synthesis is:

                      control mass lives on raw context–proof paths,
                     evidence mass lives on context-guarded provenance components,
                   double counting is a mismatch between these two masses,                        (1.2)
              contextual forgetting is holonomy around proof-and-context loops,
                     non-gluability is absence of a coherent global chart section.

Holonomy and non-gluability must not be conflated. Curvature measures path-dependent transport
when transports exist. Descent obstruction says that no global probabilistic object realizes all local
charts, even when pairwise overlaps look compatible.

   A reversible implementation makes the higher geometry literal. A PLN implication need not be
logically reversible, but a particular rule execution can be computationally reversible if it carries the
information the visible step would erase: overwritten state, provenance overlap, chart assumptions,
prior parameters, Bregman/Dykstra residuals, context labels, translation complements, and random
seeds. The visible directed system is then a forgetting projection of an invertible microstate
dynamics.

The scalar w is not sufficient for sound evidence accounting. Two capsules can each have weight

The control interpretation is immediate. The forward factor measures compatibility with the past
boundary; the backward factor measures compatibility with the future boundary. A practical
bidirectional geodesic chaining (BGC) heuristic scores a candidate rule step r by

                                Score0 (r) = ∆(log f + log g) − λ ∆Cr ,                           (2.6)

where Cr is local inference effort. Exact f and g may be replaced by factor-graph messages, Monte
Carlo meet probabilities, or learned value functions [1].
   The crucial point for this paper is that ρ is a control distribution over paths or partial proof
states. It is not automatically a measure of independent evidence. Twenty cheap derivations can
deserve more search attention than one expensive derivation while still carrying only one primitive
observation.

2.3     A precision concerning Noether conservation
Time-translation invariance of an action such as (2.3) yields a Hamiltonian or effort-like conserved
quantity under the usual regularity hypotheses. It does not, by itself, imply that a primitive evidence
token cannot be copied down two branches and later counted twice.
   We therefore distinguish:

    (i) control-energy conservation, arising from symmetry of the path action;

 (ii) evidence continuity, saying that pure inference does not mint new primitive observations;

 (iii) idempotent fusion, saying that repeated references to one token contribute once;

 (iv) flat or curvature-controlled transport, governing order dependence.

The first is variational. The second and third are algebraic/provenance constraints. The fourth is
geometric. Conflating them obscures which intervention is needed when inference goes wrong.


3     Background II: PGM–HoTT, SENF, and TransWeave
PGM–HoTT extends homotopy type theory in three directions [2]. First, identity has separate
positive and negative evidence channels. Second, paths have grades in a logic quantale, making
shortest or Pareto-minimal proofs first-class. Third, modalities can forget grades or enforce bounded
consistency.

3.1     Split quantale and graded identity
Let Q+ and Q− be commutative unital quantales. Define

3.2   Why a directed extension is needed
An identity proof in HoTT is invertible. A PLN deduction is generally not. From premises A and
A ⇒ B, deriving B does not produce a deductive inverse from B to the premises. Abduction is a
different, uncertain operation.
    Consequently, inference rules should not simply be identified with ordinary HoTT paths. The
correct separation is:

                 semantic identity is invertible, while visible inference is directed.           (3.3)

We will model visible derivations as directed 1-cells in a higher category, while retaining ordinary
PGM–HoTT identity in the maximal invertible core. Later, a reversible microstate dilation will
show how directed visible rules can nevertheless arise as projections of invertible hidden-state paths.

16.1    Prior-cycling and chart-roundtrip tests
Repeatedly enter and leave the same local beta or factor-graph chart without adding observations.
Correct prior-unwound export predicts invariant persistent evidence. A system that persists
posterior coordinates will show linear evidence inflation with the number of chart cycles. This
isolates chart-induced double counting from proof-path duplication.

16.2    Holonomy versus non-gluability
Construct two benchmark families. In the first, use one global chart with noncommuting nonlinear
rule transports: descent obstruction is zero but holonomy is nonzero. In the second, use the
anti-correlated pairwise charts with flat overlap transport: local holonomy is zero while descent
obstruction is positive. A diagnostic that reports only one scalar should fail to distinguish them;
the three-channel diagnostic (12.37) should not.

16.3    Translation naturality predicts ontology errors
For pairs of contexts with known boundary shifts, estimate δτ,r by executing infer–then–translate and
translate–then–infer. The prediction is that large mixed defects identify unsafe context translations
and correlate with ontology-smear errors better than within-context commutators alone.

16.4    Reversible corrective revision
Apply quarantine, reliability discount, retraction, and relabeling to provenance packets. Verify exact
replay from the reversible complement. Compare this with adding counterevidence: the visible
aggregate may change similarly, but only the reversible packet-level representation preserves the
distinction between “new evidence against the claim” and “old support was invalidly counted.”
    The synthesis suggests experiments that separate multiplicity, curvature, and hidden residual
effects.

16.5    Path multiplicity versus unique evidence
Construct benchmark proof graphs with many paraphrastic or permuted derivations of the same
primitive evidence and a smaller number of genuinely independent observations. Compare:

 (a) raw path-count confidence;

 (b) sketch-corrected confidence;

 (c) exact token-union confidence;

 (d) control mass allocated by the f g scheduler.

The prediction is that raw path count inflates confidence, while exact evidence mass remains invariant
under addition of evidence-equivalent paths. Control mass may still increase, validating the need
for two measures.

16.10    Storage-efficiency frontier
The ancilla bound (10.4) predicts a tradeoff between reversible auditability and storage. For families
of increasingly many-to-one truth functions, measure retained bits per step and compare with
empirical conditional entropy H(X | U (X)). Compression should approach but not beat the
information-theoretic lower bound without sacrificing exact reversibility.


17      Limitations and open problems
17.1    Choosing the context site and covers
The atlas formalism presupposes a category or restriction structure of contexts and a notion of
covering family. In practice these are learned and task-relative. Bad cover choices can hide a valid
gluing or manufacture an apparent one. The theory makes the choice explicit but does not solve
context discovery by itself.

17.2    Model-class dependence of descent obstruction
A positive Odesc is relative to a proposed class of global charts and divergences. Enlarging the model
class, adding latent variables, or refining the ontology may turn a non-gluable family into a gluable
one. The obstruction is therefore a diagnostic of the current representational layer, not an absolute
metaphysical claim.

17.3    Partial translations and untranslated residue
Real cross-context maps are partial and may leave semantically important residue. Treating a
partial translation as total can make naturality defects look artificially small because untranslatable
content has been dropped. Implementations should retain an explicit residue object and charge its
loss in the coherence grade.

17.4    Priors, hyperpriors, and chart identity
The beta round-trip theorem assumes the same recorded prior is subtracted on export. If chart
priors are revised, learned, or hierarchically coupled, the chart identity and reversible complement
must include the prior version and its provenance. Otherwise changing a prior can be mistaken for
empirical evidence.

17.5    Formal type theory
The paper supplies a semantic core, not a fully mechanized directed dependent type theory. A
complete treatment should define substitution, universes, directed dependent elimination, higher
inductive types, interaction with PGM–HoTT modalities, and a normalization or canonicity theorem
where appropriate.

17.6    Causal identity of evidence tokens
Exact token identity is not always the same as causal independence. Two sensor records may have
different CIDs but be deterministic descendants of one latent event. The ideal evidence algebra
should therefore quotient primitive records by a justified causal-equivalence relation or represent a
richer causal provenance hypergraph. Learning this relation without erasing genuine independence
is a central open problem.

17.7    Smooth reduction validity
The curvature decomposition (9.1) is conditional. Many symbolic PLN rules are piecewise, discrete,
or nonprojective. The discrete proof-surface theorem remains valid, but Fisher–Rao curvature
may not be the correct smooth invariant. One needs empirical and theoretical criteria for when a
differentiable probabilistic-circuit or energy-based realization faithfully approximates the symbolic
engine.

17.8    Approximate sketches
Idempotence at the object/CID level does not imply unbiased evidence cardinality under all sketches.
False overlap and missed overlap can respectively undercount and overcount evidence. The metric
and Lipschitz constants in the approximate de-duplication theorem should be worked out for each
sketch family and correlation regime.

17.9    Nonabelian proof holonomy
In rank greater than two, no single curvature scalar determines forgetting for all evidence directions.
The complete invariant is the holonomy conjugacy class or its angle spectrum. Efficiently estimating

the leading angles from local rule probes without constructing a huge transport matrix is an open
algorithmic problem.

17.10    Privacy and reversibility
Exact reversible logs can retain sensitive information that an ordinary lossy inference would discard.
Privacy-preserving reversible computation, encrypted provenance witnesses, retention policies, and
deliberate irreversible erasure require explicit design. Landauer’s principle reminds us that erasure
is a physical and governance choice, not merely a software omission [19].


18      Conclusion
Geodesic inference control, PGM–HoTT, πPLN, causal coding, and the curvature theory of forgetting
fit together once five different structures are given different mathematical homes.
    Search probability lives on raw context–proof paths. Semantic and evidential identities live
on higher cells. Persistent positive and negative evidence lives in an idempotent, provenance-rich
section indexed by context. Ordinary probability lives inside temporary local charts. Revision and
context translation are explicit moves rather than invisible changes of interpretation.
    This organization exposes three failure modes that a single posterior or scalar confidence cannot
distinguish:

          multiplicity error = wrong effective use of unique evidence,
                  holonomy = path-dependent transport around proof/context loops,              (18.1)
        descent obstruction = failure of local probability charts to glue globally.

Metric changes and scheduler reweighting address the first. Causal modularity, coherent translations,
and connection flattening address the second. Context refinement, ontology revision, latent-variable
enrichment, or an explicit report of non-gluability addresses the third. Treating all three as
“uncertainty” applies the wrong intervention.
   The reversible microstate formulation makes the geometry executable. Rule applications, chart
construction, context translations, and corrective revisions become equivalences when they carry
the complements they would otherwise erase. Directed πPLN is the visible projection obtained
by forgetting those inverse witnesses. Forgetting is then not necessarily destruction: it can be
hidden residual transport or total holonomy. Double counting is not an unavoidable side effect of
multiple proofs or charts: it is non-idempotent fusion after provenance, prior identity, or higher
proof equivalence has been discarded.
   The global probabilistic lesson is equally sharp. Local Bayesian coherence is valuable and fully
retained. But a family of coherent local charts need not define one coherent world model. PGM–
HoTT descent gives a principled criterion: use a global chart when the gluing type is geodesically
contractible, retain guarded alternatives when it is multiply inhabited, and preserve the obstruction
when it is empty.
   The resulting architectural principle is:

         Geodesic πPLN should search an atlas-indexed directed higher proof space,
                     run ordinary Bayesian inference inside local charts,                      (18.2)
            and transport a reversible, idempotent evidence section across them.

auditable, and separately measurable.

B      A compact axiom package for implementation
An implementation need not expose the full higher-categorical semantics. The exact discrete
guarantees require the following package.

A1. Every evidence capsule has immutable primitive provenance or a sketch with a quantified
    metric error.

A2. Merge is associative, commutative, and idempotent on provenance identity.

A3. Pure inference does not mint new primitive evidence tokens.

A4. Rule transports preserve merge and are nonexpansive in the chosen evidence metric.

                                                48
Atlas-Indexed Reversible Geodesic πPLN                        Goertzel with GPT-5.6 Pro assistance


 A5. Proof rewrites carry local evidence-coherence defects.

 A6. Candidate rule pairs are connected in a sparse confusion graph when they share
     causal/evidential support.

 A7. Reversible mode retains the data needed to reconstruct every visible rule input, including dual
     residuals for projection-like steps.

 A8. Control mass and evidence mass are stored and reported separately.

 A9. Every persistent evidence packet is indexed by a context and carries its assumption and
     ontology fingerprints.

A10. Local chart export removes chart priors and virtual counts before persistent storage.

A11. Cross-context fusion requires a covering context and explicit guarded translations.

A12. Chart, translation, and revision maps carry local coherence defects and reversible complements.

A13. The system records whether a selected chart family is uniquely gluable, multiply gluable, or
     non-gluable in the current model class.

A14. Revision is represented at packet level so quarantine/retraction/relabeling remain distinct
     from counterevidence.

   Under A1–A4, exact or approximate de-duplication holds. Under A4–A6, the single-context
proof-surface bound holds. A7 makes rule-loop holonomy executable, and A8 prevents a scheduler
from silently turning proof multiplicity into confidence. A9–A14 provide the atlas-level typing,
prior-unwinding, translation, coherence, descent, and revision conditions required by the πPLN
architecture.
