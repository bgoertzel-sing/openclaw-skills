## Summary

Companion mathematical manuscript to the patham9 software design specification. It formulates πPLN as an atlas-indexed family of directed higher proof spaces with idempotent evidence fibrations and temporary local Bayesian charts. It separates raw-path control mass from unique evidence mass, path-dependent holonomy from probabilistic descent obstruction, and logical inference from reversible execution microstates. It supplies exact discrete results, exact beta-chart special cases, conditional smooth curvature results, implementation proposals, MORK records, worked examples, and an empirical evaluation program.

## Key claims or contents

- Persistent cognition is a context-indexed evidence metagraph rather than one global posterior (§§1, 4).
- Evidence de-duplication is exact when transport respects idempotent primitive-token union (§§6–7).
- Control mass on proof paths and evidence mass on provenance components are distinct (§8).
- Reversible hidden-state dilation can represent directed visible rules while retaining erased information as ancilla (§§10–11).
- Context translation, proof rewriting, chart round trips, revision, holonomy, and descent are assembled into one atlas-level coherence account (§12).
- Holonomy and chart non-gluability are independent diagnostics; neither should substitute for the other (§12.11–12.12).
- Hybrid geodesic control combines local probabilistic bridges with quantale-valued context/revision moves (§13).
- The manuscript gives concrete MORK realization sketches and evaluation predictions (§§14–16).

## Methods or implementation details

Exact packet algebra uses finite positive/negative provenance sets with idempotent union. Local beta charts add explicit prior virtual counts and export by subtracting the same prior. Proof-order defects are measured by local rewrite cells and accumulated through proof surfaces. Computational reversibility stores complements, assumptions, residuals, provenance overlap, context labels, and random seeds. Proposed control scores incorporate forward/backward reachability, effort, curvature, novelty, revision, and descent terms.

## Limitations and uncertainties

The manuscript explicitly distinguishes theorem status: several smooth curvature transfers are conditional; homotopy descent is a semantic theorem schema; hybrid BGC/MORK realization is a design proposal. Context-site selection, causal identity of evidence tokens, partial translations, prior/hyperprior identity, approximate sketches, privacy, and full formal type theory remain open (§17).
